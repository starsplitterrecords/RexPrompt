Operational terminology

Available: assembled original recipe. Unknown: no proof. Unavailable: source failed to load. Not indexed: no approved image mapping (not proof the image never existed). Missing: required curated unit unavailable or known indexed binary missing. Generated: specifically evidenced production only. Approved: image indexed in approved-draft manifest. Mastered: requires explicit pixel-quality acceptance, never inferred. Exported: collected PDF verified. Published: StarSplitterVisions metadata or explicit canon linkage. A released issue cannot be assumed identical to unreleased working recipes.

Source-quality findings are explicitly evidence-linked; a blank finding is unknown, not a pass. Consult the quality ledger and unresolved recovery ledger.
