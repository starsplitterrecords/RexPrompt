# Rex Fleet Issues 1–2 — issue-wide contact-sheet visual review

**Status: preliminary sequence/palette review only.** The human-visible evidence for this note is the issue-wide 4-column contact sheets and luminance/saturation diagnostics produced by GitHub Actions in PR #322. Individual lettering has **not** been checked against the exact assembled recipes at native resolution and no color-managed print proof has been signed. Source deficiencies are separately recorded in the two issue-specific corrective registers.

## Issue 1, full reading order

- **Cover → editorial opener → cast:** designed as different functions with blue/gold space-opera cover branding, orbital editorial artwork, and a dark boxed cast montage. The cast's darker luminance and higher saturation are not enough to establish a defect; check portrait colors and small face labels at native print size.
- **P01:** relatively bright golden terrestrial setting, a purposeful change from the dark space/editorial opening. Its unusually tall source geometry also needs trim and text-safe assessment.
- **P02–P06:** blue/black ship and space operations alternating with darker human interiors. Preserve the deliberate cold-space palette while checking the transition into warmer industrial interiors.
- **P07–P12:** coherent amber industrial/community sequence; do not normalize these interiors to the cooler space pages. Several source canvas shapes (especially P10 and P12) require crop/bleed review.
- **P13–P15:** red faction accents and rally/backdrop lighting sit within the warm-industrial world; red emphasis appears narratively purposeful. P14 needs separate geometry inspection.
- **P16–P17:** conspicuous electric blue tactical-map/command display lighting. A plausible diegetic palette shift rather than a reason for global desaturation. Confirm character skin and uniform tonality against canonical reference pixels, especially at transitions from P15 and into P18.
- **P18:** returns to the warm human-scale industrial setting, providing a deliberate contrast with command displays. Pixel-count requirement met; exact lettering and continuity remain unreviewed.
- **Closing editorial:** much brighter, white editorial typography and design compared with illustrated story pages. This is a format distinction, not evidence it should be darkened or saturated to match preceding pages. Verify small type and black text contrast at final size.

**Flagged for deeper comparison:** cover/portrait identity; P01 and P10/P12/P14 bleed geometry; P16–P18 palette/story transition; closing editorial small print and margins. None is certified a color defect by the contact sheet alone.

## Issue 2, full reading order

- **Cover → editorial opener → cast:** the cover uses orange fire and dark spacecraft, the opener dense orbital/industrial imagery, and the cast uses dark identity cards. Intentional-looking differences by function; avoid matching all three to one histogram.
- **P01–P02:** warm inhabited settings and evacuation pressure, with muted browns and practical ship lights.
- **P03–P07:** shifts into blue Fleet command/bridge imagery, red faction uniforms and cold exterior craft. Story-scene-based palette changes are coherent at contact-sheet scale.
- **P08–P09:** warmer mixed-personnel interactions and busy interiors; do not impose a cool-only issue grade.
- **P10–P12:** space-craft and blue combat lighting. P09–P11 have native pixel-count adequacy under the working profile, but lettering/bleed/continuity still require review.
- **P13–P17:** cold battles intercut with warm decision rooms and brighter daylight exterior/interiors. Changes appear tied to setting and lighting; check whether any one page departs from the actual established scene state.
- **P18–P19:** warm populated and civilian scenes, deliberately human-scale after more technical battle pages. P18 has shape and native-resolution concerns independently of grading.
- **End-matter page:** brighter white editorial layout with large brand header. This should not be flattened into a dark cinematic palette; verify fine type, field clarity, ICC interpretation and press geometry.
- **Closing teaser:** warm street/architecture imagery; color choice appears related to the new location and editorial teaser function. Its low-resolution source blocks print mastering.

**Flagged for deeper comparison:** cast dark portrait legibility; P03/09/10/11 at true-native detail/lettering; P13–P17 transitions; editorial end-matter high-luminance intentional treatment; P18 trim geometry; closing teaser editorial safe area and typography.

## Production conclusions

1. Issue-relative brightness/saturation outliers are **candidate review targets**, not automatically erroneous pages.
2. No automatic global histogram leveling, white-balance matching, gamut crushing or genre-wide neutral-beige conversion should be applied.
3. The genuine priority defect is insufficient source resolution in **20/22 Issue 1 units** and **19/24 Issue 2 units** for the working 300-DPI full-bleed print profile.
4. Embedded source ICC is missing from **20 sources in each issue**, requiring verified color-source handling. ICC absence does not, by itself, mean the current displayed colors are artistically wrong.
5. Contact sheets provide useful reading-order and palette context, but **balloon text, punctuation, speaker ownership, physical bleed and character face accuracy cannot be certified from these reduced proofs**.
6. All approved source art stays untouched until a reviewed higher-resolution or otherwise corrected replacement is accepted. Re-run the issue-wide comparison once approved replacements are in the manifests.

No page receives a mastered flag from this note.
