ACT THREE

EXT. EXTERIOR SHIELD EMITTER — DAY

The storm hits Rae sideways the moment the outer door opens.

Jace is behind her in a sealed work suit.

Ahead of them, an old release wheel stands on an exposed deck above the clouds.

The refugee transport is descending beyond it.

JACE
We can still go back inside.

RAE
Then the ship dies.

JACE
I know.

Rae looks at him.

JACE
I was checking whether you remembered there were two people out here.

Rae reaches the wheel.

She tries it.

Nothing.

She plants both hands and pulls again.

Jace joins her.

RAE
I can do it.

JACE
You aren't.

RAE
I need room.

JACE
You need help.

The transport disappears into cloud.

Rae strains against the seized wheel.

Nothing.

Mero's voice returns in her memory.

MERO (V.O.)
You cannot save everyone.

Rae closes her eyes.

JACE
Rae.

She opens them.

Jace is right beside her.

Present.

Breathing hard.

Waiting.

RAE
Fine.

JACE
That sounded painful.

RAE
Don't ruin it.

They reset their grip.

RAE
On three.

JACE
No numbers.

Rae looks at him.

JACE
Seems contagious.

Despite everything, Rae laughs.

Then they pull together.

The wheel moves.

A deep sound travels through Vael-Tor.

The old defense barrier begins to part across the storm.

Not cleanly.

A gap opens.

Enough.

The refugee transport appears through the cloud, aimed directly at it.

Rae keeps both hands on the wheel.

RAE
Come on.

JACE
The ship can't hear you.

RAE
I'm not talking to the ship.

The barrier opens wider.

The transport passes through.

Barely.

A shockwave throws Rae and Jace to the deck.

The old wheel locks in its open position.

Rae lies in the rain staring up at the ship.

Jace reaches for her.

This time she takes his hand immediately.

INT. LAUNCH BAY SEVEN — LATER

Theo enters the launch bay and finds Adrian waiting.

The bay floor trembles beneath them.

Theo's satchel moves against his side.

Not shaking.

Pulling his attention downward.

Theo takes out the shard.

ADRIAN
Put it away.

THEO
No.

ADRIAN
Theo.

THEO
Something is under us.

Adrian says nothing.

THEO
You know that.

ADRIAN
Put it away.

THEO
Stop saying that like cloth changes what is happening.

Another tremor.

Dust falls from the ceiling.

Theo looks through the launch-bay opening toward the Vael-Tor flats.

A line appears in the ground.

Not a crack.

Too straight.

Too large.

THEO
What is buried here?

Adrian closes his eyes.

THEO
Adrian.

ADRIAN
Vael-Tor wasn't empty.

Theo turns.

THEO
What?

ADRIAN
When the cities came back, people built where they could.

THEO
Came back from what?

Adrian looks at the shard.

ADRIAN
From not remembering why they had left.

The ground outside opens.

A wall of black metal rises through sand and shattered platform foundations.

Theo cannot understand the scale at first.

Then the wall keeps rising.

And curving.

An armored prow.

A ship.

Buried beneath Vael-Tor so completely that a civilization built over it without knowing it was there.

Theo steps toward the open bay.

ADRIAN
Don't.

THEO
You knew.

ADRIAN
I knew there were graves.

THEO
That is not a grave.

The dreadnought rises higher.

Whole neighborhoods vanish behind its shadow.

Its surface is dark and scarred.

No lights.

No engines.

It does not need to look alive to be impossible.

Theo's hand finds the absent stylus.

A memory strikes him.

A launch deck.

This launch deck.

The same ship above him, intact, surrounded by smaller vessels.

His own voice saying something about its weapons.

Theo pulls his hand away from his fingers.

THEO
I've been here.

ADRIAN
No.

Theo looks at him.

ADRIAN
Someone was.

Outside, the buried ship breaks free of the last of the earth.

EXT. VAEL-TOR PLATFORMS — CONTINUOUS

The refugee transport hits the landing deck hard.

Metal screams.

One landing strut folds.

The ship tilts and stops.

For several seconds, nothing happens.

Rae and Jace stare from beneath the overhang.

JACE
Come on.

The transport door opens.

A woman comes out carrying a sleeping child.

Then an old man.

Then two teenagers supporting someone between them.

Then more.

Rae watches.

Her eyes move automatically toward the damaged hull.

Jace touches her shoulder.

JACE
No.

RAE
What?

JACE
Look at them.

Rae does.

People pour out into the rain.

Alive.

No casualty board.

No projection.

Faces.

One woman reaches the platform and immediately turns around to help the next person down.

Rae's breathing changes.

JACE
You did that.

RAE
We did.

JACE
I'm going to allow the correction.

Rae looks at him.

He is bleeding through one sleeve.

RAE
You're hurt.

JACE
So are you.

Rae looks down at herself.

A torn glove. Burned cheek. Blood at one knee.

She almost dismisses it.

Then doesn't.

RAE
We should get inside.

Jace smiles.

JACE
Now you sound reasonable.

A shadow passes over them.

Rae looks up.

The dreadnought is rising beyond the platforms.

Every survivor stops to look.

The same impossible ship whose shadow crossed the promenade is now physically there.

Rae whispers a word she does not remember learning.

RAE
No.

Jace hears it.

JACE
You know that ship?

Rae cannot answer.

INT. LAUNCH BAY OBSERVATION — CONTINUOUS

The dreadnought dominates the horizon.

Theo stands at the glass.

Adrian is behind him.

Neither speaks for a long time.

THEO
The starboard side is wrong.

Adrian closes his eyes.

THEO
It always pulled when the forward batteries were loaded.

ADRIAN
Stop.

THEO
I know where the command deck is.

ADRIAN
Stop telling me what you remember.

Theo turns from the glass.

THEO
Then tell me what you do.

Adrian looks at him.

The denial is gone.

Not softened.

Gone.

THEO
What is that?

ADRIAN
A warship.

THEO
Whose?

Adrian looks back at the dreadnought.

ADRIAN
Ours.

Theo takes that in.

THEO
You said the history was removed.

ADRIAN
It was.

THEO
Not the ship.

ADRIAN
No.

THEO
How do you erase a war and leave that under a city?

Adrian's eyes stay on the horizon.

ADRIAN
You erase the names.

A beat.

ADRIAN
You erase the reasons.

Another.

ADRIAN
You erase the people who remember what the thing was for.

Theo looks at the dreadnought.

THEO
And you thought that was enough.

ADRIAN
We needed it to be.

THEO
Was it your idea?

Adrian does not answer.

Theo understands that the silence is not ignorance.

THEO
Adrian.

ADRIAN
We thought if no one remembered the war, the war could not return.

THEO
But the ship stayed.

ADRIAN
Yes.

Theo looks again at the impossible mass filling the sky.

THEO
Is it dead?

Adrian takes a long time.

ADRIAN
It was.

Theo looks at him.

Adrian finally turns away from the window.

ADRIAN
We forgot it.

He looks at the black hull reflected across the entire room.

ADRIAN
That was not the same as killing it.

CUT TO BLACK.

END OF EPISODE
