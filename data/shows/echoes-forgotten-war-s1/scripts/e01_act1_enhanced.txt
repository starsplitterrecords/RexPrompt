ECHOES OF A FORGOTTEN WAR
Episode 1 — "THE WEIGHT OF DEAD IRON"
Enhanced Draft

ACT ONE

EXT. OLYRIA-PRIME TRENCH — DAY

The shift horn sounds somewhere above the cut.

Most of the excavation crew is already climbing out. Work lamps bleach the black trench walls. Dust hangs in the air. A sonic drill keeps hammering long after everything else has begun to go quiet.

THEO leans into it.

He is young enough that the exhaustion still looks temporary. His gloves are split at the knuckles. One thumb rubs absently against his index finger, as though feeling for a tool that is not there.

VARK comes down the ladder carrying two helmets and the expression of a man who has already had this argument.

VARK
Shift's over.

THEO
Not yet.

VARK
You said that an hour ago.

Theo does not look at him.

VARK
Theo.

THEO
There's something under us.

Vark looks at the wall. The floor. The drill.

VARK
There is rock under us. That's the job.

The drill changes pitch.

Theo freezes.

Not because the sound is alarming.

Because he recognizes it.

Vark hears the difference a moment later and reaches for the shutoff.

Theo catches his wrist.

VARK
Let go.

THEO
Wait.

VARK
The rig sounds sick.

THEO
It isn't the rig.

VARK
Then that's worse.

Theo's grip tightens. He is staring at a hairline break in the bedrock.

THEO
I've seen what comes next.

Vark studies him.

VARK
In the survey?

THEO
No.

A faint violet line appears in the crack.

It is not bright. It does not pulse or announce itself. It simply exists where no light should be.

Vark slowly lowers his hand from the shutoff.

VARK
Theo.

THEO
That's it.

The rock under the drill gives way.

The machine lurches forward. Theo kills it and stumbles clear as a slab of black stone collapses into a hollow beneath the trench.

Dust rolls through the work lamps.

When it thins, something enormous is visible below.

A dark surface. Smooth in places, scarred in others. Not a wall. Not a vessel. Not anything Vark has a word for.

Theo steps toward the opening.

VARK
No.

Theo stops at the edge.

Vark looks from the buried thing to Theo.

The question on his face is no longer What did we find?

It is How did you know?

Theo's hand rises toward the darkness below before he realizes what he is doing.

He curls it into a fist.

CUT TO:

INT. ZENITH SPIRE ARCHIVES — CONTINUOUS

Far above Olyria-Prime, the Zenith Spire is almost offensively calm.

No alarms. No smoke. Just ordered shelves, quiet glass, the soft traffic of clerks going home.

RAE is alone at a records station.

On the screen in front of her, an old map is changing.

Not updating.

Remembering.

A region that should be blank has begun to acquire a name.

Rae watches the letters appear one at a time.

She knows the name before it is complete.

Her hand moves to her thigh. Her thumb traces the same small shape it has traced there since childhood: a bent line inside a broken circle.

Footsteps approach.

Rae puts a freight ledger over the lower half of the display.

CAELUM enters with his coat still fastened, irritated at being called away from somewhere more important.

CAELUM
What happened at Olyria-Prime?

RAE
The report says a mining accident.

CAELUM
I didn't ask what the report says.

RAE
Then you should ask the people who wrote it.

He looks past her toward the screen.

Rae stands.

CAELUM
Move.

RAE
You wanted the freight manifests.

She hands him the ledger.

He does not take it.

CAELUM
I want the screen behind you.

RAE
That's a different request.

Caelum finally looks at her.

CAELUM
You look ill.

RAE
I work nights.

For a moment, neither moves.

Then Caelum takes the ledger.

He flips it open with visible annoyance.

CAELUM
These are late.

RAE
They were late yesterday.

CAELUM
And today.

RAE
A pattern.

He gives her a long look.

RAE
You asked for them.

Caelum turns away, still reading.

CAELUM
Send me the Olyria file when it is complete.

RAE
Of course.

He exits.

Rae waits until his footsteps disappear.

Then she pulls the ledger away.

The name is complete.

Under it, the archive has drawn an evacuation mark.

The same mark Rae has been tracing against her leg for most of her life.

She sits.

The breath leaves her.

RAE
No.

The mark remains.

She touches the screen.

Nothing happens.

That is somehow worse.

EXT. SECTOR FOUR EXCAVATION — CONTINUOUS

The buried structure has been uncovered enough to tower above Theo and Vark.

No one else is in the trench.

Vark has made sure of that.

He has also made sure Theo stays several steps away from it.

VARK
We wait.

THEO
For who?

VARK
Someone whose job description includes ancient nightmare buried under the mine.

THEO
There isn't anyone like that.

VARK
Then we wait for someone willing to pretend.

Theo stares at the structure.

The violet line is gone.

In ordinary work light, the thing is almost dull.

That does not make it less wrong.

VARK
You said you dreamed it.

THEO
I dreamed being here.

VARK
That's not the same thing.

THEO
It is to me.

He steps forward.

Vark blocks him with one arm.

VARK
I am not dragging you home by your ankles.

THEO
Then don't.

VARK
And that makes touching it a good idea?

THEO
No.

VARK
Good. We agree on something.

Theo looks at him.

THEO
It makes not touching it impossible.

Vark closes his eyes.

VARK
I knew you were going to say something like that.

Theo pulls off one glove.

VARK
Theo.

Theo places his palm against the dark surface.

The trench disappears.

Not in light.

In memory.

For a heartbeat Theo is standing in a blackened plaza under a sky that should not exist.

Orbital canopies burn overhead.

Thousands of people move around him with the terrible purpose of people who have practiced evacuation too many times.

A woman grabs Theo by the shoulders and shouts something he cannot hear.

Beyond her, soldiers in white and gold are dragging civilians toward an open gate while red banners fall through smoke.

Theo looks down.

His hand is not empty.

It holds a long black stylus over a command table.

Lines of retreat spread beneath it.

His hand knows where to draw next.

THEO
No—

The trench slams back into existence.

Theo is on his knees.

His hand has come away from the structure.

Vark is against the opposite wall, staring at empty air.

VARK
There were people.

Theo looks at him.

VARK
There were people here.

THEO
Not here.

Vark turns toward the buried structure.

VARK
Where?

Theo looks at his empty hand.

His fingers are still shaped around the absent stylus.

THEO
I don't know.

A sound echoes through the trench.

Not machinery.

Distant thunder, perhaps.

Or something enormous breaking in a sky that no longer exists.

EXT. STABILIZING GRID RUINS — LATER

The site has been cleared.

Theo sits against the trench wall with a bandage pressed to his forehead.

The buried structure remains where they found it.

ADRIAN walks down through the abandoned work site.

He is too clean for the trench and too unsurprised by it.

Theo sees him.

THEO
Tell me you see it.

Adrian stops.

He looks at Theo.

ADRIAN
I see you bleeding.

THEO
Look at it.

ADRIAN
I don't need to.

Theo laughs once.

There is nothing amused in it.

THEO
Then you know what it is.

ADRIAN
I know what follows recognition.

Theo pushes himself upright.

THEO
I've been seeing this place for years.

ADRIAN
Not this place.

Theo stops.

THEO
No.

For the first time, Adrian's expression changes.

Only slightly.

ADRIAN
How long?

THEO
Since I was a child.

Adrian looks toward the buried structure without turning his head.

THEO
Who are you?

ADRIAN
Someone who would prefer that you not touch it again.

Theo moves toward the structure.

Adrian catches his wrist.

No examination. No pulse. Just a firm stop.

ADRIAN
Don't.

THEO
Why?

ADRIAN
Because the first time it remembered you.

Theo looks down at Adrian's hand.

ADRIAN
The second time, you may remember it.

Theo pulls free.

THEO
I already remember things.

ADRIAN
Fragments.

THEO
A city.

Adrian says nothing.

THEO
People running.

Nothing.

THEO
A sky on fire.

Adrian finally turns toward the structure.

His composure fails.

For one second, he looks old in a way age cannot explain.

THEO
You know it.

ADRIAN
I know enough.

THEO
Enough for what?

ADRIAN
To know this did not begin today.

Theo's thumb finds his index finger again.

The absent stylus.

Adrian notices.

This frightens him more than the structure.

ADRIAN
We're late.

END OF ACT ONE
