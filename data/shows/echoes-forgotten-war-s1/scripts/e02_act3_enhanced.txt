ACT THREE

INT. ARRAY COMMAND NEXUS — CONTINUOUS

The archive has begun closing itself behind Adrian and Tallis.

Heavy doors move one after another through the dark.

Tallis has the final record key.

Adrian stands between him and the last open doorway.

TALLIS
Move.

ADRIAN
Open it.

TALLIS
No.

ADRIAN
Tallis.

TALLIS
No.

He clutches the key tighter.

TALLIS
You witness. You don't choose.

ADRIAN
I've spent a long time pretending those are different things.

TALLIS
They are different.

ADRIAN
Not when witnessing becomes permission.

TALLIS
That's a beautiful sentence for a man standing in my way.

The door behind Adrian closes.

Tallis pushes off the wall toward the remaining exit.

Adrian catches him by the collar.

Everything stops.

Adrian looks at his own hand gripping the fabric.

Tallis looks at it too.

TALLIS
Let me go.

Adrian does not.

TALLIS
You said you wouldn't touch me.

ADRIAN
I know.

Tallis fights him.

Not elegantly.

A frightened man twisting for breath in a room that is closing around them.

His nails rake across Adrian's hand.

TALLIS
You don't get to make my fear into permission.

ADRIAN
You're right.

TALLIS
Then stop.

Adrian looks at the final release plate.

Then at Tallis.

ADRIAN
I can't.

He forces Tallis's hand onto the plate.

The last door unlocks.

Adrian lets go immediately.

Tallis drifts away, coughing.

He touches his throat.

TALLIS
That was a choice.

Adrian looks at the crescent marks in his palm.

ADRIAN
Yes.

TALLIS
Was it yours?

Adrian looks up.

TALLIS
Or did you remember that too?

That hits somewhere Tallis did not know existed.

Adrian says nothing.

The final door opens.

Beyond it, shelves of black-wrapped records disappear into darkness.

Adrian does not enter immediately.

For the first time since arriving at Nuxis, he looks afraid of himself.

INT. MAIN TRANSIT JUNCTION — CONTINUOUS

Theo crosses the empty concourse.

The shard is wrapped again and tucked under his arm.

VORN steps from behind a column with three municipal officers.

VORN
Stop.

Theo does.

Vorn sees the exhaustion on his face.

VORN
They told me you were a junior attaché.

THEO
I was this morning.

VORN
Put the relic down.

Theo hears Mero's voice.

Not in the room.

Inside the certainty arriving in his own thoughts.

MERO (V.O.)
End this.

Theo closes his eyes.

THEO
No.

VORN
Who are you talking to?

Theo opens his eyes.

The concourse is changing around the edges.

The bright modern floor is becoming cracked black stone.

The officers do not see it yet.

MERO (V.O.)
They will not give you another chance.

Theo knows what the old answer is.

Use the shard.

Use the memory.

Turn everyone in the room into an acceptable cost.

THEO
Neither will I.

VORN
Theo.

Theo looks at him.

VORN
Put it down.

Theo slowly unwraps the shard.

The officers raise their weapons.

VORN
Easy.

Theo kneels.

He sets the shard on the floor.

Then removes his hand.

MERO (V.O.)
Pick it up.

THEO
I am not your general.

VORN
What did you say?

THEO
Not to you.

The station remembers.

The lights vanish.

For one impossible instant the junction is the ruin it once was—or might once have been.

The ceiling is open to a burning sky.

Hundreds of civilians are face-down on the floor.

A shadow crosses above them so large the whole concourse darkens beneath it.

One of Vorn's officers screams.

Another drops to his knees and covers his head.

Vorn sees a woman reaching for him from beneath fallen stone.

He knows her name.

He has never met her.

VORN
No—

The present returns.

Dark.

Silent.

The officers' powered equipment is dead.

Their weapons are inert in their hands.

The shard lies exactly where Theo placed it.

Theo stares at it.

Mero is gone.

For the first time all night, Theo hears only his own breathing.

Vorn is still on his knees.

VORN
Who was she?

Theo looks at him.

THEO
I don't know.

VORN
I knew her.

THEO
I know.

Theo picks up the shard and wraps it again.

Vorn does not try to stop him.

Theo walks into the dark.

He does not look back to see whether the station can be made to do that again.

INT. DEEP-RANGE RELAY STATION 04 — CONTINUOUS

The station is almost empty now.

An evacuation harness drops from the ceiling and swings uselessly beside Rae.

Kyn grabs the back of her chair.

KYN
Rae. We're leaving.

RAE
Not yet.

KYN
You have said that through three alarms.

RAE
His channel is still open.

KYN
And the room is coming apart.

RAE
I need another minute.

Kyn pulls the chair away from the console.

Rae hooks one foot around the desk leg.

KYN
Really?

RAE
Put me back.

KYN
No.

RAE
Kyn.

KYN
I'm not worried about the station.

Rae stops fighting.

RAE
What?

KYN
I'm worried about you.

The words land in a space where Rae has no prepared response.

RAE
That's badly timed.

KYN
I had better timing before you started a small apocalypse in my relay room.

RAE
It isn't mine.

KYN
Good.

He kneels beside her.

KYN
Then you don't have to die with it.

Rae looks down.

There is blood on the side of her shirt from where debris caught her earlier.

She had not noticed.

Or had chosen not to.

Kyn reaches toward it and waits.

Rae nods.

He presses a folded cloth against the cut.

Rae winces.

KYN
There. A recognizable human response.

RAE
Don't get attached to it.

The radio clicks.

A breath.

THEO (V.O.)
Rae?

Rae laughs once.

Small. Unplanned.

Kyn hears it and looks away as if he did not.

Rae grabs the handset.

RAE
I'm here.

THEO (V.O.)
Everything went dark.

RAE
Are you hurt?

THEO (V.O.)
I don't think so.

RAE
That's not an answer.

THEO (V.O.)
I can walk.

RAE
Better.

Kyn helps Rae to her feet.

She lets him.

They move toward the exit together with the radio still open between them.

EXT. UPPER SECTOR MAINTENANCE HATCH — LATER

Theo climbs the final ladder toward a sealed hatch.

Rain drums against the metal above him.

He rests on the last landing, breathing hard.

The shard is packed inside his coat.

The radio crackles.

RAE (V.O.)
I can still see patrol movement above you.

Theo looks up at the hatch.

Mero's voice is gone.

But the habits remain.

Every part of Theo's mind wants a route, a probability, an answer that will make opening the door feel like calculation instead of choice.

THEO
No.

RAE (V.O.)
No what?

THEO
Walk to the glass.

A beat.

RAE (V.O.)
Theo—

THEO
Please.

INT. RELAY STATION CORRIDOR — CONTINUOUS

Rae and Kyn stop beside a tall observation window.

Rain sheets down the glass.

Rae looks at a status display beside it.

Then deliberately looks away.

RAE
I'm there.

INTERCUT — HATCH / WINDOW

THEO
What's outside?

Rae looks.

Really looks.

RAE
Rain.

Theo closes his eyes.

RAE
A torn maintenance banner.

The banner snaps against a railing in the wind.

RAE
Empty plaza.

THEO
Anyone waiting for me?

Rae searches with her own eyes.

RAE
No one I can see.

Theo puts one hand on the hatch wheel.

He stops.

THEO
I need you to say it without the numbers.

Rae understands.

She rests her forehead against the wet-cold glass.

RAE
If you open it, you're only going to find rain.

Theo smiles despite himself.

THEO
What does the tactical system say?

Rae looks at the display beside her.

Its search pattern is still sweeping empty corridors far below Theo.

She switches it off.

RAE
Let it keep looking in the dark.

Theo turns the wheel.

The hatch opens.

Rain pours through.

Cold water hits his face and hair and coat.

Theo climbs out onto the plaza.

No army.

No burning canopy.

No general.

Only weather.

He stands in it.

RAE (V.O.)
Theo?

THEO
Yeah.

RAE (V.O.)
What do you see?

Theo looks across the empty plaza.

THEO
What you said.

A beat.

THEO
Rain.

He walks into it.

Below him, the dark transit system continues searching for a war that is not there.

CUT TO BLACK.

END OF EPISODE
