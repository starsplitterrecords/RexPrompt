ACT TWO

INT. ZENITH TELEMETRY ROOM — CONTINUOUS

A portable drive sits in the archive slot.

Rae watches names, images and fragments of testimony move onto it.

Not numbers.

Names.

Places that do not officially exist.

Evacuation notices issued by governments the current histories never mention.

A photograph of a plaza Rae saw in a dream when she was nine.

The door behind her unlocks with an angry metallic snap.

Caelum enters.

He takes in the drive.

The open records.

Rae.

CAELUM
Step away.

RAE
I can't.

CAELUM
You mean you won't.

RAE
No. I mean I can't leave it here.

He walks to the center of the room and stops.

No grabbing. No shouting.

That restraint makes the threat clearer.

CAELUM
You have a life in this Spire.

RAE
Then tell me why that place was erased.

CAELUM
Give me the drive.

RAE
Tell me its name.

CAELUM
Rae.

RAE
You know it too.

He says nothing.

The copy finishes.

Rae removes the drive and puts it inside her coat.

Caelum moves between her and the door.

CAELUM
There are people above me who will not treat this as curiosity.

RAE
I don't think you do either.

CAELUM
You found a broken record.

RAE
It wasn't broken.

CAELUM
You don't know what you found.

RAE
Neither do you.

That lands.

Rae sees it.

CAELUM
You are about to lose access to every archive in this system.

RAE
Then I should take good notes.

CAELUM
This isn't a joke.

RAE
I know.

She steps toward him.

He does not move.

RAE
Say the name.

CAELUM
Go back to your station.

RAE
Say it.

He looks away.

Rae walks past him.

At the door, she speaks the forgotten name quietly.

Caelum flinches before he can stop himself.

Rae looks back.

There it is.

Not proof of what the place was.

Proof that somebody has been afraid of it for a very long time.

She leaves.

INT. TRIAGE TENT — LATER

Theo sits on a folding cot while a MEDIC wraps his hand.

The medic slaps his wrist away when he tries to help.

MEDIC
You can either be the patient or you can do it yourself.

Theo lowers his hand.

Across the tent, Vark fills out an incident form with a pen that keeps dying.

He shakes it.

Nothing.

He steals another from a tray.

Outside, somebody is shouting about a missing boot.

WORKER (O.S.)
I came in with two!

ANOTHER WORKER (O.S.)
Then this is already a good day!

Vark drinks from a paper cup.

He immediately regrets it.

VARK
This coffee is an argument against civilization.

Theo almost smiles.

Vark returns to the form.

VARK
You want me to write "chemical fire."

THEO
Yes.

VARK
There was no chemical fire.

THEO
Then make one up.

Vark looks over.

VARK
I spent twenty years keeping idiots from lying on incident reports.

THEO
Today can be your retirement party.

VARK
I hate cake.

The medic finishes the bandage.

MEDIC
Keep it clean.

THEO
For how long?

MEDIC
Until you stop asking questions I can't answer.

She moves on.

Vark brings the form over.

VARK
They'll send people.

THEO
I know.

VARK
People with cleaner boots than mine.

THEO
Probably.

VARK
People who will call whatever is down there theirs before they know what it is.

Theo looks at him.

Vark sits on the empty cot opposite.

The humor drains away.

VARK
I saw the sky change.

THEO
So did I.

VARK
I saw people running through a place that wasn't there.

Theo says nothing.

VARK
One of them looked at me.

That catches Theo.

THEO
What did they say?

VARK
Nothing.

A beat.

VARK
That's the part I can't stop thinking about.

He looks down at the incident form.

VARK
You know what I want?

THEO
No.

VARK
I want this to be a chemical fire.

Theo looks at his wrapped hand.

VARK
That's why I'm lying.

Vark tears the identifying sheet from the form.

Theo looks up.

VARK
This buys us a few hours.

THEO
Us?

VARK
Don't make me regret the pronoun.

He stands.

VARK
And Theo?

THEO
Yeah.

VARK
Whatever you found down there, you didn't find it alone.

Vark leaves.

Theo sits with that.

His thumb presses against his index finger.

He stops himself.

INT. LOWER-TIER ARCHIVES — NIGHT

The lower archive has none of the polished beauty of the Spire above.

Old shelves. Dust. Physical books no one has requested in generations.

Rae moves through them with the stolen drive in her pocket.

She finds ADRIAN already seated at a reading table.

A sealed volume rests in front of him.

RAE
You're in my archive.

ADRIAN
Not this part.

RAE
Everything in this building is my archive.

ADRIAN
That confidence will be useful where you're going.

Rae stops.

RAE
You know what's on this.

She touches the drive through her coat.

ADRIAN
Enough to know you should destroy it.

RAE
That's convenient.

ADRIAN
Convenience has nothing to do with it.

Rae moves closer.

RAE
Why was the record erased?

Adrian looks at the sealed book.

ADRIAN
Because some histories were not lost.

RAE
What does that mean?

ADRIAN
They were removed.

RAE
By whom?

ADRIAN
By the people who survived it.

Rae studies him.

RAE
Survived what?

No answer.

She pulls a scrap of paper from her pocket.

On it, she has drawn the evacuation mark.

She places it on the table.

Adrian's eyes move to it.

A tiny reaction.

Rae sees it.

RAE
You know this.

ADRIAN
Where did you find it?

RAE
I didn't.

He looks at her.

RAE
I've been drawing it since I was a child.

Adrian closes the sealed book.

RAE
What is it?

ADRIAN
A reason to stop asking questions in public.

RAE
That isn't an answer.

ADRIAN
It's the answer that keeps you alive tonight.

RAE
What were they afraid of?

ADRIAN
Who?

RAE
The people who removed the history.

Adrian considers her.

ADRIAN
Themselves.

That is not what Rae expected.

RAE
Why?

ADRIAN
When did you first dream it?

Rae's face closes.

ADRIAN
Then you already know enough to be in danger.

RAE
I don't know anything.

ADRIAN
You remember something.

RAE
Those are not the same.

ADRIAN
They will be.

Rae takes back the paper.

She turns to go.

ADRIAN
Destroy the drive.

RAE
Tell me what's on it.

ADRIAN
I can't.

RAE
Won't.

Adrian does not correct her.

Rae leaves.

Adrian remains at the table.

Only after she is gone does he open the sealed book.

The same evacuation mark is stamped inside the cover.

EXT. SECTOR FOUR EXCAVATION — CONTINUOUS

Theo drags a sheet of wrecked plating across the trench floor.

It catches on a broken rail.

He swears and pulls harder.

The buried structure is still exposed.

A distant patrol siren rises from the surface.

Adrian watches from the trench edge.

THEO
They're coming.

ADRIAN
Yes.

Theo tries the plating again.

It shifts a little.

THEO
Help me.

ADRIAN
You spent years wanting proof.

THEO
I have it.

ADRIAN
And now?

Theo looks at the structure.

At the place his hand touched it.

THEO
Now I don't want them to have it.

Adrian descends into the trench.

ADRIAN
Good.

Together, they pull the plating over the exposed surface.

Theo looks at him.

THEO
You could have helped five minutes ago.

ADRIAN
Five minutes ago you were still deciding what it belonged to.

THEO
It belongs to whoever made it.

ADRIAN
Do you believe that?

Theo does not answer.

A patrol drone appears above the trench.

Theo ducks instinctively.

Adrian does not.

He steps into the open and raises his envoy seal.

The drone pauses.

ADRIAN
Site reviewed under neutral authority. Primary collapse is on the west cut.

A mechanical acknowledgment.

The drone turns and disappears toward the far excavation.

Theo watches it go.

THEO
You could have done that when it arrived.

ADRIAN
Yes.

THEO
You were watching me.

ADRIAN
Yes.

THEO
Why?

Adrian looks at the covered relic.

ADRIAN
Because I needed to know whether you wanted to own it.

THEO
I don't.

ADRIAN
Not anymore.

Theo looks down at his injured hand.

THEO
What does it belong to?

ADRIAN
That is not the question anymore.

THEO
What is?

Adrian looks at Theo.

ADRIAN
Who it remembers.

The patrol siren fades.

Under the wreckage, something answers with a soft pulse of light.

Theo takes one step back.

END OF ACT TWO
