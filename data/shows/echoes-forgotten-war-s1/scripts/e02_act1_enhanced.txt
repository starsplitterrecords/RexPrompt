ECHOES OF A FORGOTTEN WAR
Episode 2 — "GLASS DUST AND VIOLET LIGHT"
Enhanced Draft

ACT ONE

INT. LOWER KAELEN-SECTOR MAG-LEV HUB — NIGHT

The last train has already gone.

The lower hub is wet from condensation and almost empty except for THEO and the municipal officers surrounding him.

KORSEN stands at the front of the line.

Theo's coat is torn. One hand stays inside it, closed around the wrapped shard he carried out of Olyria-Prime.

KORSEN
Put it down.

THEO
I don't think I can.

KORSEN
That's not a choice I'm leaving with you.

Theo looks at the officers.

Not their weapons.

Their positions.

Something about the platform suddenly feels familiar.

His thumb touches his index finger.

The missing stylus.

THEO
Move your people away from the middle of the platform.

Korsen waits.

KORSEN
Why?

Theo looks toward a pillar.

For a heartbeat it is scorched black.

A body lies beside it.

Then the pillar is clean again.

THEO
I don't know.

KORSEN
You sounded very sure a second ago.

THEO
That wasn't the same thing.

KORSEN
Hand out of your coat.

Theo does not move.

A station announcement clicks on overhead.

The words distort.

For half a second the speaker carries another voice entirely.

WOMAN'S VOICE (V.O.)
Clear the center. Move to the walls.

Everyone hears it.

Korsen looks up.

The lights change.

Not off. Not broken.

Changed.

The modern hub is suddenly crowded with people who are not there.

Families pressed against the walls.

Uniformed figures dragging the wounded through smoke.

Red banners burning above the tracks.

One of Korsen's officers recoils as a phantom child runs straight through him.

Then the hub is empty again.

Korsen stares at Theo.

Theo looks as frightened as he does.

THEO
Move them.

Korsen raises one hand.

KORSEN
Back to the walls.

His squad obeys.

Theo slips through the opening before anyone decides whether that was permission.

As he passes Korsen—

KORSEN
Who told you?

Theo keeps moving.

THEO
I wish I knew.

INTERCUT — NUXIS ARRAY ARCHIVES

ADRIAN floats beside a sealed records terminal while a young ARCHIVIST tries very hard not to look afraid.

Adrian lays one hand across the terminal screen.

Nothing more.

The archivist stops typing.

ADRIAN
Where did they put the records from the erased sectors?

ARCHIVIST
There are no erased sectors.

ADRIAN
Then this should be a very short conversation.

ARCHIVIST
Take your hand off the terminal.

Adrian does.

The archivist was not expecting that.

ADRIAN
Now answer me voluntarily.

ARCHIVIST
I don't know what you're talking about.

ADRIAN
Yes, you do.

ARCHIVIST
You can't prove that.

ADRIAN
I am not here to prove anything.

The archivist looks toward the nearest exit.

Adrian notices.

ADRIAN
Tallis.

The name lands.

ARCHIVIST
What?

ADRIAN
Where is he?

The archivist's silence is enough.

INTERCUT — RELAY STATION 04

RAE sits at a borrowed work station in a room that is clearly not hers.

A coat is draped over the back of her chair. A half-eaten meal has been pushed aside to make space for paper maps.

Theo's voice crackles through a small speaker.

THEO (V.O.)
I'm clear of the platform.

Rae traces his route with a pencil.

Then stops.

Ahead of him, the current transit map shows a dead end.

Rae knows what is beyond it.

Her face changes.

She has been there before.

Not in life.

RAE
Theo.

THEO (V.O.)
Still here.

RAE
Don't take the stairs.

THEO (V.O.)
Why?

Rae looks at the blank place on the map.

RAE
Because they won't get you where you think they do.

THEO (V.O.)
How do you know?

Rae does not answer.

She draws a corridor where the map says none exists.

CUT TO:

INT. NUXIS ARRAY ARCHIVES — CONTINUOUS

TALLIS drifts backward through the sealed archive with a brass record key held against his chest.

Adrian follows without hurry.

TALLIS
You're an envoy.

ADRIAN
Yes.

TALLIS
You witness. You don't compel.

ADRIAN
Open the first seal.

TALLIS
That's compelling.

Adrian stops.

The rotating archive moves around them in complete silence.

Tallis looks at Adrian's sidearm.

Adrian sees him look.

He makes no effort to hide it.

TALLIS
You don't even know what you're asking for.

ADRIAN
People are remembering things your archive says never happened.

TALLIS
People remember things wrong every day.

ADRIAN
Do entire rooms remember with them?

Tallis says nothing.

ADRIAN
Do strangers remember the same dead city?

TALLIS
Fear doesn't cancel your mandate.

ADRIAN
No.

Adrian looks around the vault.

ADRIAN
It makes the mandate smaller than the thing it was meant to contain.

TALLIS
That's what everyone says just before they decide the rules were meant for someone else.

Adrian takes that.

TALLIS
And if you're wrong?

ADRIAN
Then you can spend the rest of your life condemning me.

TALLIS
I can start now.

A beat.

Adrian almost smiles.

Almost.

ADRIAN
Open the first seal.

Tallis looks toward the sealed door.

TALLIS
If I do this, there is no version where I chose freely.

ADRIAN
I know.

That answer disturbs Tallis more than denial would have.

He turns the record key.

A heavy door begins to open.

Adrian looks through the widening gap.

For an instant, he sees not shelves but a tribunal chamber full of people waiting to be sentenced.

He closes his eyes.

When he opens them, it is only an archive.

He enters.

INT. VENTILATION CONDUIT ALPHA — LATER

Theo crawls through a service passage barely wide enough for his shoulders.

Enforcer boots pass on the level above him.

He stops breathing until they move on.

RAE (V.O.)
There should be a shaft below you.

Theo looks down.

Metal floor.

THEO
There isn't.

RAE (V.O.)
Look again.

THEO
I'm looking at a wall.

RAE (V.O.)
I didn't say it was on the map.

Theo closes his eyes.

THEO
Rae, how do you know it's there?

Silence on the radio.

THEO
Rae.

RAE (V.O.)
I remember it.

Theo opens his eyes.

THEO
From where?

RAE (V.O.)
Somewhere I haven't been.

Theo lets his forehead rest against the conduit wall.

THEO
That's becoming a problem.

RAE (V.O.)
Do you see a plate under your left knee?

Theo shifts.

A plate almost completely hidden under old sealant.

THEO
Yes.

RAE (V.O.)
Then trust the problem.

Theo works his fingers beneath it.

It will not move.

He tries again.

Above him, the boots stop.

Theo freezes.

A flashlight beam enters through the grate behind him.

He pulls hard.

The plate tears free.

Beneath it is darkness.

A shaft.

Not on any current plan.

Theo stares down.

THEO
There it is.

On the other end, Rae closes her eyes.

RAE (V.O.)
I know.

THEO
How?

RAE (V.O.)
I was standing there when the evacuation started.

THEO
You weren't.

RAE (V.O.)
I know.

The flashlight beam gets closer.

Theo lowers himself into the shaft.

THEO
Rae?

RAE (V.O.)
What?

THEO
So was I.

Theo lets go.

He drops into darkness.

INT. DEEP-RANGE RELAY STATION 04 — CONTINUOUS

Rae hears Theo land over the radio.

A hard impact.

Then a groan.

RAE
Theo?

THEO (V.O.)
Still alive.

RAE
Try to make a habit of that.

KYN enters carrying his coat and Rae's abandoned bag.

KYN
Everyone else has left.

RAE
I noticed.

KYN
Apparently not the important part.

An alarm repeats somewhere outside the room.

Rae keeps one hand on the radio.

KYN
Come on.

RAE
If this relay dies, I lose him.

KYN
You barely know him.

RAE
He remembers the same place I do.

Kyn stops.

KYN
You could have started with that.

RAE
I was hoping not to say it out loud.

KYN
I was hoping to go home.

RAE
Sorry.

KYN
No, you're not.

RAE
Not yet.

A faint sound enters the radio channel.

Not Theo.

Bells.

Far away.

Rae goes still.

KYN
What is that?

The bells continue.

Under them, a crowd.

Voices calling to one another in panic.

Rae stands.

RAE
I've heard this.

KYN
Where?

RAE
When I was eight.

Kyn looks at the radio.

KYN
In a recording?

Rae shakes her head.

A WOMAN'S VOICE pushes through the static.

WOMAN'S VOICE (V.O.)
The living move first. Keep the gate clear.

Rae's hand moves to her thigh.

She traces the old evacuation mark.

Kyn sees it.

KYN
Rae.

The bells stop.

Theo's breathing returns to the channel.

THEO (V.O.)
Rae?

Rae grabs the radio.

RAE
I'm here.

She looks at Kyn.

For once, she has no explanation ready.

END OF ACT ONE
