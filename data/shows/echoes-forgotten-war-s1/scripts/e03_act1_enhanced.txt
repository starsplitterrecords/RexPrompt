ECHOES OF A FORGOTTEN WAR
Episode 3 — "THE ARCHITECTURE OF RUIN"
Enhanced Draft

ACT ONE

INT. VAEL-TOR PLATFORMS — NIGHT

The storm outside makes the whole platform groan.

Inside a sealed operations room, RAE and TALLIS stand before an old display that should not be working.

Tallis's palm is still bandaged from Nuxis.

He notices Rae looking at it.

TALLIS
Don't.

RAE
I didn't say anything.

TALLIS
You were about to.

RAE
I was going to ask if it hurts.

TALLIS
That's worse.

The display changes.

A modern map of Vael-Tor disappears.

In its place: the same world, but wrong.

Whole districts are gone.

The coastline is black.

A line of enormous structures stretches across the horizon where the present city now stands.

Tallis reaches for his comm.

Rae catches his wrist.

Not hard.

Enough to stop him.

TALLIS
Let go.

RAE
If you call command, this disappears before they walk through the door.

TALLIS
You don't know that.

RAE
I watched Zenith erase Olyria while I was still looking at it.

Tallis lowers his hand.

The display fills with people moving through ruined streets.

No labels.

No explanation.

Then one word appears at the edge of the image.

ATLAS.

TALLIS
What is that?

RAE
A name.

TALLIS
Of what?

RAE
I don't know.

The image changes again.

A wave of destruction moves across the world.

Not as an animation.

As memory.

Buildings fall.

Crowds vanish into dust.

A skyline Rae has seen in dreams breaks in exactly the way she remembers.

Tallis watches, horrified.

TALLIS
Is this going to happen?

Rae looks at the old streets beneath the modern map.

RAE
No.

TALLIS
How can you know that?

RAE
Because it already did.

Tallis turns toward her.

The room around them briefly becomes the ruined city on the screen.

Dust blows through a wall that is still physically there.

A man runs past Tallis carrying a child.

Then they are alone again.

Tallis's comm hangs forgotten at his side.

RAE
Now you can call someone if you want.

Tallis does not.

TALLIS
Who was Atlas?

Rae looks at the name.

RAE
That's a better question.

INT. SMUGGLING SKIFF HOLD — NIGHT

TYRUS turns Theo's wrapped shard over in his hands.

Theo hates every second of it.

TYRUS
This is what everyone is trying to arrest you for?

THEO
Give it back.

TYRUS
I expected something more impressive.

THEO
That is because you have excellent instincts.

TYRUS
You paid me to move contraband. I inspect contraband.

THEO
You have inspected it.

Tyrus unwraps one corner of the cloth.

The shard is black and dull.

TYRUS
That's it?

THEO
That's it.

Tyrus holds it up toward the small cargo window.

Something crosses the reflected glass behind him.

A vast dark hull.

Tyrus turns.

Nothing outside but the lights of Vael-Tor.

TYRUS
What was that?

THEO
Give it back.

Frost spreads across the wall beside Tyrus.

Not randomly.

It forms the outline of a long corridor.

Doors.

A military seal.

Footprints moving through it.

Tyrus stares.

THEO
Tyrus.

TYRUS
Cover it.

Theo takes the shard.

TYRUS
Cover it now.

Theo wraps it.

The frost remains for another second.

Then collapses into water.

Tyrus wipes his hand on his shirt.

TYRUS
I don't want to know what that is.

THEO
Neither do I.

TYRUS
That's a lie.

Theo puts the shard in his satchel.

THEO
Yes.

TYRUS
You leave the hold when we dock.

THEO
That was the plan.

TYRUS
No. The plan was that I decide when you leave.

Theo shoulders the satchel.

TYRUS
Now I would like you gone before the walls remember something worse.

Theo reaches the hatch.

He looks back at the wet mark where the frost was.

THEO
That wasn't one of my memories.

Tyrus does not answer.

Theo leaves.

INT. DEFENSE GRID ACCESS — CONTINUOUS

Rae and JACE stand over an old manual control buried beneath newer platform hardware.

Outside, through a narrow window, a refugee transport fights the storm on approach to Vael-Tor.

Jace looks from the ship to the rusted control.

JACE
That barrier has been dead longer than either of us has been alive.

RAE
It isn't dead.

JACE
That is not comforting.

The transport shudders.

A band of pale light appears across the sky in front of it.

The ship turns hard away.

Jace's face changes.

JACE
It's blocking them.

Rae kneels beside the old control.

There is a symbol cut into the metal.

She knows it.

Not the refuge mark.

Something else.

A warning.

RAE
This wasn't built for storms.

JACE
What was it built for?

Rae looks out at the civilian ship.

RAE
Something that isn't coming anymore.

JACE
Tell it that.

Rae almost laughs.

Instead she grips the seized release handle.

It does not move.

Jace crouches beside her.

JACE
We can get a crew down here.

RAE
By the time they arrive, that ship will be somewhere else.

JACE
Yes. In pieces.

Rae looks at him.

JACE
I thought we were speaking plainly now.

Rae nods.

Together they pull.

The handle moves slightly.

Somewhere deep below them, something enormous answers.

Not machinery starting.

A remembered impact.

The whole room shudders as if struck by a weapon from centuries ago.

Jace looks up.

JACE
What did we just wake up?

RAE
I don't know.

She grips the handle again.

RAE
Help me open it before we find out.

INT. ANTIQUARIAN ARCHIVES — LATER

ADRIAN is cataloguing fragments at a long table when Theo enters.

Theo puts the satchel down between them.

Adrian does not look at it.

ADRIAN
No.

THEO
I haven't asked anything yet.

ADRIAN
You brought it here. That is the question.

Theo unwraps the shard.

Adrian turns away.

THEO
Tell me what it belonged to.

ADRIAN
Put it away.

THEO
Name it.

ADRIAN
No.

Theo stares at him.

THEO
That's becoming your favorite answer.

ADRIAN
It has kept more people alive than you understand.

THEO
Has it?

Adrian resumes sorting a tray of ordinary metal fragments.

Theo picks up one.

THEO
What is this?

ADRIAN
A hinge from a collapsed transit engine.

Theo puts it down.

He points to another.

THEO
This?

ADRIAN
A piece of hull plating.

Theo puts the shard beside them.

THEO
This?

Adrian says nothing.

THEO
There. That's different.

ADRIAN
Only because you insist it is.

The lights in the archive dim.

A shadow appears on the far wall.

Long.

Armored.

The silhouette of a vessel so large the wall cannot contain it.

Theo and Adrian both look.

Nothing in the room could cast that shadow.

It moves slowly across the wall and disappears.

Theo looks back at Adrian.

THEO
You saw it.

ADRIAN
Put the shard away.

THEO
You're afraid it will remember you.

Adrian finally looks at him.

Theo sees the answer before he hears it.

ADRIAN
You should be afraid of what remembers anything.

THEO
What did this belong to?

Adrian closes the case in front of him.

ADRIAN
Something we had no right to leave behind.

THEO
That is still not a name.

ADRIAN
No.

Theo wraps the shard again.

THEO
Then I'll find the name without you.

He shoulders the satchel.

Adrian watches him go.

Only after Theo leaves does Adrian look toward the wall where the impossible shadow passed.

His hand is shaking.

END OF ACT ONE
