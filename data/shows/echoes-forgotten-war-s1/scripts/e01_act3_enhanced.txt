ACT THREE

INT. ZENITH OBSERVATION DECK — NIGHT

The sky beyond the observation glass is bruised with distant weather and orbital traffic.

Rae stands alone at the disposal furnace.

The stolen drive rests in her palm.

Caelum enters behind her.

He closes the door.

CAELUM
You can still give it to me.

RAE
And you'll do what?

CAELUM
What keeps this place standing.

RAE
You already erased it once.

CAELUM
You don't know that.

RAE
Neither do you. That's the problem.

Caelum comes closer.

CAELUM
Everything you have is here.

Rae looks out at the city below.

CAELUM
Your work. Your quarters. Your clearance. Every person who still answers when you call.

RAE
That's a short list.

CAELUM
It is still a life.

RAE
No.

She looks at the drive.

RAE
Everything I have is what I remember.

Caelum's expression hardens.

CAELUM
They can take that too.

Rae looks at him.

He realizes what he has said.

Too late.

RAE
Who are "they"?

CAELUM
Give me the drive.

RAE
Say the name.

CAELUM
Stop.

RAE
Say it.

CAELUM
Rae—

RAE
If it's nothing, say it.

He cannot.

Rae opens her hand.

The drive drops into the furnace.

Caelum lunges.

Too late.

The object disappears into ordinary industrial fire.

No cosmic reaction.

No spectacular detonation.

Just a small piece of evidence becoming unrecoverable.

Caelum grabs Rae's wrist.

CAELUM
Do you understand what you've done?

RAE
Yes.

CAELUM
People will come here.

RAE
I know.

CAELUM
They will ask what you saw.

RAE
I know.

CAELUM
They will not stop because you destroyed a piece of metal.

Rae looks down at her empty palm.

RAE
Then I should leave before they try.

Caelum releases her.

RAE
You could come with me.

That surprises both of them.

Caelum looks toward the door.

He does not move.

RAE
Right.

Rae walks past him.

Caelum remains beside the furnace.

For the first time, he looks small inside the Spire.

EXT. ABANDONED TRENCH — CONTINUOUS

Theo packs the last layer of spoil over the buried structure.

He sees something black on the mound.

A small recording prism.

Adrian's.

Theo picks it up.

Adrian stands near the lift.

Theo takes an industrial cutter from the ground and switches it on.

The blade hums.

His hand is not steady.

ADRIAN
If you're going to threaten me, point it at me.

THEO
I am.

ADRIAN
No.

Adrian looks at Theo's eyes.

ADRIAN
You're watching the grave.

Theo glances down despite himself.

ADRIAN
There.

Theo raises the cutter.

THEO
Swear you didn't keep the location.

ADRIAN
You still think the location is the danger.

THEO
What is?

ADRIAN
Recognition.

Theo lowers the tool a fraction.

THEO
What recognized me?

ADRIAN
That's the question.

THEO
You know.

ADRIAN
I know what it resembles.

THEO
What?

Adrian looks toward the mound.

ADRIAN
A beginning.

THEO
Of what?

ADRIAN
I don't know.

Theo searches his face for the lie.

Doesn't find one.

THEO
You expect me to believe that?

ADRIAN
I would prefer you didn't.

THEO
You've been acting like you know all of this.

ADRIAN
I know pieces.

THEO
Pieces of what?

ADRIAN
A history that should not be able to return.

Theo looks at the prism in his hand.

THEO
Then erase this.

Adrian takes the prism.

He turns it over once.

ADRIAN
If it were only buried metal, none of this would matter.

He pockets it.

THEO
That isn't an answer.

ADRIAN
Get used to that feeling.

Adrian walks toward the lift.

Theo watches him go.

Under the packed earth, a soft pulse passes through the ground.

Theo feels it through his boots.

He does not look down.

INT. ZENITH TELEMETRY ROOM — LATER

The room is dark except for a few surviving panels.

Rae is at the console deleting the last local traces of her access.

Not data.

Her.

Door permissions.

Travel history.

Archive requests.

The shape of an ordinary life in an institution.

Adrian enters.

RAE
You keep appearing in places you don't belong.

ADRIAN
So do you.

Rae removes her identification crystal from her collar.

She looks at it.

ADRIAN
You burned it.

RAE
You told me to destroy the copy.

ADRIAN
I did.

RAE
I destroyed the trail.

ADRIAN
That was your life.

RAE
No. It was my job.

ADRIAN
The distinction matters now.

Rae holds out the crystal.

Adrian takes it.

He waits.

Rae gives a small nod.

Adrian snaps it in half.

The pieces fall into the ruined console.

Rae watches them disappear.

RAE
Where do I go?

ADRIAN
Somewhere you've never dreamed of.

Rae looks at him.

RAE
How do you know about the dreams?

ADRIAN
Because you aren't the first.

Rae's composure finally breaks.

Only for a moment.

RAE
Who else?

ADRIAN
I am not answering that here.

RAE
You haven't answered anything anywhere.

ADRIAN
And yet you keep following.

RAE
I'm not following you.

ADRIAN
Good.

He opens the maintenance exit.

Darkness beyond.

RAE
What happens if I stay?

Adrian looks back at the dead consoles.

ADRIAN
You become evidence.

Rae takes that in.

Then she walks through the door.

Adrian follows after a beat.

EXT. COMPACTED EARTH ABOVE SECTOR FOUR — LATER

Night.

The excavators are shut down.

Without engines, warning lights or crews, the site feels abandoned by the present.

Theo sits on the ground with a small shard from the buried structure in his bandaged hand.

He should not have it.

He knows that.

The shard is dark.

Adrian approaches from the path.

ADRIAN
Put it down.

THEO
They said the dreams were mine.

ADRIAN
They are.

THEO
You know what I mean.

ADRIAN
Yes.

Theo stands.

THEO
Then tell me whose memory it is.

ADRIAN
I can't.

THEO
Won't.

ADRIAN
Both.

THEO
That's not an answer.

ADRIAN
No.

Theo closes his hand around the shard.

A distant sound enters the night.

Not from the excavation.

Not from the sky above them.

From somewhere memory insists used to be there.

A long, impossible tearing.

Theo's eyes lift.

For an instant, the stars are hidden by the shadow of an orbital canopy that has been gone longer than anyone remembers.

Then it is gone.

Adrian has gone pale.

Theo sees it.

THEO
You heard it too.

ADRIAN
Put the shard down.

THEO
You knew what the sky sounded like.

Adrian turns away.

ADRIAN
There are no canopies left to fracture.

Theo steps toward him.

THEO
But you remember them.

Adrian stops.

A long silence.

ADRIAN
Yes.

Theo looks at the shard.

Then at Adrian.

The relief is immediate and terrible.

He is not imagining this.

Which means the impossible thing is real.

ADRIAN
Do not mistake being believed for being safe.

Adrian walks away.

Theo stands alone under the ordinary night.

He slips the shard into his coat.

For a moment, nothing happens.

The night remains completely ordinary.

That is enough.

CUT TO BLACK.

END OF EPISODE
