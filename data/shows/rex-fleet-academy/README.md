`Rex Fleet Academy` is the umbrella for one-off stories framed through lectures, demonstrations, field exercises and case studies. The Academy can teach pilots, navigators, technicians, mechanics, scientists, statesmen and junior officers without turning the comic into a detached encyclopedia.

## Issue 1 — Between the Beacons

The first one-off explains corridor physics and corridor culture as one system:

- inverse/reciprocal tension fields and **Riding the Inversion**;
- a public corridor as a measured three-dimensional safe volume rather than an ocean-style channel;
- the certified cross-section at roughly twice the beam of the largest ship the route is rated to carry;
- beacon-to-beacon navigation close to the measured direct spine;
- green as verified low-tension/public-safe status, with red as unsafe **or unknown**;
- red-by-default behavior on sensor failure and the possibility of extreme tension physically shattering a beacon;
- the chain from **Sundering → corridor collapse → Shattering**;
- Fleet and private survey culture, repeated passes, route age, drift and provenance;
- private routes sold through brokers to legitimate and illegitimate traffic;
- BHA harmonic-data leaks and the commercial value of knowing *why* a beacon is red;
- Fleet power as King's-Road/Roman-road stewardship: control of safe movement creates structural authority even when exercised for public benefit;
- the symbolic split between green Verge dependence, blue/gold Crown stewardship and bright-red Shard opportunity outside the public network;
- the wider ship-color grammar: purple survey, subtle-cyan blockade runner, yellow maintenance/science/geology, dark-purple/gold Tempest, and deep-crimson Fleet protection distinct from Shard red.

## Continuity bridge

The present-day `Beacon Harmonics Authority` is the mature-era BHA used by this issue. `Rex Fleet Academy: Shattering of the Corridors` is already represented in RexPrompt by the historical `shattering` package, where the High-Era predecessor is named the `Beacon Harmonization Authority`. Preserve that historical distinction rather than silently renaming the older institution.

`Jia Morgan` is shared with the Rex Fleet package and should retain the same handle and visual anchor.

## Color grammar

- **Green** — the Verge, civilian continuity, public routes people depend on.
- **Blue / gold / white** — Fleet and Crown authority, stewardship and institutional order.
- **Bright red** — Shards, fracture, unsafe/unmarked/independent space as a power source.
- **Purple** — survey craft and liminal route-finding.
- **Subtle cyan** — blockade runners as a hyper-attuned echo of proto-Fleet navigation.
- **Yellow** — maintenance, construction, geology and applied science.
- **Dark purple + gold** — Tempest, Fleet force operating at the margins and beyond ordinary corridor protection.
- **Deep crimson + Fleet gold** — Crimson heavy protection; fully aligned in the current era, harder-edged by mission, not Shard-coded.
