Duplicate original #232/#112 and #233/#117 were identified in prior review; never generate both as independent jokes.

**Writing gate for every future set:** (1) retrieve original source and preserve provenance; (2) check premise against all prior gags and duplicate pairs; (3) sharpen three escalating beats with an earned final-panel turn; (4) make evil business visibly consequential, not fantasy office window dressing; (5) preserve Eric's boundaries, the serious competent Dark Lord, and established cast; (6) verify speaker-to-panel indices, crop-safe lettering and three-panel physical continuity; (7) curate 25 with varied departments, characters, evil-business operations, locations and punchline mechanisms; (8) keep the authoritative strip records in `strips_vNN.json`, and register each 25-strip issue beneath the same single series entry in `data/shows.json`.

The masthead and geometry flow from the existing generationLine / `fod-strip` direction, not a duplicated per-strip prompt. Do not use merge overlays or an assembler-side alternate script.

## Issues 3–8 — Editorial production drafts

Six additional sets of 25 scripts are staged under the **single** The Forces of Darkness, LLC. series. Each issue owns exactly one `strips_vNN.json` file. The assembler's shared generation line, character/settings shelves and `fod-strip` direction are reused without cloning or diverging. New scripts preserve exact three-panel `panelPlan`, mapped `dialogueInline`, visual setting, cast, summary, location continuity, and provenance.

**Origin distinctions (not an exhaustive recovery claim):** 22 scripts adapt historically recovered titles (#29–50 selected and #154, #155, #190), with newly authored plots and dialogue. The other 128 were newly written from the established fantasy-office premise/department pool because the original 275-item chat script transcript remains inaccessible. They are not falsely assigned original strip numbers. Their status is **sharpened-unapproved**. 203 historic original scripts remain unavailable; the intake ledger retains that gap.

**Issue routes:** RexPrompt → Series: `The Forces of Darkness, LLC.` → Issue: `Issue 3` through `Issue 8` → one of 25 `FOD_VNN_SXX` units → Build/Copy. The parent aggregates all eight files exclusively for portfolio inventory; the issue's nested source list assembles the selected 25 only.

**Writing/quality gate:** setup–escalation–reversal in exactly three panels, truthful evil business visible in each scene, explicit speaker ownership and panel-index correspondence, no duplicated titles or historic origin numbers, diverse locations and cast per issue (at least eight and ten respectively), original source provenance preserved, and no retroactive promotion to released canon. Known near-duplicates were corrected before registration: imprisoned mystery shopper (vs. red-team rescuers), counterfeit tribute (vs. courier addressing), better prisoner records (vs. elven humanitarian relief), and treasury biometric access (vs. dragon refusing to spend reserves).
