# Vikings 2026 — Conversational Depth Pass
Date: 2026-09-28

Status: COMPLETE

This pass is **guidance-driven, not rule-driven**.

The goal is not to make every page wordier. The goal is to include more scenes in each active issue where characters remain on one subject long enough to have an actual conversation: one answer produces another question; a claim gets tested; somebody clarifies what they meant; another person complicates the logic; the scene moves because people talked rather than because the script jumped to the next beat.

Intentionally terse pages and lines remain terse.
