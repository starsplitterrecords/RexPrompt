## Story lock
This issue is not a civic-system lesson. It shows that the Vikings have begun to live in New York when nobody is formally teaching them how.

The neighborhood remembers them through repetition: the same bodega, coffee cart, hallway, park, library, merchants, and ordinary routes. The Kin remain mostly background figures, quietly doing more independent living than Carrie notices. Gunnar must broaden beyond navigation/logistics. The library is one ordinary destination within a lived day, not the issue premise.

Do not explain the Kin payoff yet. Seed it visually. Do not turn recurring civilians into sitcom sidekicks. Keep recognition casual and earned.
