# Vikings 2026 — Issue 4: THE TALLY OF DISHONOR

Status: STRUCTURAL SEASON REVISION — dialogue intentionally not line-polished
Primary function: financial legibility, value, status and partial civic participation; the bank becomes the first act, while the rest of the issue follows what money changes inside the household and neighborhood.

## Revision intent
- Preserve the bank account, Carrie's fifty-dollar advance, debit card, promotional toaster and first-purchase destination.
- Resolve the institutional bank problem in the first third rather than keeping the cast at the teller desk for most of the issue.
- Use the account to reveal household needs, separate preferences, reciprocal debt, neighborhood relationships and the first discretionary act of care.
- Begin individualizing Astrid and the two male Kin while keeping their movement group-based at this stage.

## PAGE 1 — THE FORTY-EIGHT-HOUR WALK

**Summary:** Carrie gets Bjorn and Gunnar to the bank before the account deadline, but the real pressure is that every ordinary household need now depends on money the Vikings still cannot use.

**Setting:** Busy NYC sidewalk approaching the bank

5 panels.

1. **wide.** Carrie moves fast down a crowded sidewalk with a DTI folder; Bjorn and Gunnar keep pace.
   **CARRIE:** Forty-eight hours. No account, no rent setup. Then housing starts calling.
2. **medium.** Bjorn carries the small wrapped hacksilver pouch he still considers real money.
   **BJORN:** Then we get the account today.
3. **medium.** Gunnar checks the bank address against the route he learned in Issue 3.
   **GUNNAR:** I know the way.
4. **close.** Carrie glances at him.
   **CARRIE:** Thank you.
5. **wide.** They reach the glass-fronted bank.

## PAGE 4 — ADDRESS IS A PLACE

## PAGE 5 — WORTHY SILVER
