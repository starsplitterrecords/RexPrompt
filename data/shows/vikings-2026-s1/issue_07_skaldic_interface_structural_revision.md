## Character rules

### Gunnar
Curiosity is the engine. He is not mapping the library as a survival exercise. The catalog interests him because it lets him retrieve information again; literature interests him because he likes it.

### Bjorn
His historical corrections are concrete: equipment, grip, proportion, construction. He does not launch into a general grievance about modernity. His key realization is that a preserved written claim can exist without being treated as unquestionable truth.

### Carrie
She remains present and useful, but she is not the gatekeeper for every library interaction. Missing Gunnar's card application is one small sign of successful integration, not the later full realization that the household no longer needs her mediation.

### Silas
Reduced narrative footprint. His one scene moves him from reflexive content-making toward ordinary usefulness.

## Closing rule

The issue must end on **shared reading aloud**, not on administrative success. The library matters because it enlarges the household's available voices.
