# Vikings 2026 — Issue 6: TRIAL OF TOIL

Status: PRODUCTION SCRIPT — 24-PAGE WRITING + DRAWABLE PAGE STAGING; ACTIVE ASSEMBLER INPUT  
Primary function: Bjorn must discover whether he still has something useful to offer when the household no longer needs him to command it.

## Continuity lock

Issue 5 ended with a functioning household and neighborhood that no longer require Carrie or Bjorn to organize every ordinary need. Issue 6 makes that success emotionally difficult for Bjorn.

Exactly three Kin exist: Astrid and two men. Astrid is largely outside foreground action in Issues 6–7. Her absence is ordinary. Do not explain where she spends all of that time and do not add another Kin to make the apartment look fuller.

The support group is a catalyst, not the setting for the whole issue. The group occupies eight pages total, with every useful idea tested later in the apartment or neighborhood.

No job offer. No clinical framing. No therapist version of Bjorn. He remains a participant who argues, notices, learns slowly, and changes behavior because the distinction between command and requested responsibility makes sense to him.

## Page-image language for this issue

- Ordinary bright 2026 Bushwick realism; no grim social-services visual coding.
- The Safe Cave now contains the accumulated life established in Issue 5: books, laundry, groceries, coffee cups, brass key, small tools, mixed historical and modern household objects.
- The support group meets in a plain community-center multipurpose room: stackable chairs, folding table, coffee urn, paper cups, radiator, bulletin board, fluorescent daylight mix. No therapy-office furniture, no circle-of-trauma cliché, no inspirational posters.
- Group Facilitator remains unnamed in this development script. Grounded middle-aged peer-support presence, ordinary clothes, calm body language; not a clinician and not visually authoritative through costume.
- The recurring 1987 Participant remains unnamed. They should read as an ordinary displaced adult, not a period-costume joke. One or two personal objects may retain late-1980s origin, but their clothing can be practical contemporary thrifted layers.
- Use the two established male Kin as recurring individuals. Do not average them together and do not assign names yet.

## PAGE 4 — THE RUPTURE

**Summary:** A tiny electrical problem is solved before Bjorn can act; his violent frustration frightens the household more than the hazard did.

**Setting:** Safe Cave, kitchenette/sleeping edge.

**6 panels.**

1. **Insert.** A cheap overloaded power strip beside the table gives a sharp spark and a small puff of gray smoke. No open flame.
2. **Medium.** Gunnar immediately yanks the plug from the wall while the first male Kin lifts nearby bedding away with one motion.
   **GUNNAR:** Dead.
3. **Wide.** The danger is already over. Carrie, Bjorn, Gunnar, and the two male Kin all look at the inert strip on the floor.
4. **Medium on Bjorn.** He bends, picks up the dead strip, and stares at it. His jaw tightens.
5. **Impact panel.** Bjorn slams the plastic strip once into the bare wall. The casing cracks. Everyone else freezes. Do not make the impact spectacular; the shock is that he chose violence after the danger passed.
6. **Two-shot, Carrie and Bjorn, with the two male Kin visibly wary behind them.** Carrie speaks low and exact.
   **CARRIE:** That was not protection.
   **BJORN:** It failed this house.
   **CARRIE:** Gunnar already protected the house. You scared everybody because the problem didn't need you.

**Page turn:** Bjorn cannot dismiss the correction because the people he intended to protect are the people he frightened.

## Issue 7 handoff

Bjorn enters Issue 7 with a changed leadership grammar:
- ask what kind of help is wanted;
- allow an answer he would not choose;
- separate responsibility from ownership;
- tolerate not knowing every movement of the household;
- contribute without making himself indispensable.

Issue 7 must test all of those gains when the Status Reconciliation Review asks the household for a collective position that the household does not actually share in identical form.
