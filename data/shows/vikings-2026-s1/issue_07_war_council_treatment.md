# Vikings 2026 — Issue 7: WAR COUNCIL

Status: ACTIVE TREATMENT
Primary function: institutional review turns the household's accumulated life in New York into something they must defend together.

## Dramatic engine
A `STATUS RECONCILIATION REVIEW` notice arrives. Carrie and the Vikings turn the Bushwick kitchen table into a joint strategy council and begin translating lived belonging into evidence the municipal system can recognize.

## Established treatment anchors
- municipal envelope arrival
- `STATUS RECONCILIATION REVIEW` notice
- Bushwick apartment / kitchen-table war-map structure
- apartment hallway pressure
- Carrie sorting municipal paperwork
- Gunnar categorizing what the review actually demands
- Bjorn arranging objects and paperwork as serious council materials
- brass apartment key placed centrally as the tangible symbol of home
- smoke complaint escalation
- Silas and neighbor hallway pressure
- municipal forms treated visually as war documents
- scrap-metal / barricade logic
- dishware and cutlery arranged as tactical objects
- Carrie eventually joining the table rather than standing over the household as its manager
- Bjorn naming the meeting a war council
- Gunnar identifying the mismatch between the Vikings' lived relationships, routines, obligations, and contribution and what the municipal system knows how to count

## Opening movement
The review notice arrives and initially looks like another municipal document among many. Carrie's reaction makes clear that this one changes the stakes.

At the apartment table she starts sorting forms and requirements. Bjorn imposes physical order on the pile. Gunnar identifies categories and asks what each requirement actually proves. The brass key is placed among the paperwork, turning the table into a visual argument about what is being defended.

Hallway interruptions bring smoke, neighbor, and Silas pressure into the room. Ordinary domestic problems become potentially relevant because the household is now under review.

## Middle movement
The council broadens from paperwork to actual life.

Carrie asks questions she needs answered for the review and discovers that the household has more independent routines and neighborhood ties than she understood. The Kin's earlier errands, merchants, parks, culture, shopping, neighbors, and household responsibilities begin appearing as facts rather than background texture.

Bjorn responds to the threat with the leadership restored in Issue 6: gathering people, listening, weighing what matters, and treating each person's knowledge as part of the defense.

Gunnar's contribution is analytical but broader than navigation. He identifies that the municipal system is not directly evaluating whether they have a life here; it is evaluating proxies it knows how to record.

Carrie's procedural knowledge remains essential. The relational turn is that she eventually sits at the table and plans with them.

## Kin function
The Kin's integration is revealed in small pieces rather than a single speech:
- regular errands
- merchant relationships
- neighbor relationships
- parks and cultural routines
- household responsibilities
- recognizable places and people in the neighborhood

These facts should feel like things Carrie simply never stopped to ask about.

## Bjorn function
Bjorn is intense but not merely reactive. Issue 6 has reminded him that leadership still matters. Here he uses it under pressure.

His contribution is to gather the household, take the threat seriously, make room for useful voices, and turn diffuse anxiety into collective purpose.

## Carrie function
Carrie begins as the caseworker interpreting the notice. By the end she is still the procedural expert, but she is participating in a shared strategy rather than managing the household from outside it.

The key visual milestone is Carrie joining the table.

## Gunnar function
Gunnar helps distinguish:
- what the household actually is;
- what the review asks for;
- what kinds of evidence can connect the two.

His insight is that the danger lies in the gap between lived belonging and administratively legible belonging.

## Visual grammar
Primary space: Bushwick studio and hallway.

The apartment table functions simultaneously as:
- treaty table
- war table
- paperwork altar

Important tactile elements:
- brass key
- municipal envelopes and forms
- dishes and cutlery
- coffee
- smoke residue or ventilation problem
- scrap metal and improvised household objects

The comedy comes from Bjorn treating mundane administrative preparation with complete war-council gravity while the strategy itself remains bureaucratic, relational, and communal.

## End state
By the end of the issue:
- the review threat is understood as real;
- Carrie and the Vikings are planning together;
- neighborhood ties and Kin routines are becoming evidence;
- Bjorn is functioning as a leader in the present;
- the household understands that belonging must be demonstrated to an institution that cannot directly see lived belonging;
- Issue 8's filing, evidence, audit, and hearing-preparation escalation is set up naturally.
