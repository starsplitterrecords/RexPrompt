## Series purpose

Physics of the Lattice is the science/instruction branch of Rex Fleet Academy. Each issue begins from a practical Academy question, makes cadets operate inside the consequences of the answer, and returns to instruction with a more precise model. The comic teaches the invented physics through causal story rather than encyclopedia exposition.

The Academy cast deliberately spans pilot, navigator, technician, engineer, and instructor viewpoints so the same phenomenon is understood through motion, field relationships, instrumentation, and material load.

## Current physics boundary

Issue 1 compiles these established operating ideas into story:

- The Lattice is not presented as a separate magical dimension.
- A corridor is a geometric relationship / low-tension travel volume, not a fixed tunnel.
- Public corridors are centered on BHA beacon chains but the verified safe cross-section is broader than the centerline.
- Green means verified low tension.
- Red means unsafe or verification/sensor failure; operators do not treat an unresolved red segment as safe.
- Beacons verify the route; they do not create the underlying corridor.
- Navigators may ride an inverse-tension gradient inside the verified volume rather than rigidly following the beacon line.
- A route that is longer in ordinary coordinates can be shorter/easier in Lattice geometry.

Do not add a deeper explanation for why the Lattice exists or what ultimately caused the Sundering in Issue 1.

## Planned eight-issue teaching spine

1. **The Shortest Path** — corridor geometry, beacon volume, inverse tension, riding the inversion.
2. **Coherence** — why a route remains a single stable geometric solution and what happens when instruments begin to disagree.
3. **Tides** — slow changes in the local Lattice field; navigation through moving geometry.
4. **Echoes** — gravitic / signal persistence and the physical basis of Memory Wells.
5. **Drift** — evolving phase relationships, rendezvous failure, and why correct clocks can still lead to wrong meetings.
6. **The Mirrorfold** — multiple valid propagation paths, duplicated perception, and navigation without trusting sight.
7. **Fracture** — matter coupling to strained geometry; Fracture Blooms as physical records of field state.
8. **The Sundering** — students apply the full course backward to the historical collapse. Mechanism may be increasingly understood; ultimate trigger remains unresolved.

Only Issue 1 is currently compiled into production recipes. Later issue titles are development architecture, not active page packages.

## Visual language

Academy/Fleet identity uses blue, white, and restrained gold. Verified public corridor volumes and beacons use green. Unsafe or unresolved verification uses red. Lattice physics should be shown through field relationships, star displacement, propagation paths, ship motion, structural response, and controlled instrumentation—not generic magical energy, fantasy portals, or infographic textbook pages.
