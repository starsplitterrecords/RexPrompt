## Critical story facts

- Venn has been removed from ship command. She has conditional, reviewed civilian contact and administrative appeals, not a secret Fleet chain of command.
- Deka commands Thunderbreak through Issues 4–6 and remains the ship's commanding officer after Venn receives a narrow temporary task-group coordination appointment for Issue 7.
- The junior escort independently leaves formation during the Tal Corvus rescue; Deka independently covers the gap. Venn never orders either choice.
- The seven Shard captains each choose to open the Gate blockade. Venn's subsequent meeting with Abby addresses attribution and safety; it does not cause their choice.
- The public timing data came from Jex and the civilian flight network. The prospective Fleet technical-method review must not be confused with route timing, command authority or real-time rescue.
- All nine new Venn scenes are editorial proposals that must be reconciled before promotion into the exact page recipes. Nothing here has been committed to GitHub or approved for visual production.
