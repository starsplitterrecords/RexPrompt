# REX FLEET — EXPANDED DRAMATIC SCRIPT
## Source Issue 06 — Two Windows

**Development draft v1. RF_I06_P01–P16. The Issue 6 command correction governs: Tess loses Gate-line authority, not command of Gravefire. Eight atlas scenes.**

### SCENE 1 — TRADE'S PRICE (P01)

*SHARDFALL ARENA VAULT. The Trade Mask sits before a captain whose ship has been held in the picket. The captain's crew has medical patients at home and relatives waiting on a grain shipment.*

**TRADE MASK:** Gate manifests. Independent inspection. Then the cargo moves.

**CAPTAIN:** It's grain. It's been grain since it left the field. You can count it at the destination.

**TRADE MASK:** If we cannot verify the shipment, the Shards cannot guarantee their share.

**CAPTAIN:** My crew's families are Shards. We haven't seen our share either. You'll have to explain to them why a load meant for Auric can't be allowed through while they're starving on this side.

**TRADE MASK:** You confuse immediate discomfort with a lasting political advantage.

**CAPTAIN:** My youngest can't tell the difference. She keeps asking what we're having for supper.**TRADE MASK:** An effective negotiating position must last longer than a single meal.

**CAPTAIN:** I don't want a negotiating position. I want to know what to tell a child who thinks her father can bring a ship full of food home because he has the ship.

*Trade turns to an aide who has spent the hearing looking at a separate list.*

**TRADE MASK:** The captain's berth is under our registry. His crew will receive an allocation after the inspection is complete.

**CAPTAIN:** How many inspections have to be complete before the people in the warren stop begging me to get their grain through?

**TRADE MASK:** You speak as if these measures are for my personal satisfaction.

**CAPTAIN:** I'm trying very hard not to say what I think they're for.

*The aide notes the exchange without expression. The captain leaves with a suspended permit. In the outer hall, two workers ask whether his convoy will return tonight. He tells them it will not. One curses him. He takes the abuse because he knows they will have less food than he does.*

*Trade's emissary removes the captain's transit permit. The political argument is over; hunger isn't.*

### SCENE 2 — THIRTY MINUTES (P02–P03)

*AURIC GOVERNOR CHAMBER. Two measurable Gate windows are available tonight, thirty minutes apart. Shard pickets physically obstruct both. Halev, Naomi and Jex have to choose what travels first.*

**NAOMI:** Two openings. The first is narrower, but the timing is stable. The second is thirty minutes later. Nothing about either one guarantees the ships beyond the picket will behave.

**HALEV:** Food first. Medicine through the second.

**JEX:** People will accuse you of sending grain while patients wait.

**HALEV:** They may. The first freighters have enough thermal margin to hold the narrow approach. The medical craft don't. We lose both loads if we make them trade places.

**NAOMI:** That is an actual reason. You should give that to the people who will complain.

**HALEV:** I intend to.

**JEX:** Publish both times?

**NAOMI:** Publish verified updates, not the next guess someone sells to a desperate captain.

**JEX:** You really are enjoying this.

**NAOMI:** More than you think.

*Jex opens the updates. Naomi walks out to Sunlash, leaving Halev with the medical-transport families who have just heard they're second.*

### SCENE 3 — PICKETS WITHOUT NAMES (P04)

*THUNDERBREAK COMMAND. The Shard pickets remain exactly where they were, but their transponders disappear.*

**TACTICAL:** All the Shard identifiers went silent.

**VENN:** Positions?

**TACTICAL:** Unchanged. They're still there.

**VENN:** Then nobody fires at an empty patch of space because the name beside it went away. Mark their measured positions. Tell Gravefire where our cover begins and ends.

**TACTICAL:** It's unusual to coordinate with a vessel our own reports still call hostile.

**VENN:** The patients don't benefit from the distinction. Our weapons remain governed by Fleet orders. Our charts need to show what's actually present.

*Her limited commission does not change who commands Thunderbreak. Deka acknowledges the tactical exchange from her captain's station.*

### SCENE 4 — TESS'S ORDER (P05–P07)

*ARENA VAULT. Abby stands beside Tess when Trade insists on inspection control over the second medical convoy. The drugs are destined partly for Shard patients. Raid's mask turns toward Tess.*

**TRADE MASK:** No manifest, no crossing.

**TESS:** The doctors have listed the patients. You want names of the people escorting them and every household they're carrying medicine to. You don't need that to know whether the hold contains antibiotics.

**RAID MASK:** Gravefire will enforce the medical blockade.

**TESS:** No.

*The word lands without theatrics. Abby looks at her sister, who doesn't look back.*

**RAID MASK:** Relinquish your Gate-line posting.

**TESS:** I'll surrender the posting. I'm taking Gravefire with me.

**RAID MASK:** Your ship is not exempt from Confederacy obligations.

**TESS:** Neither is my crew exempt from the obligations I have to them. They follow my command aboard Gravefire. You can appoint another Gate-line captain. You can't make this one pretend the patients aren't ours.

*Outside the chamber Abby catches Tess.*

**ABBY:** You could have warned me before you did that.

**TESS:** I thought the look on my face was plenty of warning.

**ABBY:** We need seven captains to hold their position for half an hour. They can lose their berths if they do it.

**TESS:** You can't make the decision for them.

**ABBY:** I know.

**TESS:** Then ask. And let them say no without calling them cowards. I'll take the medical skiffs. You're better at making people listen. I was never any good at it.

**ABBY:** You just told the Triarch no to their faces.

**TESS:** They were listening. I wouldn't call it a friendly conversation.*Abby studies her sister's face. Tess has the flushed look she used to get after a fight at home, when her first impulse was to tell everyone she'd enjoyed it.*

**ABBY:** When you refused Raid, I thought he was going to remove your whole crew.

**TESS:** He may yet try. For now I've still got Gravefire. Her captain's quarters are a mess. I'd hate to make anybody else clean them.

**ABBY:** They're going to make you pay for it.

**TESS:** I know. I would have preferred to keep the Gate line. The crew was good at that work. The young ones were finally beginning to stop treating a passing freighter like it was a prize in a contest.

**ABBY:** Then why didn't you wait and challenge the order through the captains?

**TESS:** Because the second window is tonight, and when I looked at the medical manifest I recognized two names. One of them repaired my flight suit after the strike at Black Drift. She wouldn't let me pay. She said she'd take it out of my hide the next time I damaged it.

*Abby tries not to smile.*

**TESS:** If I obeyed that order, she might never get the chance. I didn't have time to organize a respectable political position about it.

**ABBY:** You could have told me that inside.

**TESS:** There are things I don't say in rooms full of masks. You know that about me.

*Abby knows. She gives Tess a quick hug that is half restraint and half apology. Tess allows it for a moment, then reminds her there are ships waiting.*

*Abby reaches to straighten a damaged cuff on Tess's flight suit. Tess pulls away, then lets her finish.*

### SCENE 5 — THE FIRST PASSAGE (P08–P10)

*SUNLASH HANGAR AND AURIC GATE. Naomi chooses the exposed lead route to keep two food freighters clear of the worst shear. The windows are physical, measured openings, not magical gates.*

**PILOT:** Could we wait for the second opening? It looks wider.

**NAOMI:** That one belongs to the medics. We're taking the one we can safely reach.

**PILOT:** Safely?

**NAOMI:** More safely than trying to steal somebody else's thirty minutes.

*She leads. One freighter clips an anchor marker in slow approach; its mast buckles without destroying the ship.*

**SECOND:** Freighter Two took the marker. Mast's damaged.

**NAOMI:** Keep them on course. They can replace a mast after docking. They can't replace the ship if they panic and chase the marker back into the field.

*The food ships arrive. At Auric, the damaged mast delays unloading. Halev moves the crews to the undamaged ship first instead of pretending all freight is instantly available.*

### SCENE 6 — SEVEN REQUESTS (P11–P12)

*From Shardfall, Trade learns its loyalist replacement force cannot arrive before the second window. Abby reaches seven captains with a message each can refuse.*

**ABBY:** I'm asking you to rotate your weapons away from the medical lane. I am not asking for your ships. Keep your crews. Keep your stations. When the window closes, you'll answer to your own people for what you did.

**CAPTAIN ONE:** Raid will freeze my berth.

**ABBY:** Probably.

**CAPTAIN TWO:** You can't give me another.

**ABBY:** No.

**CAPTAIN THREE:** If we're fired on, do you guarantee we'll survive?

**ABBY:** I can guarantee you'll know where the medics are. Anything beyond that would be a lie.

*She hears the long silences after sending the instructions. Some captains answer cautiously. Others say nothing until the deadline. Her officer offers to send the messages as orders.*

**ABBY:** No. Leave them requests. If I need obedience to make this work, it's already the wrong kind of victory.*She closes the collective channel and begins calling the undecided captains individually. It would be easier to claim authority she doesn't have, or to promise a berth she cannot give. One captain's reply arrives in the voice of a woman whose family has already lost its repair bay.*

**CAPTAIN FOUR:** When you say you'll remember us, what exactly will you do with the memory? My crew can't patch a hull with your good opinion.

**ABBY:** I can give you priority for Scythe's outer repair rail, for as long as I control it. That's the only berth I can actually promise.

**CAPTAIN FOUR:** And if Raid takes Scythe's berths too?

**ABBY:** Then we'll be looking for docking space together.

*The captain curses softly, mostly at herself.*

**CAPTAIN FOUR:** I will hold the lane. I won't sign on to whatever you're planning after tonight.

**ABBY:** I haven't asked you to.

*Another captain declines. Abby marks the refusal accurately and doesn't send a second appeal. Her officer looks surprised.*

**OFFICER:** We could tell him the other six have agreed. He might change his mind.

**ABBY:** The other six haven't all agreed. Don't build a new authority on the first convenient lie you can tell.

*By the second window, the seven commitments she does have are real. So are the people who have chosen not to join her.*

### SCENE 7 — THE SECOND WINDOW (P13–P15)

*Gate's second opening. Seven independent captains each rotate their guns away at their own times. A loyalist patrol moves to stop the medical convoy. Billie, low on fuel and with no assurance of supply, puts Ashfang between the loyalist and Gravefire's skiffs.*

**OFFICER:** You don't have enough reserves for another engagement.

**BILLIE:** I'm not planning to make one. They're going through.

**LOYALIST CAPTAIN:** Ashfang, get out of the line.

**BILLIE:** You can see who's behind me. You fire, everybody sees which side of my ship you wanted to hit.

*The loyalist holds fire. Tess guides the skiffs with Gravefire; cooling levels continue to constrain her maneuvers.*

**TESS:** Sunlash, the inner mouth is open. Medical One is clear. Medical Two, keep your nose where I can see it.

*The convoy passes. On Thunderbreak, a junior officer celebrates Fleet's accomplishment.*

**TACTICAL:** Fleet secured both windows.

**VENN:** Fleet secured its own lane. Those seven captains decided not to fire. Our report needs to say that.

**DEKA:** And Ashfang kept the loyalist from taking the shot.

**TACTICAL:** She's the one who destroyed our relief crates last crossing.

**DEKA:** I know. I don't intend to send her a thank-you bouquet. Put the actual maneuver in the log.

### SCENE 8 — THE WRONG KIND OF QUIET (P16)

*TAL CORVUS SERVICE WARRENS. Tess walks the reopened flow path with a technician. A physical seep along the structural seam behaves differently from normal condensation; the measurements are unsettled. She touches nothing until the technician checks pressure.*

**TECHNICIAN:** It wasn't here yesterday.

**TESS:** Yesterday we were busy trying to persuade the same people not to shoot us. Take a sample before it disappears.

**TECHNICIAN:** You think it's dangerous?

**TESS:** I don't know what it is. I'm not going to improve it by naming it a curse.*The technician sets the sample into a secured measurement sleeve. A thin line of displaced condensation makes it seem as if the seam has briefly acquired a second edge. Tess waits for the instrument reading to settle.*

**TECHNICIAN:** It doesn't behave like a leak. Pressure's normal. The temperature of the second return is almost identical to the first.

**TESS:** How close is almost?

**TECHNICIAN:** Too close to explain it with the camera angle.

**TESS:** Good. Keep the original readings, including the ones that look boring. I want Jia to see what the instruments actually recorded, not what we think they're supposed to mean.

*She leaves the technician with the work. On her way out she passes families carrying food through the service warren. One thanks her for keeping the medical route open. Tess starts to dismiss it, then thinks of the two names she recognized in the manifest and lets the thanks stand.*

*Elsewhere Fear's emissary presents a network of named civilian couriers who spread actual route updates.*

**FEAR MASK:** We have found the people who make the information move. Now learn why the others trust them.

*The unknown seep remains measured but unexplained. The political campaign against witnesses is considerably easier to understand.*
