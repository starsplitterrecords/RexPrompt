# REX FLEET — ISSUE 07
## Previously in Rex Fleet
### Seven Decisions

At Auric Gate, the Silent Triarch attempted to stop civilian traffic by placing Shard pickets across the available crossing windows. Auric had supplies waiting on the other side and two brief openings, half an hour apart. Naomi Sol led the exposed food convoy through the first. Tessa Banks brought medical freight toward the second.

The blockade did not end because one commander won a battle. Abby Saville sent a dangerous message to captains whose crews had their own loyalties and families. Seven of them independently turned their weapons away from the civilian lane. Billie Rusk used Ashfang to shield Gravefire from a loyalist attack without joining Abby's rebellion. Deka held Thunderbreak's defensive overlap and refused to let Fleet claim credit for the captains' decisions. Food came through with damage to one freighter; the medical crossing survived without a firefight.

Those decisions have consequences inside the Shards, where obedience and standing cannot be replaced by a single public statement. Around Tal Corvus, crews have also observed an unfamiliar physical disturbance in the built environment, with no reliable explanation for it yet.

Venn's influence beyond the chain of command has grown, but she has not recovered Thunderbreak. The coming problems will test whether cooperation can be built around evidence instead of authority.

For the first time in a while, the danger ahead may not be an enemy ship.
