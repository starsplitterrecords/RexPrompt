# REX FLEET — ISSUE 10
## Previously in Rex Fleet
### What the Ledger Cost

Someone tried to kill Jex Marrin while Veil Market was already close to breaking under real shortages. The attempt helped provoke panic, a fire, and a struggle over stored food. Jex survived, injured, and opened reserves that had once been part of his private commercial advantage. When the fire threatened the passage used by rescuers, he surrendered more access and allowed workers to cut through goods he had expected to sell.

That decision saved routes for food and medicine. It also left honest buyers without purchases they had already paid for. Jex's records began linking the attacker, paid agitators, and diverted cargo, but those connections do not mean all of Veil Market's hunger was manufactured. Naomi Sol followed the evidence without confusing sabotage with scarcity itself. Venn and Sera Dain helped obtain medical access without handing Fleet an invitation to occupy the market.

Jex has released enough of his transactions for others to verify the pattern while holding back private refuge routes that might still matter to his missing sister, Tali. Copies are beyond his control now.

What happens next will depend on what those payments purchased—and on the people who have to live with the answer.
