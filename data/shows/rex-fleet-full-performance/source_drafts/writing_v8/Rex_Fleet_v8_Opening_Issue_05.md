# REX FLEET — ISSUE 05
## Previously in Rex Fleet
### After the Third Window

Tal Corvus's evacuation passed through a corridor that everyone knew would be contested. The plan nearly failed when armed ships pressed the departure lane and two civilian tugs drifted out of safe formation.

Tessa Banks held Gravefire at the departure mouth, damaging the ship's already vulnerable cooling systems while guiding the final craft through. Naomi Sol used warning from Jex Marrin to keep another hostile screen from isolating the convoy. Oren Pike abandoned his carefully staged position long enough to recover a stranded tug. A junior Fleet escort officer left formation without permission to reach another; Thunderbreak's commander, Erin Deka, chose to cover the gap that officer opened. Those were two decisions by two different officers, and Fleet is keeping a record of both.

The families reached safety. Their arrival did not replenish Auric's stores, repair Gravefire, or settle the competing explanations each faction offered for the rescue. Nor did it erase the pressure on Billie Rusk, whose refusal to destroy independent Shard food production has complicated her position with the Triarch.

Off her former bridge, Ella Venn has begun discovering that an old acquaintance may answer her calls without being free to act on them. She cannot relieve Deka of responsibility, and she cannot promise Auric help the Fleet has not authorized.

The rescue brought people through. Feeding them will require another crossing.
