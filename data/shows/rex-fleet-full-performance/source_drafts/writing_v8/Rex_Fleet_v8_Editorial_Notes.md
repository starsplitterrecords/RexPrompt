# Rex Fleet — Editorial Notes for Season One Writing v8

**Scope:** Beginning matter only. V7's twelve full dramatic scripts remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Writing remains the Library authority; the separate RexPrompt adaptation is a derived visual-implementation track, not the source of truth for prose.

## Editorial design

- **Issue 1:** A 734-word, scene-setting essay introduces the Shattering, reciprocal-field corridors, Fleet/Core, Verge and Shards, the cost of lost connectivity, and the stakes of reopening routes. It stops at a Luminara departure without discussing the raid or later outcomes.
- **Issues 2–12:** Eleven narrative prose recaps, 180–244 words each. Each selects earlier events that matter to the next issue; there is no full previous-issue synopsis, no script dialogue masquerading as prose, and no information taken from the issue about to begin. Some closings are intentional forward-looking questions, not factual revelations.
- **End matter:** All twelve original editorial end-matter source entries, including Issue 1 and Issue 12 closing letters, are copied verbatim with original CAPTION/DISPLAY roles, original line sequence, and checksum comparison against RexPrompt. These are carry-forward texts, not newly revised writing.
- **Remaining opening matter:** The twelve *cast-directory pages* are not rewritten here. Their visual/headshot production is outside the prose-writing task.
- **Other closing matter:** The preexisting 'NEXT ISSUE' editorial-closing teasers in Issues 2–11 are referenced by source ID and retained unchanged for the visual tool; they are not rewritten or folded into the new prose recaps.

## Continuity guards

- Venn has lost Thunderbreak command after Issue 2, and Deka commands that ship in Issues 4–6. When Venn later gains narrow task-group coordination authority, Deka remains ship captain.
- The junior Fleet escort breaks formation at Tal Corvus; Deka chooses whether to protect the opened gap. Neither decision is secretly Venn's.
- Seven independent Shard captains decide for themselves to open the Gate blockade. Venn's behind-the-scenes relationships do not cause their decisions.
- During the Auric Gate battle, stopping synchronized salvos and the later offensive battery stand-down are distinct. The second is an actual maneuver by Deka after Venn's task-group request.
- Abby is missing, not confirmed dead, after Shatterforge; she returns with Scythe in Issue 11, not before.
- Jex and Tali's fates and knowledge are never conflated. The Triarch's responsibility for Shatterforge sabotage is disclosed at the appropriate point, not in an earlier recap.

## Items requiring editorial approval before page implementation

1. **Issue 4 original end-matter preview**: “The food arrives short, the routes are exposed, and Billie’s desperate attack has put the next relief run in danger.” The *relief-freighter attack* takes place in Issue 5, so the old preview appears prematurely retrospective. The line is preserved EXACTLY in the unchanged source copy, but should be reviewed for a future targeted correction.
2. **Issue 12 original closing editorial**: “Making these twelve issues” and “closes the first major Rex Fleet story” reflect the old twelve-issue allocation. If the v7 story later expands beyond twelve published issues, that copy will need an approved adjustment. The present v8 does not change it.
3. **Cast directory continuity**: Original Issue 4–6 cast lists omit Venn and several participants from the nine added Venn scenes. The cast directories will need an up-to-date attribution audit when publication pages are adapted. No visual catalog alterations are made here.
4. **Opening prose pagination**: Issue 1's longer introduction may deserve more than one editorial page. Issue length and placement should be decided when the final writing is adapted; do not shrink this draft merely to fill a single originally scheduled splash page.
5. **End-matter/teaser separation**: 'From the Editor' pages are archived in full here; the ten separate original 'Next Issue' teasers are source-ID carry-forwards and should be copied exactly by the production assembler when retained.
