## Previously in Rex Fleet
### The Last Skiff

The emergency at Shatterforge began with equipment that should have protected its people. Industrial service vents were deliberately locked, forcing families toward departures that could be controlled. Tess Banks reached the manual relief system. Abby Saville brought Scythe of Saville over the exhaust, using her ship's shields to keep the discharge away from occupied terraces while the evacuation proceeded.

The last skiffs took longer than the shield system could safely promise. Venn, directing Fleet resources, chose to protect the final civilian craft rather than draw that protection toward Abby's increasingly exposed ship. Scythe stayed over the vent. After the last craft cleared, the ship vanished behind the furnace bloom, its fate unconfirmed. Tess got the rescued people docked before turning to the question she could hardly bear to ask.

Abby had secured authentic evidence of the deliberate vent lock before contact was lost. Jex made the payment trail public, including his own part in the network that carried it. Billie refused orders to obstruct independent departures. Pike's attempt to control the image of events left Broken Oath damaged.

Shatterforge's families reached shelter. The evidence reached beyond it. Abby and her ship remain unaccounted for.

At Auric Gate, the next confrontation is already gathering ships.
