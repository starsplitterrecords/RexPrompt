# REX FLEET — ISSUE 06
## Previously in Rex Fleet
### The Public Clock

In Veil Market, people have been learning what a route is worth when a ration door is shut. Jex Marrin, who once earned his living selling privileged crossing information, opened a verified public timing bulletin so civilian pilots could find the next safe passage. It helped freighters move. It also gave raiders the same information.

Billie Rusk, short of fuel and supply after defying the Triarch, attacked a Verge relief shipment. Twenty-eight of the promised hundred crates were lost. Seventy-two reached Auric, where the arithmetic became smaller portions on actual plates. Gravefire, still damaged from Tal Corvus, placed itself between Billie's guns and the freighter. Billie withdrew rather than fire through Tess Banks's ship.

Deka kept Thunderbreak's limited defensive protection available without taking command of the Shard escort. Abby restricted the intelligence shared with Billie's crew, though she could not issue orders to that independent captain. The convoy that followed showed how much work cooperation required even when nobody fired.

Meanwhile, copies of Jex's route information have begun moving through working crews faster than any single authority can suppress them. The Triarch has noticed. So has Venn, who is learning to secure voluntary technical help rather than issue orders she no longer has the right to give.

The next crossings will carry both food and medicine. Someone has decided they should not be allowed through.
