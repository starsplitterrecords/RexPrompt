# REX FLEET — ISSUE 04
## Previously in Rex Fleet
### An Open Road, an Unfinished Reckoning

Auric has survived an attack on its residential ring, but the city has not escaped the questions that brought the attack to its doors. The rooms are still crowded. Heat and medicine remain scarce. Families who lived there before the refugee arrivals have claims as real as the claims of those who fled.

Dr. Richard Secundo has made public the original records from Tal Corvus. Some of the shortages imposed on Shard districts were not mechanical failures or unavoidable consequences of isolation: supplies were deliberately diverted after political protests. The evidence spread despite an attempt to silence him. Its publication has strained the authority of the Silent Triarch and divided people who once fought alongside one another.

Abby Saville and six independent Shard captains helped Naomi Sol defend civilian transports. Billie Rusk's attack failed, but the damage to her friendships and to Auric remains. Jex Marrin helped open hidden approaches for evacuees; his sister Tali has since disappeared from contact, and he does not know whether she escaped.

Fleet's tribunal has removed Commodore Ella Venn from command. Deka holds Thunderbreak. Venn still has her experience, her reputation, and relationships she cannot use as orders. Auric has chosen to offer refugees a chance to remain under its own civilian authority.

On Tal Corvus, other families are preparing to leave. They have a departure window. Whether anyone can protect it is another matter.
