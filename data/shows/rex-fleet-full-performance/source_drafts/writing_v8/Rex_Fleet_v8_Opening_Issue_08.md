## Previously in Rex Fleet
### The Trouble with a Perfect Reading

The Calm Corridor appeared stable by every ordinary measurement. Then a survey ship saw a second image of itself, following its actual maneuver after a measurable delay. The anomaly was real enough to endanger navigation, but neither the instruments nor the physicists could yet explain it completely.

Jia Morgan found that the delayed returns grew more dangerous when ships fired their maneuvering systems in unison. Formation guidance began treating the echoes as real hulls while actual civilian vessels lost safe separation. Naomi Sol broke the synchronized pattern with staggered manual instructions. Tess Banks kept Gravefire and its passengers on the real course rather than chasing an image of her own ship. The convoy came through because different captains trusted a shared measurement without making identical movements.

The Fear Mask used frightening recordings to pronounce certainty where researchers could only report observations. The attempt to turn uncertainty into obedience met witnesses who had seen the crossing for themselves.

But the Shards have other ways of contesting authority. Abby Saville's independent captains have already challenged what the Triarch can demand of them, and Tess's standing carries weight beyond her own vessel. Their problems at home have not disappeared while the corridor has been misbehaving.
