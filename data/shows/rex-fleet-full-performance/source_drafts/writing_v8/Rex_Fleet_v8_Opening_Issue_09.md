# REX FLEET — ISSUE 09
## Previously in Rex Fleet
### What a Victory Doesn't Feed

At Shardfall, Oren Pike challenged Tessa Banks before witnesses, turning the shortages and unfinished repair debts of the Shards into an argument about who deserved to lead. Pike proved that he could fight. Tess won, with a real injury to her left forearm, and then refused to kill him. The outcome changed what the witnesses were willing to say in public. It did not change how much food was available.

Two more Shard captains withdrew from the Raid Mask's muster. The Triarch responded by freezing their berths, placing costs on crews who had made their own political choices. Abby Saville must live with the consequences of offering those captains an alternative she cannot guarantee will be easy.

While the challenge drew attention, Naomi Sol found a safer way to move an overcrowded ration line through Veil Market. Jex Marrin and the market workers are still dealing with diverted supplies, incomplete deliveries, and the difference between food that exists on a ledger and food a family can actually reach.

A watched duel can end in an instant. A hungry market cannot.
