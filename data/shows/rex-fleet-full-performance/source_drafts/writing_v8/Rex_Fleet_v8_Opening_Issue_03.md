# REX FLEET — ISSUE 03
## Previously in Rex Fleet
### What the Rescue Left Behind

The evacuation freighter Mercy of Dawn came to Auric under attack, carrying Verge and Shard families who needed a safe berth more urgently than they needed permission to approach one. Commodore Ella Venn abandoned her assigned supply screen to answer their distress. The decision saved people and put Auric's heating and medical supplies at risk. She later refused a Fleet detention order that would have separated patients from their caregivers.

The rescue was hardly the work of one uniform. Naomi Sol led civilian contact. Richard Secundo and his son Paul kept people alive aboard a ship coming apart. Tessa Banks helped protect passengers from raiders wearing her own faction's colors. Abby Saville opposed Billie's use of civilians as leverage, and independent Shard captains began making different choices about whom they would follow.

Some freight reached Auric. Some was lost or damaged. Every survivor needed somewhere to sleep. Governor Halev opened disputed rooms in the Crown Ring, but those apartments still had owners, the building still had faults, and the city's heaters still had limits.

Venn has handed Thunderbreak to Lieutenant Erin Deka and must answer to the Admiralty. Auric has only a provisional civilian rescue arrangement, not a settlement between the powers.

A ship has been saved. The people aboard it still need a home.
