# REX FLEET — ISSUE 12
## Previously in Rex Fleet
### A Road Still Open

Auric Gate was carrying civilians before the fleets began fighting over it. Fleet, Verge, and independent Shard ships faced one another across a passage none could afford to lose. Pike sought a battle the cameras would remember. Venn declined a shot that would have put civilians behind the target at risk. Naomi, Halev, and Tess kept traffic moving even as the formation broke apart.

Then the Gate's physical readings began to echo the battle. Jia Morgan found that synchronized weapons fire was coupling to the same anomalous returns previously measured in the Calm Corridor. The harder the ships fought in step, the less reliable their picture of the real traffic became. Stopping synchronized volleys was a technical necessity, not a sudden end to the fighting.

Abby Saville returned aboard a damaged Scythe with survivors and captains who had chosen her. She directed them toward the civilian lane. After Verge escorts relinquished their offensive locks, Venn asked Thunderbreak to power down its offensive batteries and cover civilians. Deka, still commanding the cruiser and responsible for its exposed flank, made the ship-handling decision. Other captains chose their own restraint. Billie Rusk prevented Pike from turning the remaining confusion into a massacre.

The firing stopped. The echoes diminished. The road remained open, scarred and difficult, for the ships that had waited through the battle.

No faction surrendered its principles, command, or claim to have acted correctly. The next civilian convoy still needs an agreed time to cross.
