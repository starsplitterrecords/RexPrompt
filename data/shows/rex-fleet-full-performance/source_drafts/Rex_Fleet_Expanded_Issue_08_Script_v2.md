# REX FLEET — FULL PERFORMANCE DEVELOPMENT SCRIPT
## Source Issue 08 — Witnesses at Shardfall

**Revision v2 — expanded dramatic scenes corresponding to RF_I08_P01–P12.** Source events, command authority, the two departing captains, Gravefire's damage, and Tess's left-forearm injury are preserved. New emotional and staging details require editorial approval before live-script integration.

### SCENE 1 — THE REPAIR BILL (P01)

*SHARDFALL. The captains' chamber is filling for an ordinary dispute about repair allocations. The witnesses expected an accounting argument. Oren Pike has brought the accounting. Two pressure plates lie on the table, one bent from load and one new enough to show where it came from.*

**PIKE:** Do you know how long it takes to seal a berth after its pressure plate fails?

**TESS:** Depends on whether your crew uses the repair schedule or tries to finish it with a wrench and a prayer.

**PIKE:** Two nights. My crew slept in suits. We sent our replacement plates to Auric because you said the shelter roof would go before morning. The roof is still there. Our berth is leaking.

**TESS:** I heard.

**PIKE:** You heard. You didn't come.

*The accusation catches her off balance. Abby, standing to one side, watches the two captains rather than the gathering spectators.*

**TESS:** I sent a team.

**PIKE:** A mechanic with one borrowed welder. The repair lasted until the first pressure cycle. I don't think that's a team.

**TESS:** No. It's not.

**PIKE:** You gave away supplies I earned, then sent me an apology that couldn't hold air. I want that entered into the record.

**ABBY:** Then enter it. The plate transfer was recorded. Nobody's asking your crew to pretend the loss didn't happen.

**PIKE:** I want her standing reviewed, not just the invoice.

**ABBY:** Because she made a decision you disagreed with?

**PIKE:** Because she decided how much of my crew's safety she was entitled to spend. If that's what leadership means now, I want to know when we voted for it.

*Tess has been looking at the warped plate. She notices a repaired edge the witness hasn't mentioned.*

**TESS:** You patched it twice.

**PIKE:** Of course we did. What did you think we'd do, go to sleep and let the berth empty?

**TESS:** I thought you'd get the replacement before it reached that point.

**PIKE:** That's the thing about knowing everyone else's needs. Sometimes you miss the ones under your nose.

*She nods. It's more painful than the public challenge, because he is right about that much.*

**TESS:** The Auric roof would have collapsed. I won't apologize for keeping those people breathing.

**PIKE:** I'm not asking you to. I'm asking whether you'd have done it if your crew had to sleep in pressure suits instead of mine.

*She opens her mouth, then shuts it. Pike sees the hesitation and makes his formal challenge before the witnesses. Abby starts forward, but Tess raises a hand: the challenge is lawful, and she will answer it herself.*

### SCENE 2 — A FIGHT PEOPLE CAN AFFORD TO WATCH (P02–P03)

*VEIL MARKET. Naomi and Jex arrive to find six ration pallets missing from the distribution inventory. A crowd watches Shardfall's announcement on a secondary screen. Several people are shouting about which captain deserves to win. The queue has not moved in twenty minutes.*

**JEX:** The greatest trick in politics. Offer a fight to a hungry audience and they forget they haven't eaten.

**NAOMI:** I don't think they've forgotten. The woman at the front has been asking for her allotment since I got here.

*She goes to the distribution counter. The attendant is distracted by the duel broadcast.*

**NAOMI:** Six pallets diverted. Who authorized it?

**ATTENDANT:** I have the release marks. One signature is from yesterday. One isn't in the roster anymore.

**NAOMI:** Then why were the pallets allowed out?

**ATTENDANT:** Because people shouted at me from both ends of the hall and I had to decide whether the children at the door got fed before I found a supervisor.

*Naomi is about to argue. Then she sees the tired attendant's shaking hands.*

**NAOMI:** All right. Show me the release marks. We'll find which pallet can come back today. The children aren't waiting for us to decide whose fault this was.

**JEX:** I'll trace the buyers. If the pallets went to a legitimate shelter, we don't seize them back and call it accounting.

**NAOMI:** Agreed. I'm glad we established that before somebody tried to improve the problem with a weapon.

*THUNDERBREAK. Venn and Deka hear the duel mentioned during a route briefing. An officer wants to reposition Fleet vessels while the captains are occupied.*

**OFFICER:** We can move the patrol behind Broken Oath while the arena holds everyone's attention.

**DEKA:** You'd put an armed cruiser behind a Shard vessel while its captain is on a duel floor? I'm struggling to see how that could be misunderstood.

**OFFICER:** We would gain a defensible approach.

**VENN:** We would also turn a political challenge into an excuse for Fleet maneuvering. What transit operation requires that approach?

**OFFICER:** None at present.

**VENN:** Then no. What does require our attention?

**DEKA:** An unarmed repair tug needs a safe crossing, and the same dispute that's keeping their captain in Shardfall means no one has cleared the berth allocation. I'd be glad to spend our time on something with a real arrival time.

*Venn agrees. The officer returns to his chart, annoyed but not humiliated. No one has to pretend the patrol has no strategic value; they have decided not to use it here.*

### SCENE 3 — BEFORE THE FLOOR FILLS (P04)

*Tess enters the duel chamber before the witnesses return. Pike is inspecting his weapon and adjusting a grip. She carries the damaged fitting from Gravefire's radiator, a part her engineers have been unable to replace. She lays it beside his plates.*

**PIKE:** I told them not to let you bring anything onto the floor.

**TESS:** If I meant to kill you with a radiator fitting, I wouldn't advertise it.

**PIKE:** It's a theatrical gesture.

**TESS:** Coming from you, that's a remarkably sensitive accusation.

*He does not smile. She sees it and becomes serious.*

**TESS:** We lost the cooling margin that would have let Gravefire take another paid run. My crew is eating from repaired stores. I was wrong not to ask what giving up those pressure plates would mean for your people. I wasn't the only one who paid.

**PIKE:** The difference is that you decided to pay. My crew didn't.

**TESS:** You're right.

**PIKE:** And if I'd refused them?

**TESS:** I might have taken them anyway.

**PIKE:** There. That's the answer I came for.

**TESS:** It shouldn't be enough. Not for a captain. What happened to those families under that roof mattered. What happened to your crew matters. You can demand an accounting for both.

**PIKE:** You talk as though there's some perfect answer that would keep everyone alive and every ship repaired.

**TESS:** No. I've been trying to tell you there isn't.

*He looks at the two pieces of damaged metal on the table.*

**PIKE:** I'm a good captain, Tess.

**TESS:** I know that.

**PIKE:** People talk about me as though I pose for a camera while other people work. My crew survived a failed supply season because I kept that ship running when the Triarch couldn't be bothered to send us anything. The men and women in that berth trust me. I don't want to be the captain who lets people take their safety while I smile and accept a compliment for being generous.

*For the first time she hears more than the political demand. She puts her fitting back in her pocket.*

**TESS:** Then let's not make them watch us pretend this is a simple question.

**PIKE:** They're already watching.

*The witnesses return. Pike readies his weapon. Tess removes her outer jacket and hands it to the attendant with unusual care; the garment is repaired in too many places to be replaced easily.*

### SCENE 4 — THE OTHER DOOR (P05)

*At Veil Market, a crush has formed at the only open ration door. The missing six pallets have meant smaller portions, and people have decided the only way to get enough is to reach the counter first. An elderly man has fallen against the shutter.*

**WORKER:** I told them to form a line. They're not listening.

**NAOMI:** Because a line doesn't guarantee food. Open the other service door.

**WORKER:** The mechanism is jammed. The lock hasn't been touched since the last ration change.

**NAOMI:** Does it open from the inside?

**WORKER:** Yes, if you can get the pressure catch loose.

**NAOMI:** Then get me two people who know the mechanism and half my unloading crew. The freighters can wait.

**JEX:** If you divert that many crew, the second relief load doesn't finish unloading until morning.

**NAOMI:** Then it doesn't. That man is going to be crushed long before morning.

*Jex hesitates, then joins the workers rather than continuing to argue. The catch gives way with a metallic screech. The second door opens onto a narrow corridor that must be cleared before people can move. Naomi refuses to let the crowd rush it.*

**NAOMI:** You at the front, yes, you. I need you to take the next ten people slowly. If you run, the door closes again. Everybody behind them waits until they have space.

**WOMAN:** And if we get there and there's nothing left?

*Naomi cannot promise the inventory she doesn't have.*

**NAOMI:** There may not be enough for full allotments tonight. But there's an open door, and no one gets any food if we trample each other. Can you help me get the first ten through?

*The woman takes a breath, takes the elderly man's arm, and begins moving the group. The crowd is angry. It does not become grateful all at once. But the crush begins to loosen.*

### SCENE 5 — A YIELD IS NOT A FORGIVENESS (P06–P08)

*SHARDFALL DUEL FLOOR. Pike fights like a veteran. His first exchanges are cautious, probing Tess's damaged balance and her tendency to favor her stronger side. Tess makes the mistake of assuming his carefully staged public posture means he is less dangerous than he is. He cuts her left forearm.*

*The cut is clean and bad enough to make her fingers hesitate when she tries to tighten her grip. No one cheers. Abby involuntarily steps forward before catching herself; she has to watch the lawful challenge as a sister, not interfere as a captain.*

**PIKE:** You should have listened when I asked for those plates.

**TESS:** I'm listening now. I'd be grateful if you stopped talking while I do it.

*Pike comes in again. The cut forces her to change her guard. She recognizes that he expects an immediate counter from her injured side, and she lets him believe he's created an opening. When he commits his weight too far, she pivots, catches his wrist with her good arm and forces the weapon down. It skitters across the floor. Tess holds his arm until the witness declares the lawful yield.*

**WITNESS:** Captain Pike yields. The challenge is concluded.

*Pike looks at her, breathless and furious. Tess's sleeve is darkening. Several spectators wait for a final punishment or a political statement.*

**PIKE:** You could finish it. No one here would stop you.

**TESS:** I don't want to kill you.

**PIKE:** You think that makes you better than me?

**TESS:** No. I think it means my sister can stop making that face and somebody can put a bandage on my arm.

*Abby realizes she has been grimacing. She doesn't apologize. Tess steps away and accepts the bandage, allowing the medic to examine the cut rather than waving away the risk.*

**PIKE:** My crew still has a leaking berth.

**TESS:** I know.

**PIKE:** Winning this doesn't fix it.

**TESS:** I know that too.

*He retrieves his weapon. The attendants expect him to leave alone; instead he bends and collects the warped pressure plate he brought for the hearing.*

**PIKE:** I'll submit the replacement request again. In the morning.

**TESS:** You should. I'll confirm the original transfer was my decision.

*He leaves without thanks. His witnesses record the result. Tess is still bleeding. Abby comes closer, carrying her jacket.*

**ABBY:** You could have told me how much that cut hurt.

**TESS:** I was trying not to encourage him.

**ABBY:** I'm serious.

**TESS:** So am I. Give me the sleeve. No, not that way, you'll pull it over the wound.

*Abby helps without speaking for a moment. Tess allows the help because it is her sister's hand and because her own fingers are becoming clumsy.*

### SCENE 6 — TWO EMPTY BERTHS (P09–P11)

*Veil Market is still hungry when the duel's result reaches it. At Shardfall, two captains who had hesitated now refuse Raid's muster. They do not announce loyalty to Abby. One speaks of the medical convoys; the other of the way refusal was punished. Neither is certain where their crews will sleep.*

**RAID MASK:** Confederacy berths are assigned for Confederacy service. Your vessels have declined that service.

**FIRST CAPTAIN:** We still have repair commitments and crew aboard. Those berths were paid for.

**RAID MASK:** The berths are frozen pending review.

**SECOND CAPTAIN:** Your review will outlast our stores.

**RAID MASK:** You made your choice.

*The first captain looks toward Abby, who has been standing at the back.*

**FIRST CAPTAIN:** Don't tell them we belong to you now. We don't.

**ABBY:** I wasn't going to.

**FIRST CAPTAIN:** Everybody's going to say that's why we refused. That we've signed up to take your orders.

**ABBY:** I can't stop what they'll say. I can ask where you need to put your ships.

**SECOND CAPTAIN:** We're already doing the arithmetic. There isn't space for both. Even if someone takes us, the crews will need to work off the fuel and docking charges.

**ABBY:** Then show me the figures. I'll ask the captains who have space, and I'll tell you who can actually take you rather than make promises to both.

*On Thunderbreak, an officer proposes offering Fleet berths contingent on enlistment under the Fleet defensive chain.*

**OFFICER:** They'll accept our protection. We could make it conditional on bringing them under Fleet command.

**DEKA:** It would be easy, while they're desperate.

**VENN:** Yes. That's the trouble. Offer safe transit on request. Not a commission. Not a contract to surrender their ships.

**OFFICER:** We may never have leverage this good again.

*Venn considers the word rather than merely dismissing the officer.*

**VENN:** You're probably right. We should be careful what we become when we discover we have it.

**DEKA:** I'll log safe passage for any ship that requests it. No change to command. Their hulls stay theirs.

*The officer leaves to amend the routing order. Venn watches Deka issue it herself.*

**VENN:** You didn't need me for that.

**DEKA:** No. You were the useful witness.

**VENN:** I'll try to remain useful.

*Abby receives the Fleet route offer and forwards the exact terms to the two captains. Neither replies immediately. She cannot find them berths by producing good intentions, and the problem remains unsolved at the end of the scene.*

### SCENE 7 — THE WRONG DIRECTION (P12)

*The following day, Gravefire reaches Tal Corvus. Tess's forearm is bandaged and stiff. She has been ordered not to put weight on it, a restriction she treats with imperfect courtesy. A child by a service corridor is following a route arrow that leads to a closed door.*

**CHILD:** It was open yesterday.

**TESS:** A lot of things were open yesterday.

*The child notices the bandage and looks worried.*

**TESS:** I'm not going to bleed on your floor. That was my sister's concern too.

**CHILD:** Did the other captain hurt you?

**TESS:** Yes. I hurt him as well. Neither of us was improved by it.

*The child studies the arrow.*

**CHILD:** Then why did you do it?

*Tess reaches for a simple answer and finds none she wants to give a child.*

**TESS:** We disagreed about something that mattered to both of us. We picked a bad way of proving which of us could win a fight. It didn't settle the thing we disagreed about.

**CHILD:** That's stupid.

**TESS:** I'm finding it difficult to argue with you.

*She starts to adjust the arrow, catches herself using the injured arm, and winces. The child holds the bracket while she points to the correct passage.*

**TESS:** That way is open. Can you show the next person before they get to this door?

**CHILD:** Yes. Are you going to be all right?

**TESS:** I have a ship with a cracked radiator, a sister who's furious with me and an arm that needs a week to heal. So perhaps not all at once. But I'll be fine for a walk to the next corner.

*The child walks alongside Tess until the correct doorway comes into view. The market has not been fed, the captains have not found new berths, and the duel has settled nothing except its own result.*

**Continuity review:** Pike has a legitimate repair loss and remains a capable captain. Tess wins the lawful challenge without killing him and retains a left-forearm wound that persists. No new faction loyalties are inferred from the duel. The two captains lose berths and must find actual replacements. Naomi diverts half her own unloading crew, imposing delays rather than conjuring more labor. Venn offers passage, not coercive enlistment.
