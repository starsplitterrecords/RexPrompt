## Writing rule

Each scene gets a dramatic performance pass before comic-page pagination: distinct voices, someone wanting something, personal history, misjudgments, humor or silence when earned, concrete decisions and relationships that change. A scene may require one panel or seven pages. No mandatory monologue and no manufactured final aphorism.

The page-plan is a map, not the manuscript. Any scene dialoguer must also inspect full dialogue and panel plans in the actual source recipes and relevant overrides. For Issues 4–12, preserve strong full-performance draft dialogue as dramatic source rather than using abbreviated integration dialogue.

## Publication boundaries

Retain 12 issues as source references only. Expanded scenes can redistribute into a greater issue count following full script and pacing review; no predicted issue count is treated as canon.
