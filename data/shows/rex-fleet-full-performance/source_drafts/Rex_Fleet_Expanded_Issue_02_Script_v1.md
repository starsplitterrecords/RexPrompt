# REX FLEET — EXPANDED DRAMATIC SCRIPT
## Source Issue 02: Mercy of Dawn

**Development draft v1 · Source pages P01–P19 · 13 scene groups · Not canon or production-approved.**

A scene's dialogue is written to perform before it is allocated to comic panels. This preserves the actual story: an unscheduled mixed Verge/Shard refugee freighter is endangered during an attack meant to divert Thunderbreak away from Auric's supply convoy; Venn chooses the people and accepts discipline; Naomi leads passenger contact; Paul and Richard occupy separate physical sections after the split; Shard captains break politically; some supplies are recovered and others damaged; Auric remains precarious; Deka receives Thunderbreak only after Venn's safe handover.

### SCENE 1 — NO ROOM AT THE BERTH (RF_I02_P01)

*AURIC BAZAAR — CIVIC DOCK, NIGHT*

*The warming room Halev opened is occupied. The family from Luminara is sleeping against one wall. The boy is awake and looks past the mother toward the only heater that seems to be winning against the room. In the civic dock office, Wallace has been called out of a meeting by a new alarm.*

**BOY:** You're cold too.

**MOTHER:** I'm a grown woman. I know how to be cold quietly. Come closer to the heater.

*He moves, but not far. A new group has arrived and he sees one of them eyeing the same warm space.*

**BOY:** We could move our bags.

**MOTHER:** We could. Ask that woman if she'd like the end of the bench.

*At the dock desk Wallace arrives with three residents who have been arguing over a heated berth they've paid to keep closed for maintenance. Jex is on a communication line with a pilot. Halev looks at a distress feed.*

**WALLACE:** Nine thousand residents depend on this berth's heating circuit. It wasn't closed for decoration.

**RESIDENT:** We voted to keep the gate sealed until they could isolate the fault. My parents live on that side of the ring.

**HALEV:** I know what you voted for. What happened at the approach?

**JEX:** An unscheduled freighter is coming in with the relief convoy. Somebody's shooting at it. They've got a couple hundred people aboard, maybe more.

**WALLACE:** We do not have a couple hundred safe beds.

**JEX:** No. I imagine that's why they didn't call ahead for a reservation.

**HALEV:** Do we know who they are?

**JEX:** Verge families. Shard families. I don't know who's shooting. They're not sending a passenger list while someone cuts into the hull.

*The resident studies the feed. Her anger has not vanished.*

**RESIDENT:** If you open the gate, our circuit goes down again.

**HALEV:** It might. We'll move the sleeping families off that corridor first. I'll put the repair team at the breaker.

**RESIDENT:** And if my parents lose heat?

**HALEV:** Then they come here, and you ask me why I didn't give them a better answer. I'm not going to tell you this is without cost.

**WALLACE:** The traders will withdraw their support.

**HALEV:** They'll get their chance to tell me so. Open the berth.

*Outside, dockhands begin moving sleeping mats before the doors even finish cycling. The boy helps his mother fold a blanket for the newly arriving families. He isn't heroic. The work is simply something he can do.*

### SCENE 2 — TWO MEN, ONE SHIP (RF_I02_P02)

*MERCY OF DAWN — PASSENGER HOLD / EMPTY BRIDGE*

*The freighter is cramped, crowded, and practical. Richard Secundo is kneeling beside an adult holding a pressure panel with both hands. His nineteen-year-old son Paul works through the ship's physical service link on a repair slate. The empty bridge is still drawing power and atmosphere the passengers need.*

**RICHARD:** The latch won't hold by itself. Brace it there. No, not with your wrist. Use the bar. You won't have a wrist tomorrow.

**CAREGIVER:** There's a child on the other side who can't fit her mask.

*Richard looks at the caregiver's own dwindling air reserve.*

**RICHARD:** You need yours.

**CAREGIVER:** She needs hers more.

*The caregiver moves before he can object. Richard watches the exchange, then calls to Paul.*

**RICHARD:** How long have we got?

**PAUL:** In this section? Not long if the bridge keeps drinking the reserve. The pumps are feeding empty rooms.

**RICHARD:** Are you certain it's empty?

**PAUL:** Cameras and pressure sensors. I've checked every bridge compartment twice.

**RICHARD:** Then shut the bridge down.

**PAUL:** I'll lose half our instruments. And the helm station.

**RICHARD:** Which half do you need?

**PAUL:** I don't know yet.

**RICHARD:** Then work it out while we're breathing. Take the controls onto the slate.

*Paul begins. His father's confidence is genuine, but it is also asking a lot of a nineteen-year-old.*

**PAUL:** You keep saying that as if it's one switch.

**RICHARD:** It usually isn't. If I could do it, I'd be on your side of the room.

**PAUL:** You'd make it worse.

**RICHARD:** Almost certainly.

*The bridge starts going cold. The passenger section's pressure slows its decline. Something mechanical begins cutting through the outer skin.*

**PAUL:** Pressure's holding. But Dad—there's another noise. Something outside is making a hole.

**RICHARD:** Then stay on the link. I'll take the breach.

**PAUL:** You can't fix a boarding cutter with a medical kit.

**RICHARD:** No. But there'll be patients when it gets through.

*They look at each other across the crowd. Then each goes where the other cannot.*

### SCENE 3 — BILLIE'S PROPOSAL (RF_I02_P03)

*ASHFANG MARAUDER — BOARDING GALLERY / SHARD CAPTAIN CHANNEL*

*Billie's crew is armed for a cargo seizure. The freighter ahead is not the cargo vessel they'd agreed to approach. Abby is on an independent captain's channel; Tess is physically moving toward Gravefire's deployment link.*

**ABBY:** That's a passenger ship. We agreed on cargo.

**BILLIE:** Thunderbreak won't move for cargo. It will move for people.

**TESS:** Billie, there are Shard families aboard. I know some of the names.

**BILLIE:** I know. And I know what Fleet let happen to ours. We're going to make Venn choose with everybody watching.

**ABBY:** You don't know what she'll choose.

**BILLIE:** Then this should be educational.

*One of Billie's boarders shifts uneasily.*

**RAIDER:** The collar's on their passenger side. I can hear people through the skin.

**TESS:** Break off before you open it.

**BILLIE:** The cargo will be ours once Thunderbreak leaves the screen. We're not here to shoot children.

**ABBY:** You've put children where you intend to threaten people. Do you not hear yourself?

**BILLIE:** I hear you defending a Fleet commodore you've never met.

**ABBY:** I hear you doing something we didn't agree to. Change course.

*Billie studies the two ship vectors. The opening she wants is almost within reach. She doesn't have the distance or the grace to admit she may have gone too far.*

**BILLIE:** Lock us on. Nobody leaves until Fleet answers.

**TESS:** You want Venn's attention? You've got mine. I'm bringing Gravefire to the breach.

**BILLIE:** Tess, don't start.

**TESS:** You started. And you're going to finish it without me on your hull.

*The channel cuts. Billie is furious. Abby, on her own ship, orders her captains to hold civilian-clear vectors. Some comply. Billie still has ships willing to follow her.*

### SCENE 4 — WHO GETS TO DECIDE (RF_I02_P04–P05)

*THUNDERBREAK COMMAND / NAOMI'S ESCORT / RESCUE ASSEMBLY*

*Auric's medicine and heating convoy needs protection. A different ship is transmitting live passenger distress. Dominion's authenticated order is already on the bridge. Venn's officers don't need to be taught what the competing duties mean.*

**DOMINION (comms):** Your assignment is the supply screen. Remain there.

**VENN:** We have civilian registration data and a live distress transmission from Mercy of Dawn.

**DOMINION:** And Shard docking codes. This could be a boarding trap. You are not authorized to expose Auric's essential cargo.

**TACTICAL:** Turning now puts the medicine and heater freight outside our effective protection.

**OFFICER:** Dominion's order is on the command log.

**VENN:** So is the distress call.

*The officer glances at her. He knows what she is about to do, and what it will cost her.*

**DOMINION:** Do not turn Thunderbreak.

**VENN:** Helm, take us to Mercy of Dawn. I'll answer for the diversion.

*The turn begins. Venn speaks directly to Auric rather than waiting for a more comfortable phrasing.*

**VENN:** Auric, your convoy screen is gone. Pull what you can toward the port. We've got civilians in a damaged hull.

*Naomi answers from her compact Verge escort.*

**NAOMI:** We have a shuttle small enough for their service collar. My Second can keep the escort alongside while I board.

**VENN:** Fleet medics can launch with you. I'll have a security detail—

**NAOMI:** Not with rifles at the front. Those people have already watched strangers cut through their hull. They'll think you're another boarding party.

**VENN:** My officer has the engineering plan.

**NAOMI:** Then let the officer handle the engineering. I'm the one who has to get through the passenger door without starting a panic.

*Venn looks at the feeds. She's angry about the loss of speed, but Naomi is right.*

**VENN:** Sol leads passenger contact. Fleet engineers follow. Rifles lowered. Officer, you're responsible for the structural approach.

**OFFICER:** Understood.

**NAOMI:** I'll call when we have pressure.

**VENN:** Call sooner if you don't.

*Naomi gives a short, involuntary laugh at an instruction so obviously impossible. She launches anyway. Venn remains responsible for a rescue she has chosen not to control personally.*

### SCENE 5 — THE PERSON YOU CAN CARRY (RF_I02_P06–P07)

*MERCY OF DAWN — FRACTURED PASSENGER HOLD / SERVICE SPINE HATCH*

*Only one rescue shuttle can load before the structure shifts again. The caregiver has given part of her air to a child; now both are too weak to stand. Naomi has to decide who takes the last seats. Richard knows exactly what will happen if the patients are separated carelessly.*

**RICHARD:** We can fit the children and a caregiver, or some of the wounded. We cannot fit both groups intact.

**NAOMI:** Children first. We'll keep the rest breathing until we're back.

**CHILD:** Not without her.

*The child points at the Shard caregiver.*

**CHILD:** She gave me her air.

**RICHARD:** She can't reach the shuttle alone.

**NAOMI:** Then put her in my restraint. Carry her across.

**JUNIOR RESCUER:** That's your seat.

**NAOMI:** It was. Fly the children. Don't make me argue with you about mathematics while the door is open.

*The caregiver tries to protest. Naomi is already helping secure the harness. A nervous boy from another household helps keep the strap straight. The shuttle departs with the children and the caregiver. Naomi stays aboard with Richard and the adults.*

*Across the compartment, Paul has discovered that the service-spine joint is failing. His control link runs through it.*

**PAUL:** Dad, the ship's opening at the joint. If the spine separates, I need to be on that side or I lose the helm.

**RICHARD:** Can you make it?

**PAUL:** If I go now.

**RICHARD:** Clip your suit line. Tell me the instant you seal behind you.

**PAUL:** I know the procedure.

**RICHARD:** I know you do.

*That lands more deeply than an order. Paul goes.*

*At the far cargo breach, a Shard raider is pulling medicine cases from a rack. Tess arrives, already exasperated by a fight she didn't want.*

**TESS:** Leave the medicine.

**RAIDER:** Billie's order is to strip this hold.

**RICHARD:** Those cases are keeping patients breathing.

**TESS:** Then they're staying. Move.

**RAIDER:** You don't command this team.

**TESS:** You're right. I command the ship that can leave you here, and it's getting very difficult to remember why I shouldn't.

*The raider hesitates. Naomi appears with a tool and sees a Shard officer facing another armed Shard.*

**NAOMI:** Hands where I can see them.

**TESS:** Yours too, if they're free. I need you to hold that pressure plate.

**NAOMI:** I'm not taking orders from a raider.

**TESS:** Lovely. We can die arguing over job titles. Or we can hold this plate shut.

*Naomi moves to the plate. Air stops whistling through the bend. Tess's expression changes: the repair work is real, and Naomi knows what she's doing.*

**NAOMI:** Richard, get them behind the inner door.

*Paul reaches the spine access but cannot turn the distorted wheel.*

**PAUL:** The hatch is bent. My slate won't fit through this gap.

**TESS:** I'll clear it. Don't pull on the linkage, you'll shear the pins.

**PAUL:** You sound like you've done this before.

**TESS:** Too often. And usually on ships belonging to Billie.

*Tess frees the hatch. Paul squeezes through with his controls. The seal closes behind him. When Billie calls over the captain channel, Tess is at the far side of the rescue, not aboard Ashfang.*

**BILLIE (comms):** You handed our supplies to Fleet.

**TESS:** I handed their medicine back. You can argue about it with me when the air stops leaking.

*Tess withdraws to Gravefire, while Naomi remains with Richard's passengers. No relationship has been repaired, but the immediate work holds.*

### SCENE 6 — NO SINGLE WAY THROUGH (RF_I02_P08–P10)

*MERCY OF DAWN / THUNDERBREAK / AURIC CIVIC CONTROL / VEIL MARKET — INTERCUT AS ONE RESCUE*

*The ship's structure finally fails. The passenger hull and supply spine separate. Paul is in the spine with the repair slate; Richard and Naomi are with the passengers. A single tractor hold from Thunderbreak can stabilize only one section at a time.*

**TACTICAL:** Joint's gone. Tractor can hold either the passenger hull or the cargo spine.

**NAOMI (comms):** Three hundred people on this side. Pressure falling.

**PAUL (comms):** I'm sealed in the spine. Helm still answers. Some of it.

**RICHARD:** Paul, I can't see you.

**PAUL:** I can't see you either. I'm told it's very impressive out your window.

**RICHARD:** This is not when you need to be funny.

**PAUL:** When would you prefer?

*Richard almost answers, then the line rattles with an impact.*

**HALEV (comms):** The supply spine carries heat and antibiotics for Auric. If we lose it, I have clinics rationing by morning.

**VENN:** I know. Our tractor can't hold both.

*Venn looks at the passenger hull. There is no second tractor and no clever trick that conjures one.*

**VENN:** Hold the passengers. Keep Gravefire's approach open.

**HALEV:** Understood. I'll protect the reserves we still have. Tell me honestly what reaches us.

*At Veil Market, Jex finds Halev's feed already waiting. The broker has a private route geometry the three factions cannot otherwise share in time.*

**HALEV:** The spine still has a chance. You've used those bends before. We need the fixes.

**JEX:** Not on a public band. I built those routes for people who didn't want to be found.

**HALEV:** Can you send them to Gravefire directly?

**JEX:** Their relay's gone. Half the ships out there don't hold the same private key. They've got a moving spine, no time, and not even a common map.

**SCRIBE:** If you give them only the first turn, they'll hit the shear at the second.

**JEX:** I know. That's why it isn't public.

**HALEV:** Three safe houses use those approaches.

**JEX:** Three that I know about. There may be people who copied old routes because they thought nobody else would see them.

**HALEV:** I won't order you to expose them.

**JEX:** You don't have to. If that young man has no vector, he'll drift through a wreckage field until someone can write his name on a report.

*Jex studies the route token in his hand. It is valuable for business and for people whose lives depend on concealment. He sends the complete verified timing and anchor corrections over the emergency band.*

**JEX:** Open all the fixes. Every anchor. Every timing window. Send word to the shelters that the route's burned.

**SCRIBE:** Both rescue vessels have it.

**JEX:** Good. I am going to need to make several very unpleasant calls.

*In the Shatterwake, Naomi watches the newly shared geometry appear on a functional instrument. There is no mystical passage; the safe approach is narrow and measured.*

**NAOMI:** Paul, nose toward the blue fracture. The slower edge, not the moving bright return.

**PAUL:** The blue edge keeps moving.

**TESS (comms, from Gravefire):** Relative to you. Hold your orientation, I'll bring the reference around.

**RICHARD:** Paul, eyes on the slate.

**PAUL:** I can't see your section.

**RICHARD:** I'm here. You keep flying.

*Thunderbreak's enormous hull cannot follow the same tight line.*

**VENN:** We won't fit through. Taking the outside course.

**NAOMI:** Then go around. We can't wait for you.

**VENN:** Already turning.

*Gravefire closes against the drifting supply spine. Tess uses a controlled mechanical nudge rather than an impossible invisible force.*

**TESS:** Spine's slipping. I'll push from the side.

**NAOMI:** Your ship's already in pieces from the last raid.

**TESS:** In very specific pieces. I know which ones can take another hit. Get Paul through.

*The spine moves enough for Paul to catch the next verified vector. Nothing is secure yet.*

### SCENE 7 — PICK A FORMATION (RF_I02_P11–P12)

*SHATTERWAKE MOUTH / SHARD SKIFF CHANNELS / THUNDERBREAK COMMAND*

*Billie catches the supply spine with a physical grapple. Paul is inside; the grapple pulls him off the vector Tess fought to give him. Abby sees the alignment and calls Billie.*

**ABBY:** Release the spine. There's a man inside it.

**BILLIE:** Tess comes aboard Ashfang. Then I let the cargo go.

**TESS:** You've already cut a passenger hull. I won't send Paul into another one.

**BILLIE:** You're making your choice public, Tess. You know that?

**TESS:** You've made nearly everything public today. I thought that was what you wanted.

*Billie is furious, mostly because Tess sounds disappointed rather than afraid.*

**BILLIE:** We were supposed to make Fleet answer for abandoning us. Now you're saving its cargo.

**TESS:** There's a boy in that cargo. He happens to be nineteen, and he hasn't asked which side he belongs to before he tries to keep the engine running.

**ABBY:** Billie, listen. You have not put Venn where you think you have. You've put every Shard captain on that channel where they have to decide whether we threaten a boy to make a point.

**BILLIE:** Every captain can hear you defending Fleet.

**ABBY:** They can hear me defending him. And they can hear you call that a crime.

*The silence lasts long enough for ships to move. A few skiffs pull toward Abby's civilian-clear line; others stay with Billie. None has been taken over by another captain.*

**BILLIE:** Last chance. Move your skiffs.

**ABBY:** Cut the grapple or I'll cut it for you.

*Abby targets the mechanical line, not the vulnerable cargo. Its release damages Ashfang's assembly and sends the spine rolling.*

**TESS:** Paul, hold the attitude jets steady. I've got your side.

**PAUL:** Holding. I'm very interested in the part where we stop being attached to people who want to kill me.

**TESS:** Everyone aboard a ship eventually is. You are learning faster than most.

*Billie's crew recovers its grapple and retreats with damage. She cannot bring herself to order fire through Tess. Abby's choices have protected civilians and split the raiding confederation.*

*Thunderbreak is approaching on the longer outside course. Billie's skiffs cross one firing solution as the tractor holds the passenger hull.*

**OFFICER:** Ashfang in range. Clean shot for a moment.

**TACTICAL:** Strike power would drop the tractor. The passengers go into debris.

**VENN:** Keep the tractor. Defensive drones only.

**OFFICER:** We could end the attack.

**VENN:** We could kill the people we're trying to rescue. No.

*The ship shudders.*

**TACTICAL:** Incoming on our patched port armor.

**VENN:** Brace. Secure the bridge and keep pulling.

*Gravefire steadies the spine. Cargo is damaged but not entirely lost. The passenger hull reaches safety. Nobody's agreement has been restored, and the freighter's damage remains.*

### SCENE 8 — THE OTHER BLANKET (RF_I02_P13)

*AURIC BAZAAR — ARRIVALS CONCOURSE*

*Passengers emerge under medical supervision. The partial supply shipment comes in damaged. A civilian resident is trying to keep a newly erected faction barrier in place; an exhausted Shard caregiver from the first shuttle is still wearing the restraint that saved her life.*

**RESIDENT:** Shard arrivals stay behind the red barrier. That's what we were told.

**RICHARD:** We're keeping care groups together until the medics have everyone assessed. A man with a lung injury doesn't become easier to treat because you make him cross the room.

*The child the caregiver saved runs toward her. The caregiver tries to kneel and nearly collapses. The mother from Luminara catches her at the elbow.*

**CHILD:** She gave me her air.

**MOTHER:** Is anyone waiting for you?

**CAREGIVER:** My sister was on the other shuttle. I can't find her.

**MOTHER:** Use half our berth until you do. It's not generous. The room is too small and the heater makes an awful noise.

**BOY:** We've got another blanket. It's mine, but you can have it.

**MOTHER:** You were complaining that it's too scratchy.

**BOY:** She can complain about it too.

*The caregiver laughs once, which hurts. She accepts the blanket.*

**WALLACE:** We cannot fit everybody in those rooms.

**HALEV:** No, we cannot. Take down the unnecessary partitions. Medical triage by need. Housing assignments after.

**RESIDENT:** You keep using words like temporary. When do the people who already live here get their space back?

**HALEV:** I have to answer that too. I can't answer it by separating a child from the woman who kept her alive.

*The barrier comes down. Some residents help. Others stand aside, unconvinced, and their resentment doesn't evaporate into a neat lesson.*

### SCENE 9 — WHAT GOES INTO THE LOG (RF_I02_P14)

*THUNDERBREAK — DAMAGED BRIDGE*

*The rescued ships are moving toward a safe berth. Thunderbreak's tractor bus is overheated and the old port bow carries fresh damage over its earlier scrape. Dominion and Keating are on an authenticated Fleet link.*

**DOMINION:** Hold Shard evacuees in Fleet custody. Request separate detention of the others at Auric.

**VENN:** Auric is a civilian port. There are mixed households among our patients, including people who helped the evacuation.

**KEATING:** A security classification is not a penalty.

**VENN:** Separating patients from their carers will make it one. Some of the people you're classifying kept the children alive.

**DOMINION:** We cannot ignore infiltration merely because a rescue was successful.

**VENN:** We don't have to ignore it. We can question people who are safe and medically able to answer. We can investigate the raiders without turning the passengers into prisoners.

**DOMINION:** Your ship was diverted against orders. Supply cargo was damaged. We have now had two incidents involving unauthorized Fleet decisions and Shard movement. I am not going to let a third become an administrative surprise.

**VENN:** And I won't convert the civilian rescue into a detention operation.

*The ship's officer is working at an adjacent station, doing his best not to watch. He cannot remain silent when Dominion implies the rescue crews were negligent.*

**OFFICER:** Those were Naomi's rescue personnel, Admiral. They brought the children out.

**KEATING:** Officer, that's not your jurisdiction.

*Venn looks toward him—not reproachful, but a clear instruction to let her carry the consequence.*

**DOMINION:** Comply, and Thunderbreak remains under your command.

**VENN:** I refuse the detention order. Record it without qualification.

**DOMINION:** Lieutenant Erin Deka will assume command as soon as the ship is safely berthed. Until then you're still responsible for her.

**VENN:** Then I'll bring her to a safe berth and hand her over intact.

*When the channel closes, the officer looks at Venn.*

**OFFICER:** You could have let me answer the first one.

**VENN:** No. I couldn't.

**OFFICER:** The ship has taken worse than this.

**VENN:** I know. Let me know when the tractor bus has stopped arguing with engineering.

*They return to work. Her command is ending, but not yet over.*

### SCENE 10 — OTHER PEOPLE'S ROOMS (RF_I02_P15)

*AURIC BAZAAR — CROWN RING*

*The Crown Ring is luminous, expensive and not empty. Some rooms belong to residents who are away; their belongings remain carefully stored. A monitored structural fracture has closed the outer gallery. Halev, Wallace, Richard and Paul inspect the safe inner section with the ring's own engineering staff.*

**WALLACE:** These aren't abandoned apartments. Half the owners financed the work that made the ring possible. And the outer gallery is fractured.

**HALEV:** How much can we isolate from the glass?

**PAUL:** These monitors still respond. But I'd want a second physical watch on the crack. I wouldn't trust the sensors alone.

**RICHARD:** The patients can't keep moving between rooms. Some will not survive a second relocation tonight.

**WALLACE:** The traders will argue that the cure is worse than the problem.

**RICHARD:** They're welcome to come and examine the problem. They can start by carrying a stretcher.

*Wallace starts to object, sees the exhausted staff, and decides against it.*

**HALEV:** Inner rooms only. Every assignment recorded. The property claims remain intact. Find the owners and send them a notice they can actually answer.

**WALLACE:** The traders have already called a council vote.

**HALEV:** Good. They can tell us what their alternative is. Meanwhile, repair crew at the isolation break, and two watches on the fracture. If it moves, everyone leaves.

*Paul touches a cold frame and withdraws his hand.*

**PAUL:** We may need to shift the heat load again.

**WALLACE:** There isn't another spare circuit.

**PAUL:** I said we may need to. I didn't say there was a cheerful answer.

*Halev directs the first families into the secured inner area. A resident arriving with a key looks at them, at the door to her own apartment, and says nothing. The silence is not consent.*

### SCENE 11 — BORROWED HELP (RF_I02_P16–P17)

*AURIC SERVICE AIRLOCK / CIVIC CHAMBER — CONTINUOUS POLITICAL AFTERMATH*

*Naomi has finished a shift long enough to change the way she walks. Venn is still on Thunderbreak's side of the airlock for the last working handover. Neither has had the luxury of being simply pleased about the rescue.*

**NAOMI:** You saved the passengers. We got some of the heaters back. Medicine too, enough to start an argument over who gets it first. My people still have to keep the ring warm.

**VENN:** I should have had a way to recover more of the spine.

**NAOMI:** You didn't. Don't turn it into a private apology. Tell me what your crews can leave us when you go.

**VENN:** The emergency rescue stores are allocated for civilian use while this order stands. Your people can use them. Charts and engineering equipment, not tactical systems.

**NAOMI:** And when Dominion changes his mind?

**VENN:** My replacement can recall the Fleet personnel. I cannot promise relief beyond my authority.

**NAOMI:** I don't want a Fleet base in exchange for a loan we didn't ask to need.

**VENN:** You won't get one from me. Verge remains responsible for civilian work.

**NAOMI:** Then put the limit in writing. Halev needs to know precisely what disappears when they take you away.

*Venn takes a moment. She knows Naomi has said 'they' rather than 'if'.*

**VENN:** I'll write what I can authorize.

**NAOMI:** That's useful. Not enough, but useful.

*Elsewhere in the civic chamber, a resident holds an ownership claim while a Shard caregiver and her now-found sister wait by an open doorway. Wallace is giving Halev bad political news.*

**WALLACE:** The traders have withdrawn their votes. The bonded stores stay locked.

**RESIDENT:** My parents' furniture is in that apartment. The books, their things. You can call it emergency use. I still want to know when they can go home.

**HALEV:** Their claim stands. Nobody has transferred ownership.

**CAREGIVER:** My sister and I were told we'd get one night. If the vote closes the rooms, where do we go?

**RESIDENT:** You can use our kitchen. It's heated. The bedroom is still my parents'.

*The offer is not a political conversion. It is the limit of what this resident can bear to see happen tonight.*

**HALEV:** We'll find a place for both households. I can't promise it tonight.

**WALLACE:** We have recovered heaters for days, not a season. No replacement shipment confirmed. And you no longer have the votes to continue this order.

**HALEV:** Then publish the actual count. The order expires unless the council renews it. I'll answer them before it does.

*The resident finally looks at Halev rather than the apartment door.*

**RESIDENT:** You had better.

**HALEV:** Yes.

*Nothing in the room becomes simpler.*

### SCENE 12 — THE HANDOVER (RF_I02_P18)

*THUNDERBREAK — SECURED AURIC BERTH*

*Thunderbreak is berthed at last. The tractor bus is still hot; port armor shows the new damage over the Kaldr scrape. Erin Deka arrives with the authenticated relief order. Venn is completing a ship condition report rather than a farewell ceremony.*

**DEKA:** The relief order is authenticated. Engineering has the inspection. I have the bridge when you're ready.

**VENN:** Port armor's compromised. Tractor bus is running hot. Two watches need replacing. You will find a stubborn valve that our crew has been threatening since the last refit.

**DEKA:** Which valve?

**VENN:** You'll know it when you hear it.

*Deka checks the engineering report.*

**DEKA:** You'd have made a better impression if you'd left me a whole bow.

**VENN:** I considered it. The raiders were inconsiderate about the approach.

**DEKA:** I'll submit the repair orders. I don't intend to use the damage as an excuse to ignore the next convoy.

**VENN:** Nor should you. The ship's yours. My refusal and the rescue are both in the command log.

**DEKA:** I can read.

**VENN:** A comfort to hear from my successor.

*For a beat Deka's composure softens. She understands how much of Venn's life has been invested in this bridge.*

**DEKA:** I'll take care of her.

**VENN:** I know.

*The command changes hands with no invented ceremony. In the civic control room, a separate agreement is taking shape; it cannot be signed on behalf of Fleet by a relieved captain.*

**NAOMI:** Auric directs civilian rescue. Verge escorts lead in Auric space. Shared emergency calls, access to the docks, review in thirty days.
