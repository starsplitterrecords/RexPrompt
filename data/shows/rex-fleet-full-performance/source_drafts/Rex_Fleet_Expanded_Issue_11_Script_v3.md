# REX FLEET — EXPANDED DRAMATIC SCRIPT
## Source Issue 11 — The Gate Holds

**Development draft v1. RF_I11_P01–P22. The Issue 11 battle revision governs: P13 stops synchronized volleys; P17 limits the later battery stand-down to Thunderbreak, under Deka's ship command.**

### SCENE 1 — EVERYONE HAS ALREADY COMMITTED (P01–P02)

*AURIC GATE — DAWN. The Gate is an industrial route with maintenance craft, freight, survey markers and families already inside the certified volume. A battle is arriving around something that cannot simply be switched off.*

**TACTICAL:** We can close the lane before Pike reaches the approach.

**VENN:** How many craft inside?

**TACTICAL:** Thirty-one civilian hulls committed. Some are still in the slow section.

**DEKA:** Closing it strands the ones between the markers. Their reserve time isn't enough to wait through a battle.

**VENN:** Then we keep it open. Fleet's screen moves around the traffic rather than the other way round.

**TACTICAL:** That gives the attackers a cleaner approach.

**DEKA:** Yes. That's what makes it an unpleasant order.
*One civilian ship asks whether it may reverse course. The request has come in too late for the measured corridor margins; attempting the turn would put its broad cargo hull across the entry path of two others.*

**TACTICAL:** Freight vessel Six wants to withdraw. Their pilot says the children aboard are frightened.

**DEKA:** Of course they are. Tell them why the turn would be worse. Don't just send a denial.

*Venn watches the civilian feed while Deka takes the communication at her own station.*

**DEKA:** Freight Six, this is Thunderbreak. You have clearance to maintain the verified lane. We can cover the forward approach. We cannot safely turn you around where you are. I'll keep you informed if that changes.

**PILOT:** Are you telling me there's no choice?

**DEKA:** I'm telling you what choices I can protect. You can take the risk of turning, but I won't call it safe because you want it to be.

*The pilot mutters an oath and holds course. Deka leaves the channel open long enough to hear him instruct his crew calmly. She doesn't look at Venn for approval.*

*The task-group decision is Venn's; Thunderbreak's flight and defensive configuration remain Deka's. They are used to sharing authority now but no one mistakes it for comfort.*

### SCENE 2 — TWO CAPTAINS (P03–P05)

*Broken Oath enters the approach with a rebuilt broadcast angle after its Shatterforge damage. Pike orders its surviving camera drones toward the civilian lane. Ashfang holds beside the attacking formation but Billie rejects a firing solution through civilian craft.*

**PIKE:** Get the Gate and the Fleet cruiser in the same frame. People need to see us making them move.

**PILOT:** If they don't move, we'll be firing across the lane.

**PIKE:** They'll move.

*On Ashfang, Billie's tactical officer offers a target line.*

**OFFICER:** Thunderbreak is behind the freighters. We can hit their flank if we accept the civilian overlap.

**BILLIE:** We don't.

**OFFICER:** Then Pike gets the shot and we look afraid.

**BILLIE:** I'd rather look afraid than spend another month telling myself I didn't mean to hit a child.

*The officer cancels the shot. Billie remains in the fight against Fleet; refusing a civilian vector does not turn her suddenly into a Fleet ally.*

### SCENE 3 — A ROUTE, NOT A VICTORY (P06–P10)

*Auric governor hall. Naomi and Halev confront the fact that simply winning a battle around the Gate will not feed anybody if the route becomes unusable.*

**HALEV:** Every captain's report begins with how many hostile vessels they can destroy.

**NAOMI:** Ask how many freight ships can still get through after they're done.

**HALEV:** They'll tell me it's not a tactical metric.

**NAOMI:** Then tell them it's the one your voters can eat.

*Back at the Gate, Venn passes a legal shot at Billie because civilian freighters lie beyond Ashfang. Tess pilots Gravefire where lane markers have failed, using the known shape and transponder of her ship as a visual reference.*

**TESS:** Civilian Four, you've lost the outer marker. Look for Gravefire on your approach side. Yes, the ugly one. Aim to pass behind my stern.

**PILOT:** I see you.

**TESS:** Good. It only gets worse if you try to follow the prettier lights.

*Pike makes a showy attack run and nearly kills civilians. The cameras he wanted for his triumph record his reckless approach. He does not notice until after the near collision.*

**PILOT:** The civilians were in the frame.

**PIKE:** We missed them.

**PILOT:** You missed them. They didn't miss us.

### SCENE 4 — BURN IT (P11)

*SHARDFALL ARENA VAULT. The Raid Mask gives its emissary a direct order to destroy the Gate even while civilian ships remain committed within it.*

**EMISSARY:** We could wait until the civilian traffic clears.

**RAID MASK:** If we wait, the captains who defected will have proved that the corridor can work without our permission. The Gate must cease to be the argument they use against us.

**EMISSARY:** There are Shard families aboard some of those ships.

**RAID MASK:** They chose to sail.
*The emissary knows three of the names on the convoy list. He had arranged a repair allotment for one family only a week earlier. He begins the transmission, but his voice does not come out quite level.*

**EMISSARY:** The order will be recorded as destruction of a certified route with civilian traffic still inside.

**RAID MASK:** Record it accurately.

**EMISSARY:** The crews will ask whether we knew those ships were there.

**RAID MASK:** You may tell them we did.

*That is the last reassurance the emissary is offered. He sends the command as written, then copies the civilian manifest separately. He has no authority to cancel the attack, and doesn't suddenly find the courage to declare a rebellion. What he does with the copy remains his choice.*

*The emissary looks at the departure list and realizes it contains names he recognizes. He repeats the order to the fleet without adding the names.*

### SCENE 5 — TWO KINDS OF FIRE (P12–P13)

*At Auric Gate, synchronized weapons volleys and stabilization pulses disturb the reciprocal-field geometry. It resembles the measurable phase echo first studied in the Calm Corridor. Jia sees the relationship before anyone can explain it.*

**JIA:** Stop the synchronized volleys.

**TACTICAL:** The target solution is valid.

**JIA:** The echo is growing at the firing interval. I don't know whether the weapons or the synchronized timing is driving it. We can falsify the timing hypothesis by changing what we're doing.

**VENN:** How much time?

**JIA:** Less than I'd like. The pattern is beginning to hold between pulses.

**VENN:** Fleet task group: discontinue synchronized salvos. Independent point defense remains authorized against immediate threats. No one fires in answer to a neighbor's battery merely to preserve the volley.

**DEKA:** Thunderbreak acknowledges. Tactical, break the firing clock. Keep our defensive weapons available.

*The echoes begin to change. Jia is not ready to call the problem solved.*

**JIA:** That's a response. It doesn't tell us what made the corridor susceptible.

**DEKA:** After the battle you can be disappointed scientifically. Right now I'm delighted operationally.

### SCENE 6 — SCYTHE COMES BACK (P14–P16)

*The scarred Scythe of Saville emerges from the outer approach with surviving crew and independent captains who chose to join Abby. Its hull damage from Shatterforge remains visible. A voice reaches Gravefire.*

**ABBY:** Gravefire, tell Tess I owe her a new radiator.

*Tess grips the control rail. Her helm officer looks at her and then decides to study the instruments instead.*

**TESS:** Abby?

**ABBY:** I've been trying to reach you. The relay died. We had to take the long outer route.

**TESS:** You're going to have to explain every minute of it.

**ABBY:** I intend to.

**TESS:** Later. There's a Gate full of civilians and somebody's still trying to shoot it.
*There is a sound like a breath being taken all along Gravefire's open bridge channel. The helm officer had watched Tess work through the past days without once asking whether Abby was alive where the rest of the crew could hear it.*

**HELM:** Do you want me to tell the crew?

**TESS:** They can hear her. Tell them what they need to do now. She's arrived with people who'll need a clear lane and a repair berth. I want that ready before the first one calls us.

**ABBY:** Tess, I can still hear you.

**TESS:** Good. You owe me a radiator, an explanation and a great many very unpleasant hours wondering whether I would ever get to say that to your face.

*Abby takes a moment. Her voice is unexpectedly small.*

**ABBY:** I know.

**TESS:** I don't think you do. But we can discover it later. Keep your damaged flank away from Pike's firing solution.

*Abby's bridge crew watches her expression change from relief at hearing her sister to the command decision in front of her. She does not wipe away the moment; she carries it into the work.*

*Abby turns her scarred surviving bloc into the civilian shield. Billie hears Tess's relief over the open channel but gets no time to dwell on it. Tess confronts Billie with Pike's firing angle.*

**TESS:** You've been telling me there's a line you won't cross. Pike's about to cross it. You can keep saying you're different, or you can do something different.

**BILLIE:** You think I don't see him?

**TESS:** I think you've spent a long time hoping somebody else would stop him.
*Billie hears the accusation in the same voice that once talked her through a docking failure at Black Drift. She is angry, but Tess doesn't give her time to turn the anger into a performance.*

**BILLIE:** You've decided what I am already. You and Abby. You get to be the captains who saved the people while I get to stand here as the proof you were right.

**TESS:** I haven't decided anything that matters more than where Pike's guns are pointing.

**BILLIE:** I didn't order him to fire.

**TESS:** No. You just brought Ashfang with him and made the line stronger. You've been very careful to know which wrong thing you personally didn't do.

*Billie looks at the tactical plot. Pike's line passes just beyond Ashfang. She had kept her own battery angles clear and been satisfied with that much.*

**BILLIE:** If I shoot him, his people will call me a traitor. They'll say I turned on my own captains for Fleet.

**TESS:** Maybe they will. I can't protect you from what people say. I can tell you there's still time to stop him firing through those freighters.

**BILLIE:** You want me to do it because you're asking?

**TESS:** No. I want you to look at the lane and decide what you can live with afterward.

*The channel closes. Billie stands very still while the officer beside her waits for an order. She has spent years demanding to be seen as independent of the Triarch. For once she cannot claim the decision belongs to anyone else.*

*The channel goes quiet. Billie changes her vector.*

### SCENE 7 — BATTERIES COLD (P17)

*With the Verge escorts relinquishing offensive locks and the altered field behavior still unresolved, Venn chooses to turn Thunderbreak into a civilian shield rather than a weapon platform. She cannot order every independent ship in the corridor to disarm.*

**VENN:** Thunderbreak only: offensive batteries cold. Put available power into civilian cover.

**TACTICAL:** Commodore, that surrenders our best shot at Pike.

**VENN:** It surrenders this shot. We still have our responsibilities to the ships behind him.

**DEKA:** Full offensive stand-down will leave the port flank exposed through the turn. If he fires during that window, my crew takes the hit.

**VENN:** I understand.

**DEKA:** I need to hear that you understand it as a physical limitation, not something we'll repair afterward with a commendation.
*For a moment the bridge is quiet except for the mechanical sounds of the older cruiser under strain. The duty engineer is on the circuit, listening. Deka's question is also for the crew who will have to perform the maneuver.*

**ENGINEER:** If we cancel offensive batteries, I can bring the civilian shield up faster. But we won't have the weapons online again before Pike's next firing interval. And the port emitter already needs service from the Shatterforge run.

**DEKA:** Thank you. That's what I needed. Commodore, this is not a theoretical sacrifice of firepower. My crew will be without their best defense while the ship is physically exposed.

**VENN:** I understand. I am asking you to hold the shield anyway. If you judge the maneuver unsafe for Thunderbreak, I won't countermand your ship handling from this station.

*Deka studies the crew. One of the younger officers is pale, doing the calculations twice. He knows she can see him.*

**DEKA:** Did you find an error?

**YOUNG OFFICER:** No, Captain. I was hoping to.

**DEKA:** So was I. Keep the civilian range on your screen. If it changes, I need to know before anyone else.

*She turns the ship's offensive batteries cold. The choice belongs to the captain responsible for the hull and everyone aboard it; she isn't a mouthpiece for Venn's command.*

**VENN:** I understand that some of your crew may die holding that shield. I am asking you to make it possible anyway.

*Deka looks at her exhausted bridge crew.*

**DEKA:** Helm, on my mark. Batteries cold, route all available power to the civilian cover. Keep every civilian hull on the main display. I want to know who we're turning for.

*The old cruiser moves into the gap. Its exposed port side absorbs the strain. No invented invulnerability, no blanket ceasefire imposed on other factions.*

### SCENE 8 — BILLIE'S CHOICE (P18–P19)

*Pike refuses the ceasefire. His remaining guns continue to align through the civilian volume. Billie has a clear opportunity to disable the attack without firing through the freighters.*

**PIKE:** If I break off now, Fleet will call this a surrender.

**BILLIE:** You don't get to make the civilians your witnesses again.

**PIKE:** You were with us when we came here.

**BILLIE:** Yes. That's why I'm close enough to stop you.

*Ashfang's shot disables Pike's ability to fire into the remaining lane. It is a deliberate act against a fellow Shard captain, not a conversion to Fleet's cause. In the Relic Court, Dominion watches the various faction records arrive.*

**DOMINION:** The immediate threat is over.

**SELENE:** Because Fleet stopped its batteries, the Verge withdrew offensive locks, Abby's captains protected the lane, and Billie disabled Pike's attack.

**DOMINION:** You appear very determined to give everyone a share of the credit.

**SELENE:** I want the record to survive anyone who tries to take credit from the others.
*Dominion turns off the public tactical replay. The first attempt at an official incident title has already arrived from Fleet communications. He deletes it from the draft without explaining why.*

**DOMINION:** The Admiralty will expect to know whether the ceasefire was achieved by an order, an agreement or a refusal. They won't be satisfied with a list of captains who happened to choose not to fire.

**SELENE:** They did more than happen to agree. Fleet stopped synchronized volleys when the measurements changed. Verge escorts withdrew their offensive locks. Deka turned Thunderbreak into a shield. Abby placed her captains where they could help. Billie stopped Pike from continuing. The order of those choices is part of the evidence.

**DOMINION:** And if one of them had refused?

**SELENE:** Then the Gate might have failed. That is part of the evidence too.

*Dominion looks toward the aftermath screen, where crews are already directing traffic away from the wreckage.*

**DOMINION:** I spent months trying to keep Fleet officers from making independent decisions that would expose people behind them. Today several captains made independent decisions and the people survived.

**SELENE:** That doesn't make the first concern foolish.

**DOMINION:** No. It makes the rule I wanted less sufficient than I thought.

*He leaves the full sequence attached to the record. Selene does not thank him. It is his duty, and that is reason enough.*

*Dominion thinks of the command failures he feared in Deka's hearing. This time separate captains chose compatible restraint. They might not choose it tomorrow.*

### SCENE 9 — TRAFFIC (P20–P22)

*The next dawn, ordinary traffic resumes through the damaged Auric Gate. Crews work among wreckage and new temporary markers. Tess waits until the last delayed civilian hull passes before sitting down.*

**HELM:** All clear. Last one docked.

**TESS:** Say it again.

**HELM:** All clear. Last one docked.

**TESS:** Good. Now I believe you.

*On the public feeds every faction tries to choose an image of victory. Most of the people aboard the freighters look only at the next open berth and whether a worker is waiting to help unload them. The Gate remains scarred, operational and politically unclaimed.*
