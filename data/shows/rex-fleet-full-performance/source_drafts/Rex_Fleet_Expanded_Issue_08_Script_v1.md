# REX FLEET — EXPANDED DRAMATIC SCRIPT
## Source Issue 08 — Witnesses at Shardfall

**Development draft v1. RF_I08_P01–P12. Seven atlas scenes; physical and political consequences preserved.**

### SCENE 1 — THE REPAIR BILL (P01)

*SHARDFALL CAPTAINS' CHAMBER. Oren Pike arrives with repair invoices, not an empty declaration of honor. Tess Banks has been summoned. Abby attends but does not command either captain.*

**PIKE:** Two pressure seams on my berth failed. We gave up plates to Auric when you argued their shelter needed them more. My crew slept in suits for two nights.

**TESS:** I know.

**PIKE:** I didn't come all the way here to hear you say you know.

**TESS:** Then you've traveled a long way for an argument you can conduct perfectly well at your own dock.

**PIKE:** I am challenging your standing before witnesses.

**ABBY:** You can argue about the plates without a duel.

**PIKE:** She made an emergency decision for crews she didn't command. Everyone seems happy with that now because the Auric roof stayed up.

**TESS:** Not everyone. You're here.

*Pike invokes the formal challenge. The witnesses accept it. His grievance has substance, even if his taste for public vindication makes it worse.*

### SCENE 2 — PEOPLE STILL NEED FOOD (P02–P03)

*VEIL MARKET RATION HALL. Naomi and Jex find six diverted pallets while crowds watch news of Pike's challenge on a secondary screen.*

**JEX:** I'd love to know who thought making a spectacle of a captain's bruises would improve the ration count.

**NAOMI:** Six pallets were diverted. Who signed them out?

**JEX:** I can trace the signatures. It will take longer than explaining to this crowd why a duel isn't dinner.

*On Thunderbreak, Deka shows Venn the reports.*

**DEKA:** Some captains want Fleet to make a move while they're watching Shardfall.

**VENN:** Their duel isn't a transit closure.

**DEKA:** Good. The unarmed repair tug needs a route before the third watch. I'd rather discuss that than fencing techniques.

**VENN:** So would I. Keep the lane clear. If Pike wants Fleet involved, he can send us a request that has something to do with Fleet.

### SCENE 3 — BEFORE THE FLOOR FILLS (P04)

*The duel chamber is not yet crowded. Tess takes a small broken fitting from her pocket and sets it beside the challenge record. Pike notices.*

**PIKE:** What's that supposed to prove?

**TESS:** From Gravefire's radiator. It's why we're late for everything and why I have to be careful turning left. You told me I made other people pay for the shelter decision. I thought you might appreciate seeing what it cost my crew.

**PIKE:** Don't show me a scrap of metal and call it equality. My people slept in leaking suits.

**TESS:** And the people under that roof could breathe. Both things are true. I won't apologize for keeping the roof up. I will apologize for deciding what you could spare without asking.

**PIKE:** If I'd said no?

**TESS:** I might have taken the plates anyway. I wasn't going to let the roof collapse while we negotiated a price.

**PIKE:** Then you've learned nothing.

**TESS:** I've learned that you could be right about my decision and still be wrong to challenge me like this.

*Pike picks up his weapon. The witnesses enter. Neither wants to be the first to look away.*

### SCENE 4 — THE OTHER DOOR (P05)

*A crush forms at one Veil Market ration door. Naomi diverts half her unloading crew to open a second passage without waiting for the duel result or a political authorization.*

**WORKER:** That's your ship crew. The freighters need them at the landing.

**NAOMI:** The freighters can wait for the living to get to the food. Open the second door.

**WORKER:** The lock's jammed.

**NAOMI:** Of course it is. If one thing worked the first time we tried it, I'd begin to distrust the whole station.

*They force the passage and direct the crowd through in smaller groups. The diversion means unloading slows. Naomi accepts that cost rather than inventing more workers.*

### SCENE 5 — NO CLEAN VICTORY (P06–P08)

*SHARDFALL DUEL FLOOR. Pike moves like a veteran, not a publicity fool. Tess respects that a fraction too late; he cuts her left forearm. Blood darkens her cuff.*

**PIKE:** You should have listened when I asked you to account for the pressure plates.

**TESS:** I'm listening now. It's noisy in here.

*He presses his advantage. Tess cannot use her injured arm the way she planned. She studies his balance rather than meeting his next strike. Pike commits too far. Tess catches his wrist, forces the weapon free and pins him just long enough for the lawful yield.*

**WITNESS:** Yield, Captain Pike.

*Pike breathes hard. The room wants one of them to make a speech. Tess releases him.*

**PIKE:** You can finish it.

**TESS:** You're going to have to be considerably more annoying before I kill you.

*It is not a joke to everyone. She is shaking from blood loss.*

**PIKE:** That would take effort.

**TESS:** I know. You've already spent so much.

*The yield is witnessed. Tess wins and is injured. Pike survives with a real grievance, not an erased one.*

### SCENE 6 — THE PRICE OF REFUSAL (P09–P11)

*The hungry market does not celebrate the duel. At Shardfall, two captains reject Raid's muster after witnessing the result. Raid freezes their berths.*

**CAPTAIN:** Our crews will have nowhere to repair if you do this.

**RAID MASK:** You have chosen your association. The berth was held under Confederacy service.

**ABBY:** They'll need somewhere to dock.

**CAPTAIN:** I wasn't asking you to speak for me.

**ABBY:** I know. I was asking what you need.

*On Thunderbreak, an officer proposes bringing the two captains under Fleet control in return for protection.*

**VENN:** No. They didn't break from Raid so we could take their ships on a paperwork technicality. Offer them safe transit if they request it.

**DEKA:** It's always easier to get a captain to agree with you when you've got a dock you can withhold.

**VENN:** Then let's not become the people who discover that.

*The two captains' berth loss remains real. Abby begins finding space that neither she nor Fleet can conjure out of nothing.*

### SCENE 7 — NOT A HERO'S WELCOME (P12)

*TAL CORVUS, THE NEXT DAY. Gravefire brings Tess home with her left forearm bandaged. A child asks why a useful route arrow points to a closed doorway. Tess crouches to inspect the sign.*

**CHILD:** It was open yesterday.

**TESS:** So was my arm.

*The child looks distressed. Tess smiles faintly.*

**TESS:** I'm joking. Badly. Who told you to use this door?

**CHILD:** Everybody.

**TESS:** Then we'd better tell everybody there's a better one. Come on. You can point it out to me while I try not to bang this thing against a railing.

*They walk toward an actual open passage. Tess's victory has not made the routes safer; people still have to do the work.*

---

**Issue 08 ledger:** Pike's material loss is legitimate. Tess receives a left-forearm injury and wins without killing. The two dissenting captains lose real berths, not automatically transferred to Fleet or Abby. Naomi's ration-door diversion carries labor cost. No injured arm magically heals next issue.
