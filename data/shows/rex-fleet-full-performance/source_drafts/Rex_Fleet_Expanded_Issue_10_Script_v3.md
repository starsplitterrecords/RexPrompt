# REX FLEET — EXPANDED DRAMATIC SCRIPT
## Source Issue 10 — Shatterforge

**Development draft v1. RF_I10_P01–P30. All ten atlas situations represented. Scythe's disappearance does not establish Abby's death.**

### SCENE 1 — A WORKING PLACE (P01–P03)

*SHATTERFORGE. Beneath the industrial exhaust route, families carry laundry and small children through maintenance terraces while the great service vents cycle overhead. The alarms mean work rather than danger—until the vents fail to answer their controls.*

**WORKER:** They locked the manual bypass. We can't clear the heat if the main vent overloads.

**PARENT:** We've got people living on these terraces. How long until it gets too hot?

**WORKER:** Depends how long the main stack holds.

**PARENT:** That's a terrible answer.

**WORKER:** It's the only accurate one.
*The parent begins carrying supplies down the service stairs. A little boy keeps looking up at the great exhaust structure; he has slept beneath it his whole life and has never thought of it as a weapon.*

**BOY:** Why can't they just turn the vents back on?

**WORKER:** Somebody's locked the controls from above.

**BOY:** The people who built it?

**WORKER:** No. The people who have the key.

*Abby hears them. She asks the worker to show her the local bypass. A red manual release has been sealed under an authority cover; the plate still shows tool marks from the last maintenance inspection. She takes a photograph of the actual mechanism and its status, not a staged accusation against an unnamed faction.*

**ABBY:** When was the last successful vent cycle?

**WORKER:** Early morning. Then we lost remote release. The machine itself still answers the local checks.

**ABBY:** Keep the inspection crew here. I need the people who can tell me what the machinery actually does when we free it.

**PARENT:** Are you going to make them let us leave?

**ABBY:** I can keep a route clear for your family. I can't promise they'll stop trying to close another one. Pack what the children can carry. The rest can wait for us to come back for it.

*The parent looks toward the apartment and chooses the medicine case instead of a heavier trunk. It is not a triumphant decision.*

*Abby arrives as Fear's emissary announces that families must board Triarch-controlled transports. She watches the worker try a local release and discover the deliberate lock.*

**ABBY:** The manual vent was locked from outside the service system. Who ordered it?

**EMISSARY:** The evacuation plan requires a single controlled departure.

**ABBY:** It requires you to make people leave in the ships you've chosen. You've disabled the way they could stay while we move them safely.

**EMISSARY:** Those are the available transports.

*Abby looks beyond him to the families who were using the terraces as homes, not as bargaining counters.*

**ABBY:** Not anymore. We have other ships. And you're going to find out how many.

### SCENE 2 — NO SPACE READY (P04–P06)

*THUNDERBREAK keeps the civilian corridor open. At Auric, Halev is still repairing the market fire when Naomi brings warning of another evacuation. Jex, recovering from his wound, has followed the assassin's payment trail toward Shatterforge vent relays.*

**JEX:** Someone who paid to turn our market into a funeral pyre bought control relays for those vents. Same route through the accounts. Not the same person on the receipt.

**HALEV:** That's not enough to accuse the Triarch in public.

**JEX:** No. It's enough to tell people not to trust the vents to behave innocently.

**NAOMI:** We have to accept the ships before the berths are ready. If we tell them to wait, they'll wait under a furnace.

**HALEV:** We've still got families in the freight hall from Tal Corvus.

**NAOMI:** Then ask which ones can help us make room. Don't turn their survival last month into a reason to refuse someone else today.

*Halev looks exhausted by the truth of it.*

**HALEV:** You always know how to make a simple refusal sound impossible.

**NAOMI:** The simple ones are usually wrong.

*They begin clearing arrivals space with no promise that the shelter problem will disappear.*

### SCENE 3 — PIKE'S ANGLE (P07–P08)

*Broken Oath holds an approach position where its broadcast cameras can see the evacuees. The same hull blocks two loaded civilian skiffs.*

**PILOT:** Captain, we're across their departure path. The service plume's shifted again.

**PIKE:** Move the forward camera. I want the whole civilian line behind us.

**PILOT:** That's not a course correction.

**PIKE:** I know what a course correction is.

**PILOT:** Then I'm asking you for one.

*Pike looks at the famous image he has arranged and the ugly traffic geometry behind it. He delays moving. His choice will matter physically later.*

### SCENE 4 — BILLIE'S FIRST RETREAT (P09–P10)

*Ashfang uses engine wash to threaten the evacuation approach. A loaded skiff is driven dangerously toward a support frame. Billie sees the family shapes behind its windows and orders the engine vector cut immediately.*

**OFFICER:** We could force them into the Triarch docking line.

**BILLIE:** Back off.

**OFFICER:** We have room for another pass.

**BILLIE:** They don't. Back off now.

*The engines fall away. The skiff clears by a narrow margin. Billie watches its pilot regain a stable course. She remembers the medical freighter and refuses to make herself the center of the comparison.*

### SCENE 5 — THE HUSBAND IN THE VENTS (P11)

*Shatterforge lower runs. Tess has been directing evacuation skiffs when a resident reports that her husband Arun went to check a manual heat route and has not come back. Venn is connected by tactical comms.*

**RESIDENT:** He said he'd be five minutes. I know everyone says that. But he meant it.

**TESS:** People who go into maintenance runs always mean it. They just forget time is allowed to pass while they're looking at a problem.

**RESIDENT:** Can you get someone in after him?

**TESS:** I've got a search crew at the lower hatch. They won't leave until I tell them it's physically impossible to go farther. I won't say that while the route's still open.

**VENN:** Your evacuation margin is narrowing, Tess.

**TESS:** I know. I'm getting the skiffs out while the crew checks the route. I'll tell you when I need help.
*The resident is still staring into the lower run. Tess can see the woman's hands are badly burned from a failed attempt to open the maintenance hatch.*

**TESS:** What did Arun take with him?

**RESIDENT:** His service kit. The little diagnostic slate. He said the bypass wasn't answering and he could find out why before the families started to panic.

**TESS:** Can he work the manual release?

**RESIDENT:** He knows the vent. He's kept it alive since before we had enough people to staff the night shift. He gets angry with it. Talks to it like an old friend who's disappointed him.

**TESS:** That sounds like half my crew. All right. The search team has his route and his tools. They know where he might have stopped to investigate instead of coming back.

**RESIDENT:** You can't keep a ship waiting for my husband.

**TESS:** I'm not. I'm keeping the route open while people who know the machinery search for him. There's a difference. And you can help me by moving to the staging berth, so I don't have another person to look for.

*The woman does not move immediately. Tess waits without turning the moment into a public announcement. Finally she takes the woman's uninjured hand and helps her toward the departure line.*

*The resident watches Tess send one more family to the waiting craft before turning back to the missing man's location. The rescue and search are simultaneous burdens, not rival slogans.*

### SCENE 6 — THE SHIELD OVER THE VENT (P12–P14)

*Scythe of Saville can shield the crown vent if Tess opens the manual bypass, redirecting hot discharge away from the inhabited terraces. The maneuver may destroy Scythe's protective field and strand its crew. Abby chooses it with open eyes.*

**ENGINEER:** We can anchor over the crown and absorb the plume, but there's no guarantee we can hold enough power after the bypass opens.

**ABBY:** How long?

**ENGINEER:** Until the shield fails. I'd rather not give you a number you'll mistake for a promise.

**ABBY:** You and I are having a very similar day.

*She calls Tess.*

**ABBY:** I've got the crown vent. Once we shield it, you can open the manual relief.

**TESS:** You can't sit there indefinitely.

**ABBY:** I don't intend to. Do your work quickly.

**TESS:** Abby—
*Tess has already looked at Scythe's shield geometry. She knows the size of the ship and the length of time a working radiator can tolerate this discharge. She also knows Abby is waiting for her to say what the family taught them to say whenever one sister took a stupid risk and the other had to live with it.*

**TESS:** I know why you're doing it. I'm not thanking you yet.

**ABBY:** That's fortunate. I'm going to be busy.

**TESS:** Your port shield's been run hard since the last crossing. Do you have the actual temperature, or is someone giving you the one that makes the decision easier?

*Abby looks to engineering and repeats the question without softening it. The engineer gives the less comforting measurement.*

**ABBY:** You heard him.

**TESS:** I did. I'm still opening the bypass. But if you get enough time to pull away, you take it. You don't keep a ship over a vent because a captain thinks she's supposed to be brave.

**ABBY:** I know what my crew can tolerate.

**TESS:** You're not the only one who knows them. I've eaten at their table. Don't make me go through every name to remind you they're people.

*Neither woman is angry. They are speaking faster because they are frightened, and because the work will not wait for them to finish.*

**ABBY:** Get the families out. Let me do my part without arguing me into a worse decision.

*Tess closes the channel with an acknowledgement, not a farewell. She goes back to the bypass controls and gives the next instruction.*

**ABBY:** I know. I want to see you after, too. You can complain to me about the repairs. In person. Now get the families off the lower terraces.

*Scythe settles into the only physically viable shield position, maintaining the hull silhouette and directional scale of a major Shard vessel.*

### SCENE 7 — WHOSE SHIP IS IN THE WAY (P15–P16)

*Pike notices Scythe's rescue in his camera feed. The rising plume will reach Broken Oath's exposed position.*

**PILOT:** We should pull back now.

**PIKE:** If we leave, the record makes her the only captain who stood between the heat and the civilians.

**PILOT:** It's a record, Captain. It won't care where our hull is after it melts.

*He delays once again. On Ashfang, Billie receives the authenticated Fear-mask vent lock order.*

**BILLIE:** They locked the release to herd people into their transports. We were told the service vents were damaged.

**OFFICER:** You still have a command channel to the Triarch.

**BILLIE:** Send them the record. Tell them I'm not going to stop the people leaving on independent skiffs.

**OFFICER:** You'll be called a traitor.

**BILLIE:** They can call me when they've finished lying about what they did.
*The tactical officer keeps a hand on the unanswered Triarch channel. He has sailed with Billie long enough to know a dramatic refusal can be followed by a very ordinary shortage.*

**OFFICER:** The tanker contracts are still theirs. If they cancel them, we'll be three watches from the nearest fuel berth that will take us.

**BILLIE:** Then start counting the watches.

**OFFICER:** We have people who will leave the ship if we can't feed them.

**BILLIE:** I know. You've been the one signing the ration orders. I don't imagine you needed me to explain that.

**OFFICER:** Then what exactly did we win by refusing?

*Billie considers the evacuation craft escaping beneath her hull. She can see the child she nearly forced into the frame; her ship's wash is still scattering loose cargo straps.*

**BILLIE:** Nothing we can eat. Nothing we can sell. We stopped making it worse for people who were already in trouble. I don't want that turned into a glorious victory, either.

**OFFICER:** That's going to be a hard speech for the crew.

**BILLIE:** Then don't make a speech. Tell them what we did, what it cost, and where the fuel's going to come from. I need to decide that last part before anyone starts congratulating me.

*He returns to work. Billie watches the opened evacuation route and begins contacting independent suppliers who may demand payment she doesn't have.*

### SCENE 8 — A MINUTE OF SHIELDS (P17–P19)

*Tess opens the manual bypass. The plume rises into Scythe's shield. A search team returns from the lower runs with Arun alive. Three skiffs remain trapped at the dock mouth.*

**RESIDENT:** That's him! That's Arun!

**TESS:** Help them aboard. Keep moving. We've got three skiffs left.

*On Scythe, power margins collapse toward the engineering limit.*

**ENGINEER:** We have about a minute at this load.

**ABBY:** Tell Tess.

**ENGINEER:** She's clearing the first two.

**ABBY:** Then hold until she's got them clear.

*Gravefire draws the first two out. The third has a damaged drive mount.*

**TESS:** The last one's stuck. I'm going back in.

**ABBY:** You're not taking Gravefire inside that plume.

**TESS:** No, I'm using the tug. We can tow them if the line holds.

**ABBY:** There isn't time to make it safe.

**TESS:** I know. I'd appreciate it if you'd spend the remaining time keeping the roof on.

*Abby almost laughs. Scythe continues shielding. One of the most important choices of the story is made without anyone becoming eloquent.*

### SCENE 9 — VENNS'S TWO SHIELDS (P20–P25)

*Thunderbreak can extend shield protection to either the last civilian skiff or Scythe's damaged hull, but not both with the available geometry and power margin. Venn is directing the task group; Deka is responsible for the cruiser.*

**TACTICAL:** The skiff's exposed. Scythe is losing her field. We can reach one with a shield turn.

**DEKA:** Commodore, those are the limits of the maneuver. I won't give you a second option by pretending this ship can do something she can't.

**VENN:** Cover the civilian skiff.

*She doesn't explain the order immediately. She knows who is aboard Scythe.*

**DEKA:** Understood. Helm, bring us around. Shields to the skiff.

*Broken Oath takes the plume it remained positioned beneath. Pike abandons his lost broadcast and withdraws the scorched ship. On Scythe, Abby hears that the last skiff is clear. She secures an authenticated record of the Triarch vent lock and sends it to Venn and Tess.*

**ABBY:** You've got the original lock record?

**VENN:** Confirmed.

**TESS:** Abby, the last skiff is clear. Now get yourself out.

**ABBY:** We're going to try.
*Inside Scythe, the engineering team has made every practical preparation for a tether release. None of the displays suggests an easy escape.*

**ENGINEER:** The outer shield emitter's losing its phase reference. If we retract now, there's a chance the tether survives.

**ABBY:** The skiff hasn't cleared. Hold.

**ENGINEER:** Captain, I need an answer before the reference goes entirely. If the field collapses while we're anchored, we may never get a second release.

*Abby looks at the tiny freighter climbing away from the dock mouth on Gravefire's tow. The numbers are just barely improving.*

**ABBY:** When Tess says clear, we release. Not when you think the skiff ought to be clear. When she says it.

*The engineer doesn't like it. He has served with Abby for years and knows exactly why she said it.*

**ENGINEER:** I have family on this ship.

**ABBY:** So do I. I'm sorry.

*She lets him return to his console rather than asking him to forgive her. Gravefire's confirmation arrives just as the emitter begins to arc. Scythe receives the release command. The industrial tether parts, the furnace bloom fills the view, and the ship disappears from the other vessels' instruments. The record of the vent lock is already transmitted. Nothing establishes whether Scythe was destroyed or escaped.*

*The industrial tether fails. Scythe vanishes behind the furnace bloom. Instruments cannot confirm a destruction or a surviving escape. Venn records the last known vector rather than a death.*

### SCENE 10 — DON'T SPEAK FOR HER (P26–P30)

*Gravefire's bridge, after the civilian survivors are safely docked. Tess has been watching the last known vector for Scythe. Venn comes aboard when the search crews have moved to the outer sector.*

**TESS:** Don't tell me you came to apologize.

**VENN:** I came to see what you have.

**TESS:** A last known vector, a broken tether, and two captains who've volunteered to look. Less than I had an hour ago.

**VENN:** I can assign another search vessel when the civilian lane clears.

**TESS:** That's generous. Will the lane ever clear?

**VENN:** I don't know.

**TESS:** You had a choice.

**VENN:** Yes.

**TESS:** People keep reminding me the skiff had families aboard. I've met them. One woman pressed her child's coat into my hands because she couldn't find a way to thank me. I helped her put it back on the child. I don't need a lesson about who was on that skiff.

**VENN:** I wasn't going to give you one.

**TESS:** Good. Because I know why you did it, and I hate you for it anyway.

*Venn doesn't move.*

**TESS:** That's what I can't stand. If you'd been stupid, if you'd mistaken the range, if somebody had lied about the shield, I could spend the next year proving it. But you saw both ships. You knew who was on them.

**VENN:** I did.

**TESS:** Abby would have made the same choice.

**VENN:** I think so.

**TESS:** Don't do that. Don't make her agree with you before we've found her.

**VENN:** You're right.

*The pause stretches. Tess looks at Scythe's last vector.*

**TESS:** When we were little, Abby used to climb the cooling frames. She'd get halfway across and freeze, too proud to ask me to come after her. I'd sit on the ground and insult her until she got angry enough to move. Worked every time.

**VENN:** What would you call her now?

**TESS:** Every name I know.

*She looks again at the vector.*

**TESS:** Keep it open. If somebody hears anything—scrap, a beacon, a burst that isn't even a signal—I want it.

**VENN:** You will have it.

**TESS:** And don't bring me a death notice because someone's decided the search is inconvenient.

**VENN:** I won't.

*Tess doesn't thank her. Venn leaves the open search sector confirmed on both ships' records. At Auric, Sera admits arriving families without sorting them by faction. Jex releases evidence tying the vent lock to payments without pretending he knows where Tali is. In the Arena Vault, Raid orders a new attack toward Auric Gate; outside, an unidentified flicker appears near the outer Rift. No one can yet say what it means.*

Venn shields the final civilian skiff over Scythe, and Tess resents a choice she understands. Broken Oath suffers plume damage caused by Pike's delay. Arun survives. Billie rejects the fabricated vent failure. Jex is still wounded. Auric remains short of space.
