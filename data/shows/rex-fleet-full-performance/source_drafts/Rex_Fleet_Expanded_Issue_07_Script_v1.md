# REX FLEET — EXPANDED DRAMATIC SCRIPT
## Source Issue 07 — The Calm Corridor

**Development draft v1. RF_I07_P01–P19 plus the limited-command reconciliation. Eight dramatic scenes, source pages preserved.**

### SCENE 1 — A PERFECTLY GOOD READING (P01–P02)

*SURVEY SHIP — THE LATTICE. On a route that has behaved predictably for months, a survey crew checks field transit timing. Every verified reference is healthy. The star behind them appears again ahead of the ship, where no star should be.*

**SURVEY CAPTAIN:** Has someone left a camera feed on repeat?

**SURVEY OFFICER:** It's optical and instrument return. Different systems. Same feature.

**CAPTAIN:** Then verify our position independently.

**OFFICER:** I already did. Three times.

**CAPTAIN:** Do it again while I find someone clever enough to tell us why three times wasn't sufficient.

*The reflected star shifts with the ship's attitude change, but not by the amount the model predicts.*

**OFFICER:** The corridor is still inside the safe envelope.

**CAPTAIN:** Safe according to the model that's just shown us two copies of the same star. Reduce speed. Keep recording everything.

*They do not call it a ghost or a portent. They call home.*

### SCENE 2 — PROFESSIONAL DISAGREEMENT (P03–P04; COMMAND RECONCILIATION)

*VEIL MARKET. Jex has already discovered that the anomaly has reduced the value of his old route windows. Naomi brings him unedited survey data.*

**JEX:** The last buyer wanted a refund because a star was in the wrong place. I explained that I have never guaranteed the position of stars.

**NAOMI:** You're guaranteeing safe windows. If the timing has changed, the guarantee is worth less than the paper.

**JEX:** The timing hasn't changed.

**NAOMI:** That's what frightens me. Every clock agrees, and something is still wrong.

*At Thunderbreak, Jia Morgan measures the deviation against Academy reciprocal-field models. Selene has brought historical survey records, but not explanations disguised as discoveries. Venn is coordinating a limited Fleet task group, and Deka retains the ship.*

**JIA:** The field isn't opening another corridor. The returns are being phase-repeated through the measured volume. That's a description, not a cause.

**SELENE:** Older logs contain accounts of apparent doubled vessels near lost anchors.

**DEKA:** And how many of those logs include a surviving sensor calibration?

**SELENE:** Very few.

**JIA:** Then they're interesting, not confirmatory.

**VENN:** You can work with that distinction?

**JIA:** I can work with uncertainty. It's commanders who insist on making it uncomfortable.

*Dominion appears long enough to confirm Venn's limited task-group appointment. The tribunal has not been overturned. Deka's command remains in force.*

**DOMINION:** Commodore, this is not reinstatement to Thunderbreak. Deka remains responsible for the vessel. You coordinate the Fleet task group for the corridor emergency.

**DEKA:** Thank you for explaining who owns the chair. We've had a surprisingly spirited argument about it.

**VENN:** We haven't had an argument.

**DEKA:** Then you've been losing one without noticing.

*Jia resumes the measurements before the officers can enjoy themselves.*

### SCENE 3 — THREE WAYS TO BE WRONG (P05–P07)

*Tal Corvus. Tess keeps civilians away from the unexplained seep and anomalous viewing area before discussing theories. On Ashfang, Billie examines the same footage.*

**BILLIE:** If something's making copies of ships, I want to know what happens when I shoot one.

**OFFICER:** We've no data that suggests it has a surface or that a projectile can hit it.

**BILLIE:** We could acquire some.

**OFFICER:** By firing through a civilian corridor?

**BILLIE:** I said I wanted to know. I didn't say I wanted to do it here.

*In the Arena Vault, Fear distributes the most disturbing images as proof the corridors have become uncontrollable.*

**EMISSARY:** Some of the footage may be an instrumentation error.

**FEAR MASK:** The people won't see the instrument. They'll see a ship where no ship should be.

**EMISSARY:** You want them to think it means the passage is closed?

**FEAR MASK:** I want them to ask our permission before they travel.

### SCENE 4 — THE OTHER GRAVEFIRE (P08–P10)

*Sunlash leads a civilian convoy toward the Calm Corridor. Gravefire holds the rear. The first reflected vessel appears beside Tess's ship, matching its damage and movement with an impossible delay.*

**SECOND:** Naomi, there's another Gravefire on our starboard feed.

**NAOMI:** Which one's answering the real transponder?

**SECOND:** One. The other is agreeing with it before the answer arrives.

**NAOMI:** That's inconvenient. Tess, what are you seeing?

**TESS:** A beautifully repaired version of my ship. I'm tempted to ask it to lend me a radiator.

**NAOMI:** Don't go toward it.

**TESS:** I'd have to leave the civilians to do that. I don't think even I like my reflection enough.

*An inexperienced pilot begins turning toward the apparent path of an escort. Tess calmly orders him back to the surveyed lane. The reflection looks real and convincing. It remains a measured anomaly rather than a supernatural manifestation.*

### SCENE 5 — THE UNTIDY ANSWER (P11–P15)

*THUNDERBREAK. Jia compares synchronized ship inputs against the growth of the echoes.*

**JIA:** Every time the formation makes the same correction, the delayed returns grow more coherent. If they are phase-locking, then varying the inputs should make the pattern collapse.

**VENN:** Should?

**JIA:** Yes. I'd prefer to use a different word, but it would be dishonest.

**DEKA:** We can test it with the escorts before we put the freighters through.

**NAOMI:** We don't have an empty corridor for an elegant experiment. I can stagger the actual formation. Different speeds, different attitudes, no one copying the same turn.

**VENN:** We stop synchronized firing as well. No ship fires merely because another has fired.

**JIA:** I don't know that the field responds to weapons specifically. It may respond to any coherent input.

**DEKA:** Then we stop making coherent ones. That's unusually easy to explain to this crew.

*The convoy breaks formation deliberately. Naomi gives one freighter a wide safe turn, another a shallow reduction. Tess keeps the trailing craft from trying to replicate Sunlash. On the outer screen, Thunderbreak cancels firing coordination rather than blasting at reflections.*

**FREIGHTER PILOT:** We're losing the escort pattern!

**NAOMI:** On purpose. You're not abandoned. You're being asked to fly your own ship for the next two minutes.

**FREIGHTER PILOT:** I thought we hired escorts to avoid that.

**NAOMI:** You hired us to bring you home. Not to make the ride pretty.

*Echoes lose their crisp synchronization. The physical solution is untidy and measurable. No one claims to know why the effect exists.*

### SCENE 6 — JEX DECLINES A CUSTOMER (P16–P17)

*VEIL MARKET. A buyer demands an exclusive warning service for the strange corridor behavior. Jex looks at the contract, then at the unverified claims already circulating.*

**BUYER:** I don't need a scientific explanation. I need the safest forecast you can sell me.

**JEX:** I can tell you where the observations were made and how uncertain they are. I cannot sell you a number that means something different every time a ship passes it.

**BUYER:** You used to be more helpful.

**JEX:** I used to be more expensive. Those aren't the same thing.

*At the Arena Vault, Fear decides that the independent witnesses are becoming more dangerous than the anomaly itself. Its emissary turns from the corridor images to the courier list.*

### SCENE 7 — COUNT THE LIGHTS (P18–P19)

*The convoy emerges in a crooked order with all the ships intact. Tess waits on Gravefire until the last civilian docking light is confirmed.*

**ENGINEER:** You can stop staring now. They're through.

**TESS:** I heard you.

**ENGINEER:** You've been standing for fourteen hours.

**TESS:** Then get me a chair, not a lecture.

*The engineer produces a chair that has been pushed into a corner for repairs.*

**ENGINEER:** It tilts.

**TESS:** So does the ship. I'll feel at home.

*She sits only after the last light steadies. From the outer viewport, the corridor looks ordinary again. Nobody knows if it will remain that way.*

---

**Issue 07 ledger:** Corridor phenomena are physical, measured, and not supernatural. Jia has a falsifiable working relation, not a proven cause. Venn retains limited task-group authority and Deka commands Thunderbreak. The solution is desynchronized transit and restrained fire, not destroying a magical portal. Fear attacks contrary information.
