# REX FLEET — EXPANDED DRAMATIC SCRIPT
## Source Issue 05 — The Public Clock

**Development draft v1. RF_I05_P01–P21. All nine atlas scenes drafted; panel allocation deferred.**

### SCENE 1 — THE SHUTTER (P01)

*VEIL MARKET — LOWER CAVERN. A ration shutter jams halfway open. The people nearest it are pushed against the counter by the crowd behind them. Jex, attempting to get from one side to the other, loses his footing.*

**JEX:** There's a second distribution point at the east passage! There is no need to turn this one into a contest!

**WORKER:** The east passage closed last night!

**JEX:** I was not told that.

**WORKER:** Nobody tells the people who charge for the directions when the door's been welded shut.

*Two workers pull Jex free. One holds him against the wall until the crowd moves back.*

**JEX:** Thank you. That was very nearly expensive.

**WORKER:** For you, maybe.

**NAOMI:** You all right?

**JEX:** I have discovered how unpopular an out-of-date schedule can make a man.

**NAOMI:** It would help if the schedules were less expensive.

*She reaches behind the shutter to help release the jammed mechanism. Jex does not try to take credit for it.*

### SCENE 2 — ONE HUNDRED (P02–P03)

*AURIC FREEPORT. Wallace is moving three columns of expected supplies around an inventory board. Halev wants to authorize distribution; Naomi has just returned from Veil Market.*

**WALLACE:** A hundred crates were promised. A hundred is what I've already committed to clinics and ration halls. I cannot distribute a hundred crates we do not have.

**HALEV:** Where's the ship?

**NAOMI:** Outside the closed window. Nobody's flying an unverified reciprocal-field opening because we're impatient.

**WALLACE:** People are getting impatient because they're hungry.

**NAOMI:** And they'll be hungrier if we put the freighter through shear and spread it across the Rift.

**HALEV:** When can we verify another opening?

**NAOMI:** Jex has the live timing changes. I have the last public bulletin. They're not the same.

**WALLACE:** Naturally.

**HALEV:** Ask him.

**NAOMI:** I already have. He asked what we intended to pay.

*Halev stops writing.*

**HALEV:** Tell him we'll pay the pilots who use the information. I'll discuss his invoice when the food is here.

### SCENE 3 — WHAT A CLOCK COSTS (P04–P05)

*JEX'S PRIVATE VAULT. Three different live opening corrections are visible on a simple instrument record. Jex has kept the verified updates to sell. Naomi recognizes one shift she has already seen in the field.*

**NAOMI:** You're not selling a route. You're selling the difference between a safe crossing and a collision with the boundary.

**JEX:** I prefer to think I'm selling the work of people who measure that difference.

**NAOMI:** And the ship whose food is sitting on the wrong side of it?

**JEX:** They could have bought the update.

**NAOMI:** Nobody told them the old one was wrong.

*He opens his mouth, then closes it. There's no defense that doesn't sound like a price.*

**JEX:** If I open the current clock, Billie can read it. Pike can read it. Every fool with a gun and a receiver can read it.

**NAOMI:** Yes.

**JEX:** You've become an unhelpful conversationalist.

**NAOMI:** You know what I think. I came to see what you think when you can't hide behind a price.

*Jex transfers the verified current updates to a public bulletin, including corrections as they arrive.*

**JEX:** Keep your pilots checking measurements. If the field changes, this paper will become a lie before I've finished publishing it.

**NAOMI:** Good. Put that on the notice too.

**JEX:** It's remarkable how much more you ask when I'm giving things away.

*The bulletin becomes available to the civilian freighter. Elsewhere, Ashfang receives the same timing.*

### SCENE 4 — ASHFANG HAS NO FUEL (P06–P10)

*ASHFANG MARAUDER. The Triarch has cut Billie's fuel deliveries because she refused to burn independent Shard fields. Her officer has been stretching the remaining stores and no longer bothers to pretend the figures are adequate.*

**OFFICER:** Enough to reach the next staging point. Not enough to fight there and come back.

**BILLIE:** Then we don't fight there.

**OFFICER:** A hundred crates on a Verge relief freighter. We've got their crossing time.

**BILLIE:** That's food for somebody.

**OFFICER:** So are the stores we don't have. You told Trade to keep their tankers.

*Billie stares at the timing bulletin Jex opened. She chooses an interdiction she knows Abby would reject.*

**BILLIE:** We take the supply rack. Quick and out. Keep the weapons off the cabins.

*In the Rift, Ashfang fires first. The aft rack tears loose. Twenty-eight of the hundred promised crates are destroyed, not captured. The freighter struggles to maintain course.*

**FREIGHTER PILOT:** We're losing the rack! Stop firing! This is food!

*Billie's officer watches a cloud of ruined cargo move away from the ship.*

**OFFICER:** We didn't get any of it.

**BILLIE:** I can see that.

**OFFICER:** The rest could still feed us if we catch the freighter.

*Gravefire enters on its already damaged port side. Tess places its heat-weakened radiator between Ashfang and the freighter.*

**TESS:** Billie, don't make me explain the people inside that hull to you.

**BILLIE:** Move. We need the remaining freight.

**TESS:** So do the people it belongs to.

**BILLIE:** You haven't missed a meal yet.

**TESS:** My crew has. I can still tell the difference between a meal and somebody else's supper.

*Gravefire's cooling warning climbs. Tess is not bluffing about what one more shot would do.*

**OFFICER:** Captain, her port section is barely holding together.

**BILLIE:** I know. She put it right where I'd have to hit it.

**OFFICER:** We can turn her.

**BILLIE:** You can stop suggesting I shoot Tess. Break off.

*Ashfang withdraws with no supplies, a damaged reputation, and less fuel than before.*

### SCENE 5 — THE OTHER SIDE OF THE CLOCK (P11–P12)

*VEIL MARKET SERVICE CAVERN. Naomi follows a worker's chalk arrow toward a ration door that is actually open, ignoring an older official marker. A loader confronts Jex while workers tally the destroyed delivery.*

**LOADER:** Twenty-eight.

**JEX:** I heard.

**LOADER:** You heard someone say a number. You didn't open the broken crates to find out what was inside.

**JEX:** Was there anything salvageable?

**LOADER:** You could make soup out of the labels.

**JEX:** I'll send a recovery crew.

**LOADER:** With what? The clock you opened told every ship where we were. Ashfang read it as well as anybody.

**JEX:** So did the freighter. So did the escorts who came to help.

**LOADER:** You always have another side to it.

**JEX:** Usually three. That's how I make a living.

**LOADER:** I had a cart, Jex. They had guns. You're welcome to find the third side.

*Jex picks up a ruined strap, then leaves it where it is.*

**LOADER:** My sister gets her allotment every fourth crossing. She asks me for an extra bag of grain. I've spent a month telling her no. Now I get to explain why we lost twenty-eight bags' worth before anyone got to eat them.

**JEX:** I didn't tell Billie to attack.

**LOADER:** I know. If I thought you had, we wouldn't be talking.

**JEX:** Then why ask me to close the clock?

**LOADER:** I didn't ask you. Don't close it. My sister's still waiting. But if you've got information about who's coming to take her grain next time, I wouldn't mind hearing it before the shooting starts.

*Jex looks at the bulletin. He continues posting corrections. Tali's private token stays out of public view.*

### SCENE 6 — WHAT THE CAPTAINS CAN WITHHOLD (P13–P14)

*Thunderbreak receives a narrow defensive order. Venn can direct support within the temporary authority but cannot turn another faction's ships into Fleet auxiliaries. On Scythe, Abby learns what Billie has done.*

**OFFICER:** One of the Shard escorts needs a shield extension. Its captain hasn't requested Fleet protection.

**VENN:** Send the offer. Don't cross the boundary until the captain accepts.

**OFFICER:** Dominion will call that excessive courtesy.

**VENN:** Then he may record that no one was shot while deciding whether to say yes.

*On Scythe, Abby removes Ashfang from the shared escort intelligence bulletin.*

**OFFICER:** Billie will say you're leaving her blind.

**ABBY:** She chose to fire at a relief shipment whose movements we all agreed to protect. I'm not giving her the next one. She can still fly wherever she likes. She just won't be doing it with our trust.

**OFFICER:** She's been your friend a long time.

**ABBY:** I know how long.

*Abby keeps staring at the confirmation. Her officer leaves her the privacy she hasn't asked for.*

### SCENE 7 — SEVENTY-TWO (P15–P17)

*Auric freight hall. The ship arrives with seventy-two crates. Sera insists the board keep the missing twenty-eight visible as a debt the station cannot fulfill.*

**SERA:** Don't write 'hundred received' because the manifest still says a hundred. We have seventy-two on the dock.

**WALLACE:** I know how to count crates.

**SERA:** I wasn't speaking to your arithmetic. I was speaking to the very persuasive way people edit their own expectations.

**HALEV:** How much do we cut each allotment?

**WALLACE:** The clinics get their original share. Everyone else gets less. The people who already had very little get less as well.

**HALEV:** Put the numbers where they can see them.

*A family at the distribution bay voluntarily breaks a small allotment to share with a resident whose household missed registration. The boy from Luminara passes a bag across the counter.*

**BOY:** We can come back tomorrow, can't we?

**RESIDENT:** They didn't say we'd have more tomorrow.

**BOY:** No. I suppose they didn't.

*Halev sees the exchange. He does not turn it into a speech; he goes to find the next freighter.*

### SCENE 8 — THE NEXT SHIP (P18–P20)

*The next relief convoy approaches. Naomi leads with Sunlash, Tess brings Gravefire despite the limitation of its damaged cooling system, and a Fleet escort holds to the agreed defensive role.*

**FREIGHTER PILOT:** Your approach looks too shallow. I'm going to overshoot the mark.

**TESS:** You're going to overshoot if you keep staring at the wrong instrument. Look at the physical reference off your port side.

**FREIGHTER PILOT:** That's not the published course.

**TESS:** It is the course that exists right now. You can argue with me after you're docked. I like a captive audience.

*The ship corrects. At Veil Market, civilians update another chalk route to a working distribution door. The official notice is already behind events. A woman checks the opening herself before telling anyone else to follow.*

**WOMAN:** This one works. Don't trust yesterday's arrow.

*No one confuses the guidance with a permanent solution. The food arrives and the people carry it home.*

### SCENE 9 — A MAP OF COURIERS (P21)

*SHARDFALL CITADEL. The Fear Mask watches the open timing bulletin copied between independent civilian hands. Its emissary expects an order to shut down the original transmitter.*

**EMISSARY:** We can seize the board.

**FEAR MASK:** And the copies?

**EMISSARY:** There are too many to trace.

**FEAR MASK:** Then stop looking for boards. Find the people who carry them. They can be frightened, bribed, or made to doubt what they've seen.

*The emissary begins collecting courier identities. Outside, another verified route correction is already passing from hand to hand.*
