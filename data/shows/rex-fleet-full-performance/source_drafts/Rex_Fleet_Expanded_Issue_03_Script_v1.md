# REX FLEET — EXPANDED DRAMATIC SCRIPT
## Source Issue 03 — The Cost of Shelter

**Development draft v1. Source RF_I03_P01–P30. Dramatic scenes precede panel allocation. New dialogue and incidental actions are proposals, not canon.**

### SCENE 1 — SOMEBODY LIVES HERE (P01–P02)

*AURIC CROWN RING — MORNING. The polished concourse has a view worth a year's rent. An elderly woman is waiting outside her own apartment, short of breath, while her daughter argues with a placement clerk. Inside are Richard Secundo, Paul, and the belongings of an exhausted refugee family.*

**RESIDENT:** I can see her table through the door. That's my mother's table. Whoever told you this apartment was vacant didn't bother opening it.

**RICHARD:** The placement office gave us a key. We arrived after midnight. We thought—

**RESIDENT:** I know exactly what you thought. Everybody's been telling me what they thought since I got here.

**PAUL:** We can move our things.

**RICHARD:** And sleep where? You almost passed out walking over.

**PAUL:** I can sleep sitting up. I've had practice.

**RESIDENT:** So has my mother. That's rather the problem.

*Halev and Sera Dain arrive with a clerk carrying two contradictory housing allocations. Halev accepts both papers; Sera takes them out of his hands before he can make promises.*

**HALEV:** Your mother keeps this apartment. The emergency placement is temporary. Nobody is being thrown into the concourse this morning.

**RESIDENT:** That is three families you've just fitted inside one sentence. How many beds do you think we have?

**SERA:** Two, by the inspection. The third is a chair that someone has called a bed because it doesn't have an armrest.

**HALEV:** I can find another room.

**SERA:** You can ask. Those are different verbs.

*The elderly woman coughs. Richard turns away from the argument and goes to her.*

**RICHARD:** May I listen to your breathing?

**RESIDENT:** She's not a prop in a housing negotiation.

**RICHARD:** Neither am I. May I?

*The daughter nods. Richard crouches beside the older woman. Inside, the heater ticks, attempts to start, and stops.*

**PAUL:** Dad. Heat's out.

**RESIDENT:** It was working when we left.

**SERA:** I'll send engineering.

**RESIDENT:** You said that about the lock yesterday.

**SERA:** Then today you'll get the same engineer twice. It's the most economical arrangement we have.

*Richard opens a precious medical kit. The daughter notices the few remaining doses.*

**RESIDENT:** Aren't those for your people?

**RICHARD:** At the moment, your mother is my people. Help me sit her up.

*Later, in the corridor, Sera has drawn a route to a warmer room; there are no spare beds there either. The daughter takes her mother's hand. Paul lifts their bag rather than ask anyone to choose who carries what. For once, no one discusses legal entitlement.*

### SCENE 2 — THE MAN LEAVING (P03–P04, P12)

*AURIC DOCK — DEPARTURE GLASS. The damaged Thunderbreak is no longer Venn's command. She watches workers struggle to move a heater cart through a too-narrow service door. Naomi Sol approaches, hair still damp from a rushed wash.*

**NAOMI:** You're going back to the people who took your ship.

**VENN:** They ordered me to appear.

**NAOMI:** Very persuasive people, the Admiralty.

**VENN:** You know I couldn't ignore the summons.

**NAOMI:** I know you can ignore an order when you're convinced it's wrong. We've established that.

*Venn finally looks at her.*

**VENN:** I gave the rescue order. I'll explain it myself. My crew shouldn't have to guess whether I intend to stand behind them.

**NAOMI:** They'll ask how many passengers you saved. You'll tell them. Someone will mention the heaters that never arrived; you'll tell them that too. Then the doors open and a transport carries you someplace warm.

**VENN:** You're angry.

**NAOMI:** That's observant.

**VENN:** I don't know what you want me to say.

**NAOMI:** I want you to look at the place you're leaving. Not at the window. At the people trying to fit a heater through a door that was built for freight.

*Venn watches the crew rotate the cart, fail, then remove its side panel and try again.*

**VENN:** I thought the rescue was the difficult decision.

**NAOMI:** It was. It wasn't the last one.

*On Dawnward Horizon, Dominion runs the hearing without relish. Admiral Keating reads the assigned convoy protection order; Kerr watches Venn's hands rather than the record.*

**DOMINION:** The supply convoy carried heat and medicine for thousands. Did you know that when you turned Thunderbreak?

**VENN:** Yes.

**KEATING:** And when you refused to separate the Shard patients, you understood that the detention order came from this command?

**VENN:** I did.

**DOMINION:** You seem determined to answer the easy questions.

**VENN:** You've asked them twice.

**DOMINION:** I'm trying to understand what happens the next time someone on a damaged civilian hull asks for help while your assigned convoy is at risk. What should a captain do?

*Venn hesitates, not for effect.*

**VENN:** I can tell you what I did. I can't tell you it would be safe to do it every time.

**KEATING:** That is not a reassuring command philosophy.

**VENN:** I wasn't trying to reassure you.

**DOMINION:** The people in Auric need a military that can keep a promise. The freighters we did not protect had people waiting for their cargo, too. I won't let those families disappear because you can see a distressed ship on a screen.

**VENN:** Neither will I.

*During recess, Kerr intercepts her with a revised defense.*

**KERR:** If you say you protected Fleet's strategic standing at Auric, this may survive. It isn't even false. Auric might have turned against us if we'd abandoned those passengers.

**VENN:** That wasn't my reason.

**KERR:** People don't ordinarily insist on damaging themselves this thoroughly.

**VENN:** It would be a rather obvious lie. I'd have to pretend I made a calculation I didn't have time to make.

**KERR:** You're allowed to be right for the wrong reason, Ella.

**VENN:** Then let the record say what the reason was. If they dismiss me for it, at least there won't be two versions of me walking around Fleet headquarters.

*Kerr studies her and removes the rewritten statement from the table. For the first time, he has nothing useful to add.*

### SCENE 3 — THE CLINIC COPIES (P05, P07)

*An improvised clinic has been installed in an Auric apartment. Richard has laid printed service orders beneath a repaired medical lamp. Paul holds up a second set with different timestamps. Tessa Banks has taken off her coat to work at a pressure regulator.*

**TESS:** These patients came from three districts. Why do they all look like they've spent the winter in the same broken room?

**RICHARD:** They were deprived of the same power system.

**PAUL:** Officially, three unrelated failures.

**TESS:** That's unfortunate. The machines must have been conspiring.

**PAUL:** You can see the command entries here. Somebody replaced the original log.

*Richard spreads out the original distribution orders. The districts lost heat and medicine after protesting Triarch policy. Another district continued receiving supply.*

**TESS:** I was on a supply run the month these dates start. They told us the shutoffs were emergency load management. We carried spare parts to the wrong side of the line and went home.

**RICHARD:** If you'd known?

**TESS:** I don't know. That's a question people ask when they're trying to make themselves feel better about what they didn't do.

*Paul opens his mouth, thinks better of it, and resumes sorting.*

**RICHARD:** The orders are signed. The medical copies were changed twice. Someone intended the patients to be invisible.

**TESS:** They failed. You've been keeping them alive long enough to annoy their government.

*They are interrupted by the returning resident and her mother, who is wheezing again. A Luminara boy follows his mother into the warming room. The boy looks at the tiny heater.*

**BOY:** The room outside's warmer.

**PAUL:** Outside has fewer people trying to breathe in it.

**MOTHER:** Here. Use our blanket.

**RESIDENT:** You don't even know us.

**MOTHER:** I know she needs it. I can be introduced afterward.

*The older woman laughs once, then coughs. Richard adjusts her position. Tess stops speaking about the records to hold the lamp steady. The evidence can wait for one human body to find its breath.*

### SCENE 4 — THE ROOM COUNT (P06, P13)

*Auric civic chamber. Halev has asked for the number of warmed rooms. Sera has brought a number he doesn't like. Wallace has brought a contract he doesn't like. Naomi has brought a route report nobody wants to read.*

**SERA:** The next section is closed. The heaters are not holding.

**HALEV:** We can't leave people in the concourse.

**WALLACE:** We can lease private apartments. The owners will insist on a security deposit, and before anyone looks at me that way, those are people who paid for rooms they haven't been able to use themselves.

**NAOMI:** You can lease a thousand beautiful cold apartments. We'll still have a thousand cold families.

**WALLACE:** Well, if you have warm apartments I've neglected to mention, Captain, this is a marvelous time to produce them.

**SERA:** Stop. You can both win later. I have twenty-three rooms we can heat safely and more than a hundred people to move.

**HALEV:** Can we divert power from freight?

**WALLACE:** For a night, yes. Then the shipments stop. The freight motors aren't decorative.

*A message from Deka arrives. The Admiralty has ordered Thunderbreak beyond Auric's defense boundary; its temporary medical-power detachment is leaving with it.*

**NAOMI:** They've called back the people who have been keeping the clinic equipment going.

**HALEV:** Can Venn stop it?

**SERA:** Venn doesn't command the ship anymore.

**WALLACE:** I don't imagine Dominion enjoys making us cold.

**NAOMI:** That'll be a comfort to the people who are cold.

*Halev sees the quarrel hardening into slogans.*

**HALEV:** Twenty-three rooms. Assign them to the people we can move safely. We tell everyone else what the count is. If a merchant offers us a room with no heat, we take the room, but we do not call it shelter.

*Wallace starts reviewing the lease terms. Naomi takes the revised route sheet to dispatch. Nobody has solved the problem, but three people have something real to do.*

### SCENE 5 — THE ROUTE THAT WASN'T ON THE BOARD (P08, P20–P21)

*Veil Market. Jex is trying to close a public approach without admitting how many people are still using it. His sister Tali stands at a private terminal connected to a family shelter that must not appear on any published chart.*

**TALI:** Three strangers asked for your Auric crossing by name. None had a family aboard.

**JEX:** It is possible I have achieved a degree of fame.

**TALI:** You've been charging strangers for secrets so long you've mistaken their interest for admiration.

**JEX:** I've been providing people a service.

**TALI:** You provided Billie with a service too.

*He looks away. Tali refuses to make the accusation twice.*

**JEX:** I'll close the public entrance once the last vessels clear.

**TALI:** They're following the people who leave it. Those people arrive at my door.

**JEX:** I'm aware of the geography.

**TALI:** Then start behaving as if you care what happens at the other end of it.

*Later, Naomi brings evidence that the public passage is under observation. An evacuation convoy cannot get through it.*

**NAOMI:** I need another approach. I can cover arrivals. I can't see where the raiders are sitting behind the departure points.

**JEX:** There's one, but I cannot hand you a map without handing somebody my sister's front door.

**TALI:** It's my front door. Ask me.

*Jex holds her gaze.*

**JEX:** If I send it, the watchers can work backward. Even with the traffic dispersed.

**TALI:** Then I warn the families, move them off the access, and tell you when. You can complain about the cost afterward.

**JEX:** You think I wouldn't pay it?

**TALI:** I think you always make sure you get an invoice. This time I'm writing it.

*She contacts the families. The next civilian vessel is already nearing the ambush.*

**TALI:** Open it now.

*Jex sends the private timing. Naomi brings the first transports through. Incoming hostile contacts converge on the exposed safehouse.*

**JEX:** Tali, leave. You've done what you can.

**TALI:** There's one family still at the last access. Give me another minute.

**JEX:** You don't have it.

**TALI:** Then stop wasting it talking to me.

*The signal disappears. Jex keeps his hand on the control. Naomi has seen too many people disappear from sensors to mistake that for a death certificate.*

**NAOMI:** We lost the connection.

**JEX:** Not the person. You haven't shown me that.

**NAOMI:** No. I haven't.

### SCENE 6 — THE OFFER (P09–P11)

*At the clinic, the Trade Mask's public message promises restored commerce and amnesty in exchange for Richard Secundo. Tess listens without speaking. Paul leans against the wall, tired enough to look older than nineteen.*

**PAUL:** If he went back, do you think they'd actually leave Auric alone?

**RICHARD:** They want the records. And me.

**PAUL:** That's not what I asked.

**RICHARD:** I don't know.

**TESS:** We know what they did with districts that took their word for it.

**PAUL:** We also know what they'll do to this station if he refuses.

*Richard does not correct him. Paul is not a child who can be comforted with a promise.*

**RICHARD:** I've been thinking about that since the message arrived.

**PAUL:** You were thinking about going?

**RICHARD:** I was thinking about every patient whose life depends on someone continuing to deliver medicine. I don't think they will honor the bargain. I wish I were sure enough to stop thinking about it.

*Outside, Abby arrives unarmed through an Auric security lock. Sera watches her hands; Naomi makes no attempt to conceal her distrust.*

**SERA:** Your ships remain beyond our defense line.

**ABBY:** They are. I left my weapons in your little locker. It took longer to persuade it to accept them than I expected.

**NAOMI:** Tell us why you're here.

**ABBY:** Billie and Pike are gathering captains to take Richard. Six might agree to protect your civilian transports. Maybe fewer when they understand where they'll have to fly.

**HALEV:** Why should we trust you?

**ABBY:** You shouldn't. Trust the arrangement you can inspect. Your traffic control. Your destinations. Every captain agreeing separately. If we depart from it, you tell us to leave.

**SERA:** And if we can't make you leave?

**ABBY:** Then you have discovered the problem we've spent generations arguing about.

*Naomi considers her, then turns the local defense chart toward her.*

**NAOMI:** Let's begin with the ships that are actually here, not the ones you're hoping will be brave.

*Later, Abby finds Tess in a clinic passage.*

**ABBY:** Come with me. I can't keep you safe if they arrive.

**TESS:** Nobody's asked you to keep me safe. I'm old enough to know where the doors are.

**ABBY:** I grew up being sent to find you whenever you vanished. Some habits stick.

**TESS:** I spent those years commanding a ship. I heard about the shutoffs. I didn't look too closely at who had to go without heat.

**ABBY:** We weren't the ones giving the orders.

**TESS:** No. That's what I've been telling myself. It's a comfort, and there's not much of it left.

**ABBY:** I don't want to lose you here.

**TESS:** Then help Naomi keep those craft clear. And stop trying to make me come home when there are patients on the floor.

*Abby leaves her a locator token. Tess pockets it without surrendering her post.*

### SCENE 7 — THE TRUTH GOES OUT (P14–P15)

*Auric archive booth. Halev has approved a public broadcast of the records. Sera checks access and evidence authentication; Richard insists that no testimony authorize Fleet custody or occupation. Paul elects to remain off camera.*

**SERA:** We can show your documents and protect your location on the feed. We can't promise the Triarch won't identify the room afterward.

**RICHARD:** They know where I am. They offered to take me back on air.

**HALEV:** I'd still like you to live long enough to see whether your testimony makes a difference.

**PAUL:** Dad, you don't have to read every name.

**RICHARD:** I think I do.

**PAUL:** I meant you don't have to do it alone.

*Richard looks at him. Paul puts the original records within reach, then steps out of frame.*

**RICHARD:** Nemi Vale. Forty-two. The breathing system in her district lost power the day after the protest. Ors Dane was denied antibiotics when the new consignment was diverted. Sergeant Pell took medicine into a closed district. They called it theft. They executed him for it.

*On other ships and in the warrens, people make copies. A listener speaks the name of a sister from Dane's district before asking for the full log.*

**RICHARD:** These aren't anonymous outages. These are orders. Here are the originals. Here is what was changed in the official copies.

*Paul watches his father struggle to say Pell's name again. He reaches from outside the camera to steady the paper. Richard does not thank him; the two have a way of recognizing help without turning it into ceremony.*

### SCENE 8 — PIKE'S PLAN (P16–P19)

*Broken Oath's war room. Pike has arranged the attack as neatly as he arranges his broadcasts. Billie listens with the expression of someone trying to convince herself that a bad idea is merely unpleasant.*

**PIKE:** Secundo's records are already copied. We can't put them back. We can make Auric decide it isn't worth keeping him.

**BILLIE:** How?

**PIKE:** Cut the luxury ring's heat exchanger. Leave a medical patch near it. They'll start accusing the people in the clinic. We collect Secundo while security is trying to keep residents from fighting one another.

**BILLIE:** You could kill someone doing that.

**PIKE:** If we move quickly, we won't have to.

**BILLIE:** That's what you say when you haven't bothered counting.

**PIKE:** You've asked me to get the doctor. I'm telling you how.

*She stares at the station's schematic.*

**BILLIE:** Away from the clinic. Secundo comes out breathing. No firing into the patients.

**PIKE:** Understood.

*He has heard the restriction. It has not changed his plan enough.*

*The exchanger is cut. A resident finds the patch and begins shouting. In the crowd, Paul tries to prevent someone from knocking down a woman with a child.*

**RESIDENT:** That thing was next to the broken heat unit. It's like the doctor's equipment.

**PAUL:** It's a patch. There are thousands of them in every supply hold.

**RESIDENT:** He came here and now our heat is gone.

**RICHARD:** No one's leaving until we get the patients clear.

*Sera arrives with Halev and Naomi. The crowd wants certainty, not maintenance instructions.*

**HALEV:** The exchanger was cut by hand. That's sabotage.

**RESIDENT:** So arrest the person who did it.

**HALEV:** I don't know who did it. Neither do you. A patch isn't a confession.

*At civic control, Halev hesitates before authorizing a warning that may produce panic.*

**NAOMI:** People already know the heat's failing. They're moving blind.

**SERA:** We tell them which rooms still have working systems, and which doors are actually open. We tell them what we cannot promise.

**HALEV:** If they rush the inner shelter—

**NAOMI:** Then give them a route instead of giving them a rumor.

*They broadcast the real instructions. At the inner shelter a guard refuses the refugees under a residency rule Halev himself signed.*

**GUARD:** Your order says registered residents first. They're not registered.

**HALEV:** Then my order is wrong.

**GUARD:** Governor, my instructions—

**HALEV:** I know. I signed them. Let them through. Write down that I corrected it, and if someone wants to remove me for it they can get in line behind the heater engineers.

*Sera offers her family's berth, a concrete loss she is not eager to romanticize. The people move again.*

### SCENE 9 — TWO DEFENSES (P22–P25)

*Attack craft dock to the service side of Auric's luxury ring. A pressure corridor rattles under impact. Billie moves toward Richard's clinic; Pike stays with his projected line of retreat.*

**BILLIE:** Secundo comes out alive. Tell the boarding crews twice, since once doesn't seem to last.

**PIKE:** We're already losing our window.

**BILLIE:** Then make it count.

*At Auric escort control, Naomi counts six independent Shard captains who have actually agreed to Abby's terms.*

**NAOMI:** You said there could be more.

**ABBY:** There could have been. Six said yes. The rest are deciding whether they want to be called traitors tomorrow.

**NAOMI:** My pilots coordinate the civilian lane. Your ships hold the outer cover. Don't chase anyone who pulls away.

**ABBY:** That's the deal I brought them.

*The six Shard craft intercept fire intended for unarmed transports. Naomi directs vessels off the exposed approach.*

**PILOT:** We can't outrun those ships.

**ABBY:** You don't have to. Keep your bow on the route. My people are coming between you and the guns.

*On Thunderbreak, acting commander Erin Deka sees the same attacks from beyond Auric's defense boundary.*

**OFFICER:** Admiralty has not released us to cross.

**DEKA:** I can read the order. Put the civilian hulls on the main display.

**OFFICER:** That won't change what it says.

**DEKA:** No. It will change what we can honestly say we saw.

*At the tribunal, Dominion offers Venn a path back to Thunderbreak in exchange for accepting controlled withdrawal and civilian witness custody.*

**DOMINION:** You can return to command if you support a managed withdrawal. Secundo comes under Fleet protection; Auric stops being the battlefield for this evidence.

**VENN:** You mean custody.

**DOMINION:** I mean a living witness. This is not a philosophical distinction to me.

**VENN:** It isn't to Secundo either. He's told us he will not go into Fleet detention. What authority does Auric have if we solve every dangerous civilian problem by taking the civilian away?

**DOMINION:** You've spent this hearing refusing to distinguish your convictions from your authority.

**VENN:** I know exactly where my authority ends. I don't command Thunderbreak now.

*Deka's communication opens. The captain hears the exchange but receives no order from Venn.*

**DEKA:** We can see transports being hit. I want you to know what we're looking at.

**VENN:** My suspension stands. I can't tell you where to take your ship.

**DEKA:** No, Commodore. You can't.

*Deka looks back at her crew.*

**DEKA:** Helm. Take us across the boundary. Bring our armored side between those ships and the clinic approach. If someone wants my explanation later, tell them I'll have a very good view.

### SCENE 10 — FAMILY AT THE CLINIC (P26–P27)

*The clinic corridor is full of patients being moved in improvised restraints. Tess stands in its narrowest point. Billie comes around the damaged bulkhead and stops when she sees her.*

**BILLIE:** You're really going to stand there.

**TESS:** I've been standing here all day. It's a dreadful position. There are better places to make an argument.

**BILLIE:** Come with me. Secundo has to answer for what he published.

**TESS:** He published the records. Taking him won't put the words back in his mouth.

**BILLIE:** Abby's made me an enemy. You didn't even tell me you'd chosen her.

**TESS:** I didn't choose her. I chose this corridor. There are people at the other end who can't walk.

**BILLIE:** You were my family before these people ever knew your name.

*Tess is silent. That one reaches her.*

**TESS:** You still are. I hate that I have to tell you to stop frightening them.

**BILLIE:** You think I enjoy this?

**TESS:** I think you've got very good at making yourself angry enough not to notice.

*The breach shakes. Billie steps toward Tess; Tess does not move. A stray impact wounds Billie during the retreat. Pike's craft depart without waiting for her. Richard reaches her first with a pressure dressing.*

**BILLIE:** Doctor, you'd be safer letting me bleed.

**RICHARD:** Probably. Paul, hold this dressing. Tessa, is that inner door secure?

**PAUL:** She's the one who came for you.

**RICHARD:** I remember. Press harder.

*Abby appears at the opposite end.*

**ABBY:** Pike's gone. You can come with me.

**BILLIE:** You think carrying me out makes this all right?

**ABBY:** No. I think you should be alive for the part where we all decide what to do with you.

*Billie refuses the hand. Richard continues working. No one calls it reconciliation.*

### SCENE 11 — THE DECISION THAT LASTS (P28–P29)

*After the attack, emergency crews seal damaged ring compartments. Patients still need beds. More Shard captains have helped defend civilian craft, and their crews are now distrusted by the people they saved.*

**SERA:** We can hold the heated inner rooms tonight. Tomorrow we lose another section unless we get the exchanger rebuilt.

**HALEV:** What if we offer residency to the families who've come here? Not a promise that we have homes ready. A right to stay while we build them.

**WALLACE:** You'll be asking people with contracts to finance an arrangement they voted against.

**HALEV:** Yes.

**WALLACE:** I thought you might at least hesitate.

**HALEV:** I have. For three days. That's been long enough to get everyone angry with me.

**NAOMI:** The defense agreement stays civilian. Shard captains can volunteer, but Auric directs traffic and rescue. No permanent Fleet base arrives inside a paragraph nobody read.

**ABBY:** My captains decide individually. Nobody's joining an army because I liked the look of this place.

**TESS:** And we still have ships to fix. It would help if we stopped shooting the docks we're trying to use.

*At Veil Market, Jex waits beside Tali's silent token. Halev calls with the offer of help.*

**HALEV:** We have contacts watching the safehouse approaches. None has seen her.

**JEX:** That's not the same as seeing she didn't get out.

**HALEV:** I know.

**JEX:** I've always been able to buy someone who knew which way to look. Now they're all asking me for information I don't have.

*He closes the call before he says something cruel to a man who has tried to help.*

### SCENE 12 — A TRAVELING ORDER (P30)

*Venn is leaving under Fleet disciplinary orders. Naomi finds her at the transit compartment. Through the port, Auric's beautiful rings are still half repaired and crowded with people.*

**NAOMI:** Auric voted for residency. It means some families will stay in freight halls a month from now, but at least nobody can send them away because their permit is late.

**VENN:** That's more than we gave them.

**NAOMI:** You sound surprised.

**VENN:** I am. I spent a lifetime believing that if Fleet could reopen the roads, the rest would follow. I never had to calculate the number of beds at the end of one.

**NAOMI:** When you left, I was angry. Then I got angrier because I wondered if you were glad to be leaving.

*Venn looks through the glass for some time.*

**VENN:** There were mornings I was grateful to have a clear order. The kind that could be completed, logged, and handed to someone else. It's an ugly thing to admit while you were still moving people out of the cold.

**NAOMI:** You could have told me.

**VENN:** I should have.

**NAOMI:** So what happens now?

**VENN:** I'm being sent away without Thunderbreak. That much is settled. Beyond that, I intend to find a way to come back useful.

**NAOMI:** Then bring something we can use. Not a speech about the importance of connection.

*Venn lets herself smile, briefly.*

**VENN:** You've heard that one?

**NAOMI:** Every Fleet officer who ever needed a docking bay has delivered it to me.

*The transit hatch closes. Neither woman has forgiven the other for what she cannot fix. Both know what the next conversation will have to concern.*

**Issue 03 continuity ledger:** Venn remains removed from Thunderbreak and cannot command Deka. Six Shard captains independently defend civilians. Richard and Paul retain original Tal Corvus evidence; Paul is nineteen. Tali's signal is lost, not proof of death. Billie survives treatment without forgiveness. The Crown Ring remains damaged, cold, and legally contested. No ship's damage is reset.
