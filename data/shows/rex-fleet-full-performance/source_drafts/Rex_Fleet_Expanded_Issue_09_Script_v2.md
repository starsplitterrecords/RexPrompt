# REX FLEET — EXPANDED DRAMATIC SCRIPT
## Source Issue 09 — The Market Fire

**Development draft v1. RF_I09_P01–P17, six atlas scenes. Tali unresolved; evidence must distinguish paid provocation from real scarcity.**

### SCENE 1 — THE PEOPLE BELOW (P01–P03)

*VEIL MARKET. Jex stands on the upper balcony of his bonded stores. Below him, a ration hall is filling faster than its shutters can be opened. Naomi has brought an injured Shard caregiver seeking food for people who cannot stand in the queue.*

**CAREGIVER:** There's grain behind that door. I'm not asking whether there's enough to feed the entire station. I need four days for my patients.

**JEX:** It belongs to people who paid me to keep it here.

**NAOMI:** The people downstairs can see it. You can't make that part of the arrangement invisible.

**JEX:** I'm aware that stored grain has become a controversial decorative feature.

**CAREGIVER:** Do you know what I did when the medicine stopped arriving? I divided each dose until it stopped being medicine. I don't want to do that to food.

*Jex glances down toward the crowd. A payment record has been left on his desk for a delivery he is about to cancel. Behind him a figure approaches, too quickly and too quietly. Naomi sees the blade before Jex does.*

**NAOMI:** Jex! Down!

*She strikes the assailant aside, but the knife catches Jex. The attack sends people below running. A flare ignites a vendor row at precisely the wrong moment. Some of the shouting is paid agitation; most of the panic needs no payment.*

**JEX:** That was spectacularly rude.

**NAOMI:** Stop trying to be funny and hold pressure on it.

**JEX:** The two ambitions are not incompatible.
*Naomi presses a folded cloth into his hand. He can't hold it steady until she pins his wrist against the balcony rail. The caregiver looks down the staircase where the sudden movement has sent people rushing toward the only open food shutter.*

**CAREGIVER:** Somebody's going to get crushed. The people at the bottom don't know about the knife.

**NAOMI:** Get to the lower hall. Tell the workers to open the side lane. Don't tell them an assassin has arrived unless you want them to turn around and run into him.

**JEX:** That side lane's been closed for three weeks.

**CAREGIVER:** I know. I helped close it. The hinges were pulling out of the wall.

**JEX:** I had a report saying the mechanism was repaired.

**CAREGIVER:** Then you've discovered what reports are good for.

*She starts toward the stairs. Naomi catches her sleeve.*

**NAOMI:** Are your patients down there?

**CAREGIVER:** Three of them. The fourth can't walk this far. I left a neighbor with him.

**NAOMI:** Go. I'll keep him alive long enough to argue about the door.

*Jex makes a weak sound that might have been a laugh. Then the first flare catches the vendor row and the balcony fills with hot, oily smoke. He stops making jokes and helps Naomi move them away from the railing.*

*He puts his hand on the wound and sees blood. It no longer seems an entertaining observation.*

### SCENE 2 — THE HELP THEY CAN ACCEPT (P04–P07)

*Sera reaches Venn on a civilian emergency frequency. The Veil Market is burning; there are burn victims. Sera requests medics but refuses armed intervention. Venn accepts the condition.*

**SERA:** We have a landing point. Medical personnel only. No Fleet patrols inside the market.

**VENN:** Understood. Our medics can come without a security detachment, but they need a clear route from the landing.

**SERA:** I'll get you one.

*She looks at the crowded feeds, realizing the access is blocked by a Triarch barrier. She does not invent a route.*

*Jex, wounded and stubborn, gives Naomi access to his bonded food reserve.*

**JEX:** Open it. No levy. Tell them the releases are mine to authorize.

**NAOMI:** The buyers will make claims against you.

**JEX:** They have my address. They've been using it enthusiastically for years.

**CAREGIVER:** We can move the grain ourselves.

**JEX:** Then do. And if somebody starts a crush, tell them they will have to answer to me. I am told I'm very impressive when bleeding.

*The Trade Mask cannot lawfully close Jex's private shutters. Its loyalists can block the streets.*

**TRADE MASK:** Let the store open. Restrict the approaches. It will teach them what an unlicensed passage is worth.

*At the Fleet medical landing, a medic faces the physical barrier.*

**MEDIC:** We have supplies for burns and smoke exposure. There's no path through.

**VENN:** Don't force it. Ask Sera for the civilian route. We go where we've been invited, or we don't go.

**MEDIC:** People are hurt in there.

**VENN:** I know. That doesn't give us permission to turn their market into a second battlefield.

### SCENE 3 — THE WEST FREIGHT DOOR (P08–P09)

*Naomi returns to Jex's office with the assassin's payment chit and legitimate cargo records. The amount matches a sequence of freight diversions, but the signatures are indirect.*

**NAOMI:** Somebody paid for the knife and for empty-looking ration doors. These transactions are connected.

**JEX:** The connection isn't the explanation. People were hungry before the payments. You can't buy that with a single envelope.

**NAOMI:** I wasn't planning to call everyone down there a hired actor.

*The fire reaches a freight door that Jex owns. Opening it will sacrifice private access and expose records he has protected for years.*

**JEX:** If I break that seal, I lose a route I've spent fifteen years building.

**NAOMI:** If you don't break it, the fire crews will have to turn back through the food queue.

*He presses the release. The door doesn't move. The caregiver brings a cutter.*

**CAREGIVER:** I'm told you're the man who can open any door.

**JEX:** I'm very much a man who can purchase the services of someone with a larger cutting tool.

**CAREGIVER:** Good. Stand back.

*The passage opens. Jex does not pretend the rescue has solved his wound, the shortages or the contractual damage.*

### SCENE 4 — CUT THROUGH THE GOODS (P10–P12)

*Auric. Halev is at the market, while Sera receives incoming burn victims. She is asked to evict people already sheltering in the receiving hall to make space.*

**STAFF:** There's nowhere else to put the wounded.

**SERA:** There are people living behind those screens. Some cannot be moved. Clear the freight aisle, bring beds in from the empty storage room, and ask for volunteers who can walk. Don't start by throwing out the people who happen to be nearest the door.

*The fire reaches Jex's bonded storeroom. The open food crates slow the rescue crews. He authorizes workers to cut through merchandise to create an aisle.*

**WORKER:** These belong to customers. We could move them piece by piece.

**JEX:** How long?

**WORKER:** Forty minutes, maybe more.

**JEX:** Then take the cutter. I'll tell the customers what happened.

**WORKER:** You'll owe them.

**JEX:** Yes. It seems an appropriate day for debts.
*The worker examines the cutter. Its battery pack is barely large enough for the first shelf of reinforced casing. Behind the merchandise, smoke has begun spilling from the ceiling joint.*

**WORKER:** We might get through one bay before the battery dies. After that we need a second cutter.

**JEX:** Where's the second cutter?

**WORKER:** In the lockup you leased to a merchant who hasn't paid us in six months.

**JEX:** That sounds like an unfortunate administrative arrangement. I'll have a conversation with myself about it later.

**WORKER:** You can't make a passage through the shelves by signing a release. Somebody has to be standing here holding the tool.

*Jex looks at his hand. The dressing is turning red again.*

**JEX:** I can't hold it. Tell me where you need people, and I can get them here.

**WORKER:** You're injured. Go sit down.

**JEX:** If I sit down, I'll hear the buyers complaining before the smoke gets to them. Neither of us deserves that.

*He uses his working hand to clear a wire junction and calls three freight handlers by name. They arrive angry at being pulled from the ration line; seeing the fire through the shelving changes their minds. The first cutter bites into the private crates. A bundle of expensive textiles falls through the opening and is trampled under a stretcher. Jex watches it happen without issuing a protest.*

*With the west route open, medics reach patients through a civilian-coordinated path, not a Fleet assault. Venn refuses an officer's proposed forced approach.*

**OFFICER:** The barrier is still there. We can remove it.

**VENN:** We have a usable route. Send the medics through it.

**OFFICER:** It will take longer.

**VENN:** Not as long as repairing the consequences of shooting our way through a market that asked for doctors.

### SCENE 5 — NAMES ON THE LEDGER (P13–P15)

*PRE-DAWN. Jex sits at a scorched counter. The wound has been dressed. Naomi and the caregiver compare three independent pieces of evidence: the assassin's payment, the flare agitation payment, and the diversion accounts. None proves the original food shortage was invented.*

**CAREGIVER:** The people who paid those men wanted the crowd angry.

**NAOMI:** They found a crowd with good reasons already.

**JEX:** That's why it worked. They didn't have to persuade anyone that food was scarce. They just had to persuade them that somebody nearby had stolen all of it.

*Jex makes the transaction ledger public while keeping private refuge routes redacted. He knows some honest customers will be financially injured by what it reveals.*

**NAOMI:** Your protected buyers will lose things they already paid for.

**JEX:** They already have. I can stop pretending otherwise.

**CAREGIVER:** Do you know whether the man who paid the assassin is the same one who ordered the fire?

**JEX:** I know they used overlapping accounts. If I say more than I can prove, somebody will use my mistake to make the entire record disappear.

*The caregiver points to a lost order.*

**CAREGIVER:** This one is mine. Medicine. Paid for before the attack.

**JEX:** I gave the access to emergency crews. Your consignment was moved.

**CAREGIVER:** I needed it.

**JEX:** I know.
*The caregiver doesn't leave. She turns the damaged payment chit toward Jex and makes him look at the shipment particulars. The medicines were purchased before any of the recent provocations. Their disappearance was a consequence of his rescue order, not of the assassin's plot.*

**CAREGIVER:** I can tell my patients the emergency crews needed the route. They know what an emergency is. But they're still going to need the drugs, and I have nothing to give them when they ask where they went.

**JEX:** I'll reimburse the purchase.

**CAREGIVER:** From whose account? The customers whose goods you cut through? The broker's reserve you just opened? You're running out of things that belong to you.

*Jex looks at the record. For the first time all night, there is something he can put right without pretending he can reverse the fire.*

**JEX:** The loss is mine to report. The reimbursement comes from my own account. We can trace each consignment affected and ask the original buyer what would actually help. I won't make you discover the damage when you arrive at the next door.

**CAREGIVER:** Don't make it another promise that needs a courier to explain why it's late.

**JEX:** No. I'll do the first one before you leave.

*Naomi starts to offer help. Jex shakes his head. This is one task he intends to do himself while the caregiver watches the entry become real.*

**CAREGIVER:** I'm glad you opened the door. I'm angry that you didn't ask me. You're going to have to learn to live with both.

*He has nothing clever to say. He gets out his own payment account and starts making the loss right where he can.*

### SCENE 6 — WHAT COPIES DO (P16–P17)

*The Fear Mask orders original authentication seals seized so the distributed copies can be challenged. The records have already moved beyond one vault.*

**EMISSARY:** We cannot recall every copy.

**FEAR MASK:** Then we dispute the signatures. People will believe anything or nothing when they no longer know which page is original.

*At dawn the caregiver leads an injured adult through Jex's sacrificed west passage to the waiting Fleet medics.*

**MEDIC:** Are you family?

**CAREGIVER:** No. I found him in the smoke. His wife is on the other side of the market. I'd rather she hear he lived before she hears anything else.

*The medic helps the man onto a stretcher. Jex watches from the scorched corridor, holding a route token that says nothing about where Tali might be.*

**Issue 09 ledger:** Assassin and agitator payments are supported by independent evidence, but actual scarcity is not denied. Jex is wounded, loses protected stores/access, and harms innocent buyers by his emergency choices. Tali's private route remains unresolved. Fleet medics enter only through civilian-authorized corridors. Fear attacks evidence authentication.
