## Series purpose

This Academy line teaches the invented science of damaged post-Shattering space through dramatic instruction rather than encyclopedia exposition.

The repeatable story engine is:

1. an instructor poses a practical problem;
2. cadets commit to an interpretation or decision;
3. a simulation, archival reconstruction, lab, or field exercise forces that interpretation against measurable reality;
4. choices have operational consequences;
5. the class returns to the original question with a more precise model;
6. unresolved science remains unresolved.

The Academy frame may vary by issue: lecture lab, simulator, engineering bay, oral examination, accident review, survey practicum, or supervised field exercise. It should never collapse into characters simply reciting lore at one another.

## Governing physics principles

These are the production rules for this series unless deliberately revised by later approved canon.

- Ordinary local physics continues to function. Thrust accelerates mass. Cables have length. Heat transfers. Light propagates. Instruments can be healthy.
- Broken space is defined operationally by damaged relationships among quantities that normally map cleanly: position, distance, duration, information, force/gradient, and matter-energy coupling.
- A disagreement between instruments is not automatically an instrument failure. Two instruments can measure different quantities correctly while the crew incorrectly assumes those quantities share one geometry.
- Reciprocal-field transit, asymptotic inversion, anchor behavior, phase, shear, timing, and gap measurements remain part of established Rex Fleet technical vocabulary.
- Anomalies are objective and measurable. Avoid magic, mystical consciousness, dream logic, or cultural belief changing physical law.
- Pilot tradition and local practice can preserve useful empirical rules that formal models have not yet sampled; this does not make physics subjective.
- Casual backward time travel is not part of the working model. Temporal anomalies may produce differential elapsed time, timing shear, delayed arrival, or frame disagreement without providing an easy mechanism to rewrite the past.
- Classification is a tool, not an explanation. Several anomalies can express more than one family at once.
- The Ebon Maw remains a deliberate boundary case where the prevailing models do not explain all observed behavior.

## Academy working anomaly families

The course uses five provisional families:

1. **Metric anomalies** — position, distance, and available path no longer map one-to-one. Mirrorfold behavior is the principal later case.
2. **Temporal anomalies** — different valid paths or frames accumulate materially different elapsed time. Lattice Drift is the principal later case.
3. **Echo anomalies** — information takes trapped, recursive, or delayed paths through damaged geometry. Memory Wells / gravitic echoes are the principal later case.
4. **Gradient anomalies** — changes in Lattice state across space produce measurable stress, force, shear, or rotational behavior. Lattice Tides, broken-geometry storms, and Void Gyres are principal later cases.
5. **Phase anomalies** — damaged geometry couples unusually to matter or energy. Fracture Blooms and Veil Auroras are principal later cases; Mirror Star observations may intersect this family.

The five-family model is an Academy working classification. Do not write it as final cosmological truth.

## Issue 1 dramatic purpose

The cadets enter a recorded boundary survey around Calm Eddy Forty-Seven expecting one bad instrument. The verified calm eddy itself remains locally coherent, but its charted boundary has drifted; just outside the safe pocket, optical range, tether length, inertial motion, and reciprocal-phase measurements remain individually valid while disagreeing with one another. A controlled run deliberately leaves the primary beacon behind, holds a locally straight vector, and then encounters that same beacon ahead without any local turn. They recover the survey recorder only after they stop navigating by apparent position and instead follow measured changes across phase and anchor relationships.

The debrief establishes the series thesis:

**Broken space is not where physics stops working. It is where familiar relationships among valid measurements stop staying simple.**

The closing beat introduces a small live phase offset in a nearby monitored training lane and tees the next lesson: **Distance is not position.**

## Production style

- Prestige full-color portrait sequential comic pages.
- Mature Rex Fleet-era Academy, not the pre-Shattering visual world of *Shattering of the Corridors*.
- Fleet design language remains blue / gold / ion white; Lattice phenomena can introduce restrained violet, cyan, green beacon light, and physically motivated anomaly effects.
- Young adults should behave like serious trainees and coworkers, not school-comedy caricatures.
- Scientific visuals must remain readable as story action: a tether spooling too far, a beacon appearing on the wrong side, an unchanged coordinate producing a different route, a local straight vector closing into a global loop.
- Avoid textbook infographic pages unless explicitly scripted as something physically present in the classroom.
- Avoid generic psychedelic space, fantasy portals, runes, magical particles, or consciousness-driven effects.
- Integrated lettering should contain only scripted dialogue, captions, or explicitly scripted system text.
