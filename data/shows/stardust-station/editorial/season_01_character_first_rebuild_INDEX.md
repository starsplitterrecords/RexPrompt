## Protection against a false production handoff
- **Issue 01 remains released canon.** No retcon.
- **The prior Issue 02 production dialogue and "character-first" page recipe draft are superseded as a dialogue *direction* by this packet**; they have *not* been overwritten and are still present in RexPrompt. They should not be treated as polished final comic lettering.
- **Issues 03–10 existing production scripts and dialogue overlays are still older working versions.** This packet is a series-wide structural/voice redevelopment and sample script package, not a line-by-line rewrite of their every panel.
- No finished images or canonical references were edited.
- Adapt written exploratory scenes to already-approved panel actions and available balloon space before promoting as production text. New backstory should be established onscreen through characters' words and visible reactions.
- No narrator or institutionally tidy morale. The season is a comedy about people who know and sometimes dislike one another, told against believable work and grounded science fiction.

## Editorial acceptance test
Each finished page must stand on what a first-time reader sees and hears; each scene needs a reason to exist beyond the series arc; jokes must arise from actual character knowledge or choices; speech must not be a volley of objections; characters must have personal life beyond the episode problem; material is physical and nonsemantic; and the season ends with a recognizably funny human-scale scene rather than a corporate lesson.
