# Editorial readout — narrative-visual recipe survey

**November 7, 2026.** This is a qualitative interpretation of the full-recipe structural screening, cross-checked against a small set of actual page scripts (including Shattering I01 P02–P03/P18, Academy Anomalies I01 P01/P09, Division Threshold E01 P01–P02, and Full Performance I01 P001–P002). These cross-checks are *not* a manual read of all 2,971 units. The detailed unit-by-unit findings are in [all-pages.csv](all-pages.csv) and the [25 series reports](index.md).

**Primary answer:** the world/location model is the strongest and most consistent aspect of RexPrompt. The most common unfinished ingredients are (1) the exact picture hierarchy for a page, (2) where bodies, props, and sight lines change across panels, and (3) visible behavioral evidence of a character's emotion. More words do not necessarily mean these ingredients are present. A restrained scene can be more effective with fewer instructions.

## Calibrated page examples

- **SHAT_I01_P02** has a strong three-step composition: ceremonial confidence → Liora comparing timing sources → the private discovery. The scene and setting are clear. What is not specified is Liora's visible emotional change as she recognizes the recurrence; her determination is in the story beat, but the actor's expression, tension and decision remain open to invention.
- **SHAT_I01_P03** does better at protecting interpretation: Lochran studies the evidence long enough to understand it before choosing institutional calm, and the direction explicitly prevents him looking foolish. That is usable behavioral intent even though not every reaction is micro-staged.
- **RFAA_ABS_I01_P01** is a persuasive four-beat opening: a real lab; Jia clears the slates; four differentiated cadet responses; final question. It deserves a more explicit validated page composition, but the page's physical staging and acting are substantially better than a missing-layout flag alone suggests.
- **DT_E001_P01** has tactile, concrete design material and a clear cause-and-effect sequence (scan → diversion → vibration → Ostra's response). However, its final panel-plan entry is an essay about six protagonists' psychology and bodily materials, including characters not on this page. Repeating global series direction inside page panels risks stealing image-model attention from the actual checkpoint scene. The main need is to separate persistent character/world reference from one-time page blocking, and to author a page-specific composition.
- **RFFP_I01_P001** establishes dock architecture, moving freight, mother and boy, a physical scale incident, and ten exact dialogue lines. It currently uses the unsupported composition token "four-panel". This is a genuine assembler blocker, not an artistic preference.
- **RFFP_I01_P002** contains distinctive microacting: the mother replies too quickly and the boy detects her controlled concern; ticket, baggage and scale continuity are also identified. It still uses an unsupported "three-panel" composition. The drama is stronger than the schema validity.
- **SHAT_I01_P18** has a readable institutional event (briefing edits before the Council), but the recipe primarily says Lochran directs and Damian edits. It does not fully stage who notices which omission, whether a pause changes the power dynamic, or what Damian visibly does with the decision. These may be intentionally understated; editorial review should test the reader experience.

## Recommended editorial order

1. **Fix actual assembler-invalid pages first**, especially Full Performance Issue 1, without rewriting its dialogue.
2. **Add missing page composition where genuinely absent**, prioritizing Division Threshold, Stardust Episodes 3–10, Academy/side-series and Full Performance. Do not mechanically paste one layout across a whole series.
3. **Audit actor choreography and eye lines at scene boundaries.** Keep setting inherited when continuous; re-establish it only on actual cuts.
4. **Strengthen emotions as visible behavior rather than exposition.** Give a reaction a cause and a target, preserve understatement when it serves the story, and distinguish four characters' different responses instead of using a generic reaction strip.
5. **Remove repeated cross-cast instruction from single-page panels** when reusable production references already deliver it. Do not delete unique art directions, approved beats, or intentional dialogue.

**Do not promote these screening ratings to approvals or change any released or approved artwork on the basis of automated flags.** An editorial reader should use each issue in sequence, confirm its first causal breakdown, and repair only that scoped piece.
